Tor instantly notices it because, while not being magical at all, he can tell that this is very much not normal, and has a very strange feeling.  He is instantly very curious and asks her what it is. Ursula tries to pass it off as just jewelry, but Tor insists that it is so much more than that.  Ursula breaks down and admits that it was a present from Count Ward, but that she honestly hasn't broken any terms of her engagement with Rolph.  Note: Rolph mentions that this was pre-arranged between their parents back when he was 7 and she was 4 (maybe sometime early in the introduction before Tor gets too insistent about the jewelry.

He has heard enough from outside to understand that something is wrong with the jewelry, but his entrance disrupts the flow.  Rolph, of course, greets him: "Father!" and Ursula curtsey's "Your majesty", and Tor comes to the sudden realization that Rolph is Prince Alphonso and his mother is Queen Constance.  He is sleep deprived and a bit hopped up on seeing a new device that intrigues him, and so blurts out that if Rolph's mom is Connie than his Da must be... Rick.  The king winces and claims, no, not Rick, he was teased by his younger brother when they were kids as icky Ricky, so please call him Rich instead.
- Tor's adrenaline spikes because he realizes he is talking to the king like a real human and that is not normal.  He has to sit down.
- But Richard picks up the thread on the jewelry and asks exactly when Count Ward gave it to her, how much she has been wearing it, and what kind of relationship she has with him.
- Suddenly both Rolph and Tor see exactly what the questions are leading to, as does Ursula who bursts out crying.  Tor believes he just fingered her as a spy, but can feel from her field that she is not at all a woman who has been caught spying but someone who realizes that she has been taken advantage of most seriously, and that she has unwittingly betrayed the trust of the king by bringing some foreign device that (as Tor insists) is not magical at all, and so must be a high tech device and so must obviously be a plant from Austra.

- Tor, of course, feels responsible and offers to marry her to ensure that she is not left destitute. Ursula appreciates the support and hugs Tor.  The king sees the nobility of the offer, but says that since Tor isn't of the peerage, his station is perhaps too far to allow a marriage.
