# Homework

- **Summary:** story points carried over from the chapter 7 beach-house plan (`outline/07-chapter-7/03-beach-house.md`, the two bullets "moved to chapter 8"), plus the next morning. Canon unless marked.
  1. **The homework.** Trice's "homework" is a spy assignment from the special school: every student there is expected to carry out one over the summer (canon). The flying rigs and the cover of a far-off holiday make this the perfect night: they can reach places in a couple of hours that nobody would expect them to be, and get away before they're noticed (canon). They don't let Tor in, because he's a builder student (canon). Rolph isn't technically at the special school but has much the same training as heir, and goes too (canon).
  2. In order to change the mood, and try to get Tor back on track, the girls decide that tonight would be a good night for homework, but the first step is to sneak out on Tor; Sara's idea, probably egged on by Rolph's interest in her, is to act in a way that gets Tor thinking about something else -- maybe being a bit cruel by using his current anti-dating feelings by insinuating that there might be dating going on.
  3. **The giggling.** Trice and Sara giggle and go off to their room (canon). Rolph sighs: it's his job to get Tor lost in a build so they can all slip out unnoticed (canon).
  4. **Tor's misreading.** Tor gets entirely the wrong idea and fears it's some depraved noble pastime (canon). Rolph assures him it isn't (canon), and keeps it light, never explaining (ours: no sexual content, per AGENTS.md §9; Tor's fear stays vague and prudish, never named).
  5. **The trance.** Rolph asks him about the off-switch flaw, the field that "only listens," until Tor is thinking about work and goes under (canon).
  6. **Sleeping the night away.** Tor works, then sleeps where he sits, on top of his bed, and never knows they were gone (user, November 2, 2026).
  7. **The morning.** Tor wakes earlier than everyone, and sees that they are all fast asleep and disheveled.  He decides to go down to the beach and work on the build.
  8. He decides that the tide keeps coming in and out, and he isn't sure exactly how far it is going to go, so an extra mile out to sea would probably be okay; to be safe, make it two miles.
  9. He checks it out by making a new flow of water, snaking out, over the beach, and back into the ocean a couple hundred yards down.  Then, he turns it off and watches it stop sending new water, but the existing water in the air keeps going until it ends up back in the sea.  Success!
  10. After a couple of hours, he comes back and makes breakfast from some corn flour in the pantry, with jam for toppings, a side of canned meat, and Walt's eggs.  Apparently Rolph drank all the milk last night and there isn't any left for breakfast.
  11. Trice comes out all groggy and unkempt, ready for breakfast. Sara apparently had time to use the bath room while Tor was cooking, and comes out looking fresh and put together.  Rolph drags himself out as well -- nervous that Tor suspects that they were being a bit disreputable; Tor can tell they are all holding some secret, but he dismisses it all as noble behavior that he just can't understand.
  12. they fly back to the capital, stopping to have lunch at an inn in a village along the way. It turns out to be much more expensive than tor would have thought -- but the woman innkeeper explains that it is because of the drought.  Everything looks green now, but in a few months there will be no food, and it will cost too much to have things shipped in from the merchant houses, so they have to collect as much money as possible now before all the food runs out.
  13. Tor is consumed with worry on the last leg back; they end up following the King's river to the capital, and as they are flying over it, Tor realizes that he doesn't have to get water to all of the different farms individually -- if he can just supply the main rivers then the standard irrigation can take care of the rest.

## Beats

1. **The giggle** (Rolph). The fire's low; Trice gets up, Sara after her, and they go off down the passage, giggling, the door shutting on a burst of it. Rolph sighs at the ceiling: his turn.
2. **"Is it a noble thing?"** Tor, very red, very polite, asks whether he ought to go to his room, or for a walk, or to Walt's. Whether it's something nobles *do*, on holidays. Rolph nearly chokes, then says no, gods, nothing like that, it's lists, it's dull; Tor visibly doesn't believe him and is too courteous to say so.
3. **The bait.** Rolph asks, idly, what a field that "only listens" would listen *to*. He knows exactly what he's doing and is a little ashamed of how well it works. Tor's eyes go past him. Copper on the kitchen table, the lamp, lips moving.
4. **Checking.** Rolph waits. Says "Tor" twice, then "the house is on fire," then puts a biscuit by his hand. Nothing. He fetches a blanket and puts it round Tor's shoulders before he goes (invented).
5. **Out by the sea side.** The three leave by the terrace and the dune path, not the gravel path past Walt's porch; Trice has the pipe's orange point in view the whole way (invented). Amulets, dark, gone. Scene cut.
6. **Dawn** (Tor, alone). He wakes with his cheek on a copper square, the lamp burnt dry, the blanket on him and no idea who put it there. Bell count lost; the light says early. One square finished, the pattern still faintly warm under his hand. The house is silent.
7. **Evidence.** By the terrace door: three pairs of boots, wet to the ankle and sanded, that weren't there last night, and a black thread caught on the latch (invented). He looks at them for a long moment, goes pink, and decides very firmly it is none of his business. "Breakfast, Tor."
8. **Breakfast.** The milk crock is empty (Rolph drank it during the story). Tor makes do: eggs, the last of the flour, water, the honey (invented). The kitchen fire from scratch, properly this time.
9. **The sleepers** (Sara). They come in late and wrecked, yawning; Rolph has sand in his ear. Tor is excessively kind and quiet to them, carries plates, doesn't ask anything, and won't meet anyone's eye. Sara works out what he thinks they were doing and has to leave the room.
10. **Walt.** Comes up with more eggs. Looks at Rolph, says "Sleep well?" in a voice with nothing in it at all, and goes (invented).
11. **The real build.** Down to the rock in the surf. Tor shows them the new square: it works, and when Sara puts her hand on it and thinks *stop*, nothing happens. Then he starts the real thing, the sea end of the Ford line, and has to be dragged off it.
12. **Back.** Rolph counts the sun and says they'll miss the luncheon; they fly back to the capital, Tor in front, lips still moving.
