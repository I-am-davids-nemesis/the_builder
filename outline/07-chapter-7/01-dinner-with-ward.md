# Dinner With Ward

- **Summary:**
- When Rolph turned to look, a nearly frightened frown suddenly crossed his face. “I didn't realize... Tor, honestly, I didn't know she’d married Ward. I wouldn’t have had you here, if...” His friend sounded slightly panicked, which got the attention of several other people in the room instantly. Without missing a single beat, the Queen stepped just past him and turned, using her own body to block Tor from sight for the moment. Holding a questioning look on her face. Tor couldn't manage to speak, a mix of anger and embarrassment hitting him too solidly for that, so he shook his head slowly, instead of using words to convey real meaning. It was stupid and not consequential at all. Just because Maria had been the one that was there at the most embarrassing moment of his life, causing it to happen with her words and screaming tantrum, that didn't mean anything in the here and now. He took a deep breath and let it out slowly. Then tried to drop into a trance. Deeply enough to block out the room. That, of course, didn’t work, in the moment. He did calm a bit. Not enough for his own comfort. “I'm fine. It's nothing important.” He knew his face had turned red with shame. Worse, he hadn't even known back then that the girl he'd asked out had noble blood at all. She’d been no taller than himself at the time. No wonder she'd reacted so forcefully to his asking her to that party. Connie looked concerned and tilted her head at Rolph, who shook his head slightly. “Um, like with Count Wylde and Doretta? Only in this case... possibly for real. And worse. Doretta in the tale was much kinder and gentler in her words, and had greater reason for them.” The Prince muttered this softly, causing a reaction from the whole group standing around him. No one spoke for a moment as the Count and his young wife approached, but the Queen turned and smiled at them, her arm going protectively around Tor's shoulders. The whole bowing process was done again, except that when all the bending over was done, Maria refused to look at Ursula at all, obviously snubbing her. That... honestly made sense to Tor, since the woman had been having relations with her husband but meant that she ended up looking at the Queen and Rolph. It wouldn't do to stare at the King after all, and that didn't leave a lot of people to look at.
- when she looks at Tor, she smiles (but a troubled feeling comes off of her). Tor thinks she doesn't recognize him.
- Note: the story of Count Wylde and Doretta is an important story in the Noram nobility mythos.  It informs a lot of how they interact with each other.  I don't recall all the details (and they shouldn't be explained to the reader here), but essentially, Doretta spurns a young Wylde before he has any status.  Afterwards, Wylde devotes himself to growing in power until he ends up a count.  Once a count, he declares war on Doretta's home county, and goes in killing everyone who protected her.
- They are finally sat to eat
- Tor is so distraught -- even as he tries to sink into a trance -- that he doesn't realize that he must wait to drink until after the king.  He takes a sip of his juice (note: Tor doesn't drink any alcohol -- he feels like it clouds the mind and prevents him from having a clear enough mind to do his building wizardry.  Everyone understands this and only serves him juice, even when everyone else is drinking wine).
- The King, of course, sees this, and stands and bows to Tor, saying "you honor us". Ursula immediately takes a large drink as well, receiving another small bow from the king. Then the king drinks, the queen, Rolph, Trice, and then (after prodding by both Trice and Ursula by kicking him less or more gently under the table, and a prod from the servant behind him) Tor, the Thorgoods, and finally Ward -- who looks at his cup for a long moment before drinking.
- Tor is still extremely out of sorts, and doesn't know how to act, partly because of the remembered depression after he had asked Maria out
- Dinner conversation proceeds -- it is a noble tradition to not talk about business during a meal. Meals are reserved for pleasant small talk.
- Countess Thorgood asks how they keep the room so cool? Maria responds that it must be one of the new cooling plates -- they have an order in at Debri house but there is a waiting line, even for counts.
- "It was a throwaway line from the way she said it, but the Queen answered her anyway, her voice soft and pleasant sounding as well. Almost like the way velvet felt against the skin. “Yes. I've heard from sources that the new shields and flying devices are being given priority, since those are needed for the common defense. Luckily for us the devices to cool this room have been loaned to us by Wizard Torrance himself. Along with virtually priceless devices to keep our servants cool when they leave to get our next course. A gesture of grand magnitude.” She turned slightly to address the man standing behind the King, about six feet back. “How are they working Quavel?”
- Quavel understands that he needs to make a show of how wonderful they are, and answers -- perfectly.  Even standing next to the ovens it keeps one cool
- Trice looks at him and mouths "I want one" -- she wasn't there at Debri house when he meant to give her one, and didn't keep the one he brought for her since Heather already had it.
- Tor noticed that his serving sizes were smaller than everyone else's. He thinks this a great courtesy since he was too stuffed at the meal yesterday with Connie, and he couldn't even finish his dessert.
- After the meal wine was served for everyone but Tor, who got water.
- Then, the argument began, rapidly devolving into a screaming match between counts Thorgood and Ward.
- Thorgood accuses Ward of suborning his daughter, and Ward counters that his daughter is obviously of low character to accept jewelry from random men, clearly just a gold digger trying to get something for nothing from him
- Trice tries to add helpful suggestions
- (note: this dinner seems a bit hard to rewrite as the issue of spying is quite a bit harder to talk out than pregnancy )
- At some point, with Ward casting aspersions on Ursula, Rolph breaks -- he stands up and goes into a combat rage: he uses his power to flip the table and starts walking towards Ward who goes into a defensive combat rage himself.  Tor realizes that this will definitely devolve into war unless he does something.  He does the only thing he can think of: he turns on his shield and steps between them.
- Ward tries to hit him out of the way, and fails. Rolph never tries to hit Tor, just step around him, but Tor keeps himself between the two much larger men.
- Everyone else scrambles back to the walls, and the royal guard comes out to protect the king.
- Tor comes to a realization: Rolph's combat rage gives him the ability to push force in a straight line, and anything in the way gets hit by that force.  Some chairs are hit and go flying; but luckily Tor's shield blocks this effect (along with the numbing mental effect that one of the two giants has going on).  he can feel that the field is basically a line of force, and any object caught in it should follow along until it hits something.  He starts thinking about the drought, and thinking that maybe he can use the same kind of field effect to move water.  Then all he would have to do is filter the water.  it starts coming together in his head while he keeps moving to stand between the two counts.  he feels perfectly safe as neither of them can get within his shield space, and Rolph isn't even trying.  Eventually Ward focuses directly on him and tries to destroy the shield with as much force and hits as he can muster.
- Tor realizes that they are not making any progress on Ursula's problem of having her betrothal broken, so he calls out to all the people standing at the edges of the room if they know any noble that Ursula could be married to.  After a while, Ursula's mother calls out in a bit of a sarcastic voice "Howard Turnbull?" This, it turns out is a bit of a joke: Howard Turnbull is the name of a missing or absent relative.
- This idea strikes home, and comes at an opportune time as both men seem to come out of their combat rage then.  Rolph looks at Tor and understands what must have happened. Ward just looks very confused; Maria has to come up to him and lead him back mummurring quietly to him. Clearly Maria is leading the count around in more than one thing.
- Tor looks down and sees the floor is full of cracks -- presumably from all of the force that was redirected by his shield into the ground.  The fractal pattern reminds him of how a giant river has to branch and branch again in order to bring water to all the different farmer fields.
- A guard comes up and tries to shake his shoulder (failing because the shield is still on),and asks if he is okay.  Tor shakes himself out of his half trance, and says, oh yes, just thinking about something.  Where were we? Howard Turnbull? Who is this (obviously Tor doesn't know the noble shorthand). is he good enough for Urusula?
- Everyone in the room felt like they were ready to flee to Tor, and most people were quite upset.
- The Countess explained that Howard Turnbull is everyones idiot or defective relative -- not a real person; she was suggesting a sham marriage.  Someone where she could step out of royal society for a couple of years, and then return after her poor husband passed away.
- (this needs a bit more impact -- in the original Tor is upset that nobody would be there to play with the child, and there would be no real father -- everyone else is grudgingly accepting this)
- Rolph is clearly suffering from a major headache (this happens post combat rage, as the rage is powered by taking advantage of the bodies internal energy; coming out of it is a combination of being dehydrated, hungry, and hungover)
- They decide that Ursula would marry Baron Hyrees since that barony was currently empty.  After a year, the 'baron' would die and Ursula could return to royal society and Rolph could potentially marry her then.
- After the Counts, wives, and Ursula leave, Trice pounces on Tor, asking if he is okay. Everyone, except the king, is very concerned.  The king recognized that the shield not only blocked out the physical force, but also the mental effects.  He asks how Tor produced such an amulet.
- Tor explains that he is afraid that the royal guard at the school, Wensa, is going to kill him -- probably not really, but he still kind of is worried about it anyway -- so he is constantly thinking about ways to improve the shield.  he admits that he still hasn't figured out poisons, so asks everyone not to tell her that.
- Trice is still worried, pointing out the cracked floor shows how much force he had to be enduring.
- Tor redirects (since he really did not have any issues), and says he was working on the drought problem. he thinks he might have a solution, and asks to meet with King Richard in a few days after he has a chance to think a bit more about the solution.
- The king agrees to meet in three days at luncheon -- he holds up two fingers (in a gesture completely foreign to Tor, but obviously meant for one of the royal guards to add it to his calendar).
- After that tor and Rolph walk back to their rooms.  Rolph is clearly upset.  he doesn't want Tor to think that he loses his temper so easily. tor consoles him that they have lived together for 2 years, and of course he knows his friend is normally very calm and collected. Rolph is a bit surprised that his father didn't declare war.  But, combat rage excuses most social errors, but it is considered shameful to let it happen.  But Ursula's honor was defended, immediately, and then what Tor did...
