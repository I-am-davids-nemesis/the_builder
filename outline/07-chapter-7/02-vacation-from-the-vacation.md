# Vacation From the Vacation

- **Summary:**
- karina knocks on Tor's door early the next morning, waking him up. he answers the door, and she ducks inside -- to Tor its like she wants to keep the cool inside. To Karina, she wants more.  She asks about getting one of the temperature equalization amulets.  She is going out for the day, and it would be so much nicer to be cool all day. Tor has sisters, so he thinks he understands her.  He yawns, but not rudely -- he's just super tired still.  he apologizes that he doesn't have any, not even his own since he gave it away the previous night.
- he suggests she help him get all the materials together and then he could make them up in a couple hours. one thing he definitely needs are chains for them, so he has something nicer than a bit of hemp string. Also, he is out of acid, if she could get him some of that, or even sand paper might work. she pouts because she wants to leave by noon. Tor thinks it reasonable (because of course other peoples desires always seem reasonable to him), and asks what time it is so he can get it ready by then. its nearly 9am. (she has a gold watch on a chain she can check).
- Tor makes 10 more copies -- even faster this time.
- he had time to brush his teeth and take a quick bath before the princess got back
- the brushes the princess brings back are made of a material that will fall apart in the acid, so tor has to reject the brushes; Karina is disappointed, but Tor says they can make do.  They go outside and put all the plates on a canvas and use the lid for one of the containers to put acid on the plates -- the resist that he put on covers the plate where the acid is not going to eat away at the copper, so they don't have to be super precise -- and it is mostly to distinguish the amulets from other amulets anyway, so it isn't a big deal.
- they test it after washing off the acid, and they both shiver in the delicious cool.
- Karina thanks Tor for the amulet, and Tor asks where the kitchen is so that he can get some breakfast.  She points it out to him, and then gives him a quick kiss on the cheek before pretending to blush and scurry away.
- Tor is shocked, and wonders what the folks back home would say to a princess kissing him; he thinks nobody would possibly believe such a thing, so laughs it off.
- Tor finally wanders over to the kitchen, afraid that they would just put him to work since he is wearing his brown school outfit.
- After waiting around at the open kitchen doorway for 5 minutes, someone finally notices him and asks what they can do for him.
- he asks for whatever food is laying around since he missed breakfast, and was worried that he would miss lunch.
- The cook apologizes for the heat and finds some bread and fruit for him, plus a stack of cheese slices. he realizes that the fruit was dried with one of his new food dryers and the texture is super interesting -- almost like it melts in his mouth, but retaining the essential flavor of the fruit, with a slight chewiness at the end.
    - The woman tries to get him out the door by waving him off, and Tor awkwardly tries to hold all the food and his cooling devices.  suddenly he realizes that the cook doesnt' have any way to keep cool in her kitchen. he gives half of the devices to the cook -- who doesn't understand at first until she realizes that they are the same as the ones the servers were wearing the night before. She tries one, and basks in the cool a second before trying to give it back; Tor insists that the crew in the kitchen use the ones he handed to her, and that if they need more to let him know.
    - before the cook can fully understand what just happens, tor thanks her again for the food and walks away.
    - he goes to sit down in the shade to eat and to think about the drought problem
    - he starts thinking about how to keep the water pumping, and realizes that he has a bathtub perfect for testing things at a small scale. he goes to his room and fills the tub halfway, and starts making a couple of simple weak field devices to pump water, and makes a credible fountain after a few failures
    - Rolph eventually finds him in his bath room, and is very happy to see his friend playing with an obvious decorative fountain plan.  He says that it would be a fantastic thing to put in the middle of the decorative pond out side -- his mother has high society ladies coming over later, and a new fountain would be a nice distraction from their usual conversations, which he has always found to be a bit catty.
    - Tor is pretty excited by the idea of making something pretty out in a comparatively giant pond, and drags his friend out.  It turns out that it isn't a single pond, but a whole decorative water area with a dozen different water areas, including a rather large pond in the center.
    - over the next four hours Tor and Rolph design a fantastical fountain with water looping and flying between the water features and the main pond. Tor decides that maybe they have enough water in the fountains when he sees that about half the water from the main pond is now transiting through the air at once -- he doesn't want to kill off the fish in the ponds after all.  He didn't spend much time on the fields so he estimates that the fountain will only last a couple of weeks.
    - He mentions that now he just needs a bunch of salt water to test this out on, and work on putting a purification field on it as well to clean out the salt. he is thinking of asking the kitchen for a pound of salt and a barrel.  Rolph blinks at him and asks him if he has ever been to the ocean.  Tor, of course, has not.  He has heard about it, but he is concerned that if he will take a full river of water from it, it will dry up rather quickly.  Rolph laughs, and says that they really need to go out to the ocean and Tor will understand that will not happen.
    - Rolph sets up a trip out to the coast for the following day, inviting Sara and Trice -- after all, they can just fly over, so its not like it takes much time, and he has a beach house he used to visit a lot as a child.
    - Tor laughs and asks if they are taking a vacation from their summer vacation
