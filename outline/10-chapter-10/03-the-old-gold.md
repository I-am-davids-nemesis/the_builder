## Beats

1. **The border** (Varley). As the dessert dishes go out, another shouting match erupts between two men seated near each other. Tor is too far away to hear exactly, but it sounds like a border dispute: where a county line is drawn, and what's on each side of it.
2. **The strangling.** Tor wants to ignore it, but then one man grabs the woman with the other man and starts strangling her. Reddish sparks are coming off him, and the air round him is hot as a forge; his grip doesn't tire, and Priscilla's natural shield only just holds. Tor is shocked nobody intervenes, but he gets the feeling at least one of them is in a combat rage, so the people backing away are likely protecting themselves from the combat aura, whatever this one is. The guards hold a ring and wait, as they always do with a rage.
3. **Him, then.** If anyone is going to save the woman, it will have to be him. He checks his shield once as he leaves his seat. He stumbles over, checking it again on the way, and the second check turns it off. She's already turning blue. He drops his walking stick, so he won't have a weapon, and hits the man in the kidney as hard as he can, which isn't hard enough for the man to notice.
4. **Again.** Tor thinks this will probably get him executed, but tries again, because he has to do something to save her.
5. **The kick.** Finally he kicks the man (awkward, with one leg in a splint). The man drops the woman, turns and tries to kill him instead. Tor has to goad him a few times to keep his attention. Pale gold sparks fly from the air at Tor's sides and back, out past where his shield would have held them; he never sees them. Near Varley, Lady Arden says under her breath that it's "the old gold," the color nobody has thrown since the first king's line ended; Varley half remembers the lesson from her tutors and doesn't finish the thought (invented; accepted by the user, November 8, 2026). Only Varley and the few round Lady Arden hear it; it travels later as gossip. He doesn't tire on the splinted leg and takes blows that should break him, and the air round him presses on people the way the duke's does. The man finally runs out of steam after about twenty minutes.
6. **Coming to.** The man seems suddenly to come to, and asks Tor if he's all right: he was discussing border issues with Baron Rochester, and the next thing he knew he was hitting Tor.

7. **The joke** (Rolph). Tor, sure he's going to be killed since everyone saw what he did, makes a joke, shaking: they had a disagreement about a lady, and he's afraid she's going home with him (the joke is on the situation, not on Priscilla; user, November 8, 2026). He smiles at the man, who looks too dazed to follow, and like a killer headache is coming on.
8. **The shield to Rolph.** Rolph has walked up beside Tor; Tor sighs and hands his shield over. After a couple of minutes of nothing happening, he realizes his punishment will likely come after the party, or at least not in front of the guests.
9. **The garden.** He limps out to a side garden; Rolph follows. Two royal guards come out too, looking a bit nervous.
10. **"Something you need?"** Rolph asks if there's something he needs, still holding Tor's shield amulet uncertainly.
11. **The guard's question.** The guard asks Tor if he intends to pursue this further. Tor doesn't understand. The guard needs to know whether he's satisfied or there will be further conflict with the duke; the king's forces will need to know how to respond.
12. **Rolph settles it.** Rolph intervenes, formally, speaking for the crown: the matter is over as far as Tor is concerned; it was never personal, and he only meant to protect Lady Priscilla when he saw her attacked. Rolph doesn't believe Duke Winchester means harm to Tor or to Priscilla; it was a disaster narrowly avoided, and these things happen. Let them know if the duke changes his mind, though Rolph wouldn't fancy the duke's chances against Tor; that's for the duke's honor to decide. But the duke is normally cool-headed and just, so they should expect it to be truly over. He hands the amulet back to Tor and motions him to put it on. (Content from the user's draft of the speech; the prose keeps its content and none of its wording, user, November 8, 2026.)
13. **Shaking.** Tor is shaking with fear.
14. **Connie.** Connie comes out after a bit and hugs Tor. Lady Priscilla asked her to thank him: luckily her natural shield came on enough to keep her alive until Tor came and distracted the duke. Connie commends his bravery. (Priscilla has gone home with Rochester.)
15. **"Why haven't I been arrested?"** Tor, still confused, asks.
16. **The crest.** Rolph, back in his own voice, asks if he's joking: he's still wearing the king's crest over his heart (on the breast of Burks's blue coat). That means Tor wasn't the one doing anything; as far as everyone is concerned, it was the king. Which explains the guard's question. A good thing Tor doesn't hold grudges.
17. **The rage they saw.** Does Tor understand? People saw him go into a battle rage himself, sparks and all, and worried he wouldn't recover at all (that can happen the first couple of times). And the duke hasn't the manpower, or the magical help Tor has or could give the king's army; even his army of 20,000 men would be no match for Tor if he were determined.
18. **The shield off.** Rolph and Connie are a bit confused about why Tor turned his shield off: the sparks flew free of it. Tor is confused again: he felt as if he had a shield on. It worked just like his normal one; he could feel the field and how the forces moved round him, draining into the ground. He says so.
19. **Back in.** Tor just wants to go back to his room, if he's still welcome in the house. The queen: of course he's welcome, but he has to go back to the party to show he's in control of himself. It will terrify those smart enough to understand what's happening. So he has to go in, smile, and pretend nothing happened.
20. **The hush.** He walks back in and the room goes quiet. At once several people converge on him: the duke he just fought; Tovey, to stand at his shoulder in case the duke's rage comes back (he knows rage from the inside, and says nothing); and another big man, even taller than the king. They're much faster than Tor, hobbling on his splinted leg.
21. **The knee.** The duke reaches Tor first and, instead of resuming the fight, goes down on one knee and asks forgiveness for the rage and for attacking him.
22. **No debts between friends.** Tor bows and tries what he did with Tovey (chapter 3): it was nothing, and should be nothing, since they could be friends, and there are no debts between friends, as long as he's asked forgiveness of the person who was actually hurt.
23. **To Priscilla.** The duke thanks him, rises, and goes to see to that directly.
24. **Count Ford.** The big man looks satisfied and holds out his hand to shake Tor's. He's Count Ford, and wants to thank Tor for all his help: the river already made, and, as he heard from the queen, Tor's plan to make up some of the lost crops by giving away more of his own work, all without asking anything of the count (chapter 9, scene 4). If there's anything he or his people can do for Tor, he's at Tor's service.
25. **The king's river.** Tor tries to turn the praise to the king; he's just happy he could play a small part.

26. **The rounds** (Trice). Connie has sent Trice to him. Tor has to stay another couple of hours making the rounds and being introduced. Luckily Trice is beside him, keeping track of everyone, since Tor is certainly not going to remember them all.
27. **Leave to go.** The queen finally indicates he may leave.
28. **His station.** Walking toward the door, Trice tells him she hasn't forgotten what they talked about earlier (scene 01): they just need to get Tor an improvement in his station.
29. **The hand.** Tor blushes, but tries to be courtly and gallant, bends over Trice's hand and kisses it. It's Trice's turn to blush, and she walks away, leaving Tor on his own.
