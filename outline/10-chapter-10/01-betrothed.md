# Betrothed

- **When:** the tenth day, the queen's dinner at the ninth bell of the night, straight on from chapter 9, scene 5 (Trice walking away from Tor).
- **Summary:** Tor catches Trice's sleeve before she reaches the terrace doors. She puts off telling him what's wrong, sees Collette pressed close and takes her for Tor's date, and brightens when Tor says at once that Collette is Tovey's. Collette spots Tor's newest shield on Trice's neck and learns he gave it to her; Trice laughs off the idea that they're courting, and Tovey says that what matters about Tor is that he came to his defense the night of the courtyard. The herald calls the Morgans forward, and the king gives them Tor's present in a flowery speech; the room wonders what they did to earn it. Trice takes that as the plan she needs: get Tor a title, convince her parents he loves her, and marry him instead of— She breaks off. Tor, who knows from the Wylde tale how long a rise like that takes, goes still and very polite. Facts from the user's notes; canon unless marked.
  1. **The sleeve.** Tor manages to grab Trice's sleeve before she gets too far away. She looks annoyed, but then finally focuses on Tor.
  2. **Put off.** Tor asks her what the matter is. Trice doesn't really want to talk about it and puts him off: later, it's a conversation not meant for here.
  3. **Collette.** Trice sees Collette pressed up against Tor to hear what they're saying, and is clearly sad about it, thinking Tor must be with another girl tonight.
  4. **"Tovey's."** Tor feels that from her field and quickly introduces Collette as Tovey's date for the evening.
  5. **The idea.** This cheers Trice up immensely, and suddenly she has an idea; Tor can feel her having it, and feels her sizing him up as a way out of her problem.
  6. **The dress and the shield.** Trice is in a dark blue dress, a few shades brighter than navy (user, November 8, 2026), wearing one of Tor's newest shields as jewelry on a fine silver chain. Tor notices how it sets off the cornflower blue of her eyes.
  7. **Collette's envy.** Collette notices where Tor is looking and gets very excited: is that one of the wizard Tor's amulets? She's very jealous; she hasn't been able to get one, which tracks, because almost no one has one.
  8. **"He gave it to me."** Trice, amused, points at Tor. He gave her one months ago, before the military got any, but this one is the newest, fourth-generation model, better than even the military's.
  9. **Not like that.** Collette is shocked and wonders whether Tor and Trice are courting. Trice laughs it off: they aren't friends like that. Tovey agrees that Tor is generous with his friends (his own shield, his flying rig), but says that isn't the most important part of Tor's character: Tor came at once to defend Tovey's honor when he needed him, and Tovey won't soon forget it. He means the courtyard night in chapter two, when his rage turned on Trice and Tor stopped him (user, November 8, 2026; `codex/characters/tovey.md`).
  10. **Trice agrees.** Tor doesn't quite get it; he doesn't realize what a big deal it is to Tovey that he lost his temper, went into a battle rage and nearly killed his cousin.
  11. **The stir.** A stirring through the crowd turns everyone toward the king's table. Tor thinks it must be some kind of mind magic, and wonders at the subtlety.
  12. **The Morgans.** The herald calls Trice's parents, Eric and Mercy, Duke and Baroness Morgan, to the front. (Mercy is a sitting baroness in her own right and a duchess by marriage, `outline/09-chapter-9/04-lunch-with-the-queen.md`; the herald gives her both: "His Grace Duke Eric Morgan, and the Sitting Baroness, Her Grace Merciful Thorgood Morgan," user, November 8, 2026.)
  13. **The present.** King Richard presents the present Tor asked him to give them, from "the Master Wizard Tor": two sets of flying gear and two full shields, with a flowery speech on how impressive and important it is.
  14. **Surprised.** Tor is a bit surprised that people are so enthusiastic about his gifts.
  15. **The room wonders.** People around them wonder what the duke and duchess could have done to earn such gifts from Master Wizard Tor, and what they could do to get something like that as well.
  16. **The plan.** Trice takes this in as part of her plan: get Tor a title, convince her parents that Tor loves her, and that they should be married instead of… (she doesn't finish).
  17. **Three steps.** Tor is stunned; he thinks his station is far too low ever to rise within three steps of Trice's (nobles marry within three steps, `codex/canon-notes.md`).
  18. **Wylde.** He has heard of warriors gaining enough fame and favor to rise through the ranks, like Count Wylde, but that took decades. He goes into a bit of a meditative state to deal with the overwhelming social pressure.
- **POV / mode:** rotating third, **Collette** throughout, continuing from chapter 9, scene 5 (user, November 8, 2026). She is pressed close enough to hear most of it, and as the daughter of a small house that learns the great ones by heart, she knows exactly what a public gift from the king means.   - **The dress** (beat 6): Collette sees where Tor's eyes go, and that he's looking at the shield, not the dress (invented). The cornflower eyes are Collette's noticing, not Tor's (invented).
  - **Mind magic** (beat 11): Tor murmurs "That's clever" and looks round for whoever cast it; Collette can see it's only the king rising, and the room doing what rooms do when a king rises (invented).
