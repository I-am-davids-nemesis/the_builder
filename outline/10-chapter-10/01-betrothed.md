# Betrothed

- **Summary:**
- tor manages to grab Trice's sleeve before she gets too far away.  She looks annoyed, but then finally focuses on Tor.
- tor asks her what the matter is -- Trice doesn't really want to talk about it, but just puts him off -- later, a conversation that is not really meant for here.
- Trice sees Collette as press up against Tor to see what they are talking about, and she is clearly sad about that, thinking Tor must be with another girl tonight.
- Tor kind of feels that from her field, and quickly introduces Collette as Tovey's date for the evening.
- This cheers Trice up immensely, and suddenly she has an idea -- Tor can feel her having the idea, and he feels her sizing him up as a way out of her problem.
- Tor notices Trice's deep blue dress, and the fact she is wearing one of his newest shields as jewelry with a fine silver chain holding it on her. He thinks how it sets off the cornflower blue of her eyes nicely.
- Collette notices where Tor is looking and gets super excited, asking if that is one of the wizard Tor's amulets -- she is very jealous -- she hasn't been able to get one yet, which tracks because almost no one has one.
- Trice is amused and points at Tor and says that he gave it to her; he actually gave her one months ago before the military got one, but this one is the newest fourth generation model, better than even the military ones.
- Collette is very shocked and is wondering if Tor and Trice are dating.  Trice laughs it off and says they are not friends like that.  Tovey interrupts and agrees that Tor is very generous like that with his friends, pointing out his own shield and flying device; but he claims that is not the most important part of Tor's character -- Tor instantly came to defend Tovey's honor when he needed him, and that is something he will not soon forget.
- Trice agrees. Tor doesn't quite get it since he didn't/doesn't realize what a big deal it is to Tovey that he lost his temper, went into a battle rage, and nearly killed his cousin.
- Just then, a stirring through the crowd brought everyone's attention to the kings table. Tor thought it must be some type of mind magic, and wondered at the subtlety.
- The herald calls out for Trice's parents, Eric and Mercy, Duke and Baroness Morgan, to come to the front.
- There King Richard presents the present that Tor had asked the king to give to them, from "The Master Wizard Tor" two sets of flying gear and two full shields. a bit more flowery speech to emphasize how impressive and important it is.
- Tor is a bit surprised that people are so enthusiastic about his gifts.
- People around him wonder what the Duke and Baroness could have done to earn such amazing gifts from Master Wizard Tor, and what they could possibly do to get something like that as well.
- Trice, takes this in as part of her plan -- scheming to get Tor a title, convince her parents that Tor loves her, and that they should be married instead of... (doesn't finish the statement)
- Tor is momentarily stunned as he thinks he is far too low of a station to move up to be high enough to be within 3 steps of Trice
- he had heard of warriors gaining enough fame and favor that they moved up the ranks (like the story of Count Wilde), but that would take decades.  He decides to go into a bit of a meditative state to deal with the overwhelming social pressure
