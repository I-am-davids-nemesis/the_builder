# The Next Day

- **When:** the eleventh day, the morning after the queen's dinner (scene 03 ends past the first bell of the morning with Tor asleep). Late morning: everyone else has been up for hours and is waiting on him (invented).
- **Summary:** Tor wakes sore everywhere, hung over though he drank nothing, and Burks bathes and dresses him and sends him to a nearby dining room for breakfast. There the royal women are waiting for him, almost an ambush: Queen Connie, Mercy, Karina, Varley and a stranger. Varley doses him with brown sludge for the after effects of the combat rage he still doesn't believe he had. The stranger is Daritha Scove, a professional go-between, and she's there because someone wants to marry him: Meredith Anne Sorvey. Tor wants nothing to do with the Sorveys (Dorgal, and the dangerous merchant), and after the royal women pull down every polite excuse, he falls back on a prior commitment and then has to come clean: the presents to the Morgans were a campaign to win them round so he could marry Trice, once he earns a title. Daritha takes back a complication, not a refusal. With nothing for him to do, and the queen wanting him kept in the palace, Tor offers to work on small side projects, survives the sisters arguing over whether Lady Priscilla counts, and escapes to his room. ## Beats

1. **Waking** (Hale). Tor wakes up the next day sore and hurting. His head hurts and his body hurts.
2. **Burks.** Burks helps him bathe and dress, and points him to a nearby dining room where, he's told, breakfast is waiting.

`* * *`

3. **Stumbling in** (Daritha). When he finally stumbles in, he finds several people drinking tea, also waiting for breakfast. Enough people that he almost leaves, before the queen tells him to stay.
4. **The ambush.** It turns out a bunch of women are waiting for him, in ambush almost: Connie, Mercy, Karina, Varley, and an older woman Tor hasn't met yet.
5. **The hangover.** Tor is suffering a hangover: not fair, he thinks, since he hasn't been drinking (he mutters it; invented).
6. **Not up to it.** The queen is very pleasant, but he isn't quite up to being as polite as he wants to be. He's a bit grumpy, and asks forgiveness because he's feeling so poor.
7. **The sludge.** Varley hands him some brown sludge to drink, telling him it will help with the headache, the after effects of the combat rage. Tor, who still thinks of himself as a commoner, still doesn't believe he could possibly have a combat rage, but he's game to try any drink that might help his pain at the moment.
8. **Drink it fast.** Varley tells him to drink it fast; it doesn't get better with waiting. He thinks it tastes terrible, but apparently medicine has to taste terrible in order to work.
9. **Daritha Scove.** The queen introduces the stranger, naming her Daritha Scove, as a professional go-between.
10. **"Who was it?"** Tor doesn't think he needs a professional to tell him that someone isn't interested in him, but he's curious to know who he's been too forward with.
11. **Someone wants to marry him.** Obviously that isn't what the woman is there for; she's there because someone is trying to marry him.
12. **A cruel joke?** Tor asks if this is a cruel joke. The queen denies it: marriage is far too serious for such a thing.
13. **Open to marriage?** Tor, a bit baffled, looks to the woman for some kind of confirmation. First, she asks if he's open to marriage. He wasn't expecting anything in the near future, but he always imagined he would marry eventually, so he answers yes.
14. **Above or below.** Daritha continues that Tor's current position might be considered below her clients', but maybe that's a misunderstanding, and his position could be higher than her clients'. She's fishing, on the strength of a whisper from last night (user, November 8, 2026).
15. **Get on with it.** The queen thinks this is taking a bit long and indicates it should be sped up.
16. **The card.** Daritha hands Tor a beautiful card with the woman's name on it: Meredith Anne Sorvey. Tor doesn't think he's met her, but recognizes the last name as his least favorite person at school (Dorgal). Not completely daft, he knows not to say anything too negative out loud. He tries to make himself believe that maybe she just has a bad relative, and maybe she's a great person.
17. **His advisors.** He comes up with a polite excuse: he'll have to discuss this with his advisors here (presumably the royal family), and wouldn't want to drag the social standing of such a fine lady down.
18. **Pushback.** Of course all the royal women push back, with kind things to say about him: his deeds, his personality, his potential.
19. **Not the Sorveys.** Tor really doesn't want to be connected to the Sorvey house. Dorgal was bad enough, but there was also that merchant with the same name who he knew was dangerous (Douglas Sorvey, chapter 2; Tor doesn't know he's Dorgal's father).
20. **A prior commitment.** Tor looks for an excuse, but nobody there is being very helpful, so he eventually lands on using a prior commitment that he's in the middle of trying to resolve, but it's complicated (he doesn't want to reveal the plot to Trice's mother, after all).
21. **Coming clean.** But he eventually has to come clean: the presents to the Morgans were part of a campaign to get them to feel better toward him, so that he could eventually marry her (Trice). All he has to do is earn a title from the king. He knows he's stretching it, and goes pink; the room takes it at face value (user, November 8, 2026).
22. **Mercy.** Mercy doesn't seem to be against the idea: a measured smile, one look at Connie, very few words (user, November 8, 2026).
23. **Karina.** Karina pouts that she didn't already ask Tor to marry her.
24. **Knight.** Varley says Tor should be able to get a title of Knight at the least, since it requires three heroic acts, and he has saved a county (Ford, the river) and saved children (the midden, chapter 8, scene 3) already. A knight counts as minor nobility, enough for Trice to work with (user, November 8, 2026).
25. **The third.** For the third he should have to do something truly great: fight a monster, sail around the world in a boat he made himself, or, since he's a wizard, come up with a feat of magic that is brand new and a true masterwork, no copying allowed.
26. **Connie.** Connie seems to think this is a great idea. She promises to ask the king for him, but he should be prepared for the king to say no.
27. **Not a refusal.** The go-between has to take back news of the complication, but of course it isn't an outright refusal, so there's still hope on that front for the Sorveys. Daritha takes her leave.

She's at a loss for entertainments he could have. Tor volunteers to work on a few small side projects (which ones: to be worked out next; user, November 8, 2026).
