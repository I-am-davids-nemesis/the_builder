# The Next Day

- **Summary:**
- Tor wakes up the next day sore and hurting. His head hurts and his body hurts.  Burks helps him bathe and dress, and points him to a nearby dining room where he is told breakfast is waiting
- when he finally stumbles in, he find several people drinking tea, also waiting for breakfast. Enough people that he almost leaves before the queen tells him to stay
- turns out there are a bunch of women waiting for him, in ambush almost: Connie, Mercy, Karina, Varley, and an older woman Tor has not met yet.
- Tor is suffering a hangover -- not fair, he thinks, since he hasn't been drinking.
- The queen is very pleasant, but he is not quite up to being as polite as he wants to be
- tor is a bit grumpy, and asks forgiveness because he is feeling so poor. Varley hands him some brown sludge to drink, telling him that it will help with the headache, the after effects of the combat rage.
- Tor still doesn't believe that he, who he still thinks of himself as a commoner, could possibly have a combat rage, but he is game to try any drink that might help his pain at the moment.
- Varley tells him to drink it fast -- it doesn't get better with waiting
- he thinks it tastes terrible, but apparently medicine has to taste terrible in order to work
- The queen introduces the stranger, naming her Daritha Scove, as a professional go-between.
- Tor doesn't think he needs a professional to tell him that someone is not interested in him, but he is curious to know who he has been too forward with.
- obviously that is not what the woman is there for; she is there because someone is trying to marry him.
- Tor asks if this is a cruel joke. The queen denies it, claiming that marriage is far too serious for such a thing.
- Tor is a bit baffled, and then looks to the woman for some kind of confirmation.
- First, she asks if he is open to marriage.
- while he wasn't expecting anything in the near future, he did always imagine that he would eventually get married so he responds 'yes'
- Daritha continues that Tor's current position might be considered below her clients, but maybe that is a misunderstanding and his position could be higher than her clients.
- the queen thinks this is taking a bit long and indicates that it should be sped up
- Daritha hands Tor a beautiful card with the name of the woman on it: Meredith Anne Sorvee. Tor doesn't think he has met her, but recognizes the last name as his least favorite person at school.  Tor, not completely daft knows not to say anything too negative out loud. He tries to make himself believe that maybe she just has a bad relative and maybe she is a great person.  He comes up with a polite excuse and says that he will have to discuss this with his advisors here (presumably the royal family), and wouldn't necessarily want to drag the social standing of such a fine lady down.  Of course all the royal women push back and have kind things to say bout him, his deeds, his personality, his potential.
- Tor really doesn't want to be connected to Sorvee house.  Dorgal was bad enough, but there was also that merchant with the same name who he knew was dangerous.
- Tor tries to look for an excuse, but nobody there is being very helpful, so he eventually lands on the idea to use the a prior commitment that he is in the middle of trying to resolve, but its complicated (he doesn't want to reveal the plot to Trice's mother after all)
- but he eventually has to come clean, and says that the presents to the Morgan's were part of a campaign to get them to feel better towards him, so that he could eventually marry her -- all he has to do is earn a title from the king.
- Mercy doesn't seem to be against the idea
- Karina is pouting that she didn't already ask Tor to marry her
- Varley says that Tor should be able to get a title of Knight at the least, since it requires three heroic acts, and he has saved a county and saved children already.
- For the third he should have to do something truly great -- fight a monster, sail around the world in a boat he made himself, or since he is a wizard, come up with a feat of magic that is brand new and a true master work, no copying allowed.
- Connie seems like this is a great idea. She promises to ask the king for him, but be prepared for him to say no.
- the go between has to take back news of the complication, but of course it is not an outright refusal, so there is still hope on that front for Sorvee.
- Tor feels like he just barely escaped a perilous encounter, like going down a river rapids in a canoe by oneself, where you barely avoid the rocks
- Connie seems mildly stress -- she has nothing for Tor to do, and doesn't think he should leave the palace.  She is at a loss for entertainments he could have.  Tor volunteers to work on a few small side projects
- the girls, Karina and Varley tease him a bit more about needing another feat of valor; one arguing with the other that the saving of Lady Pricilla doesn't count because he claimed to be the Duke's friend and made it a joke of romantic rivalry between them, not a serious event at all.
- Tor eventually escapes from their evil clutches and gets back to his room.
