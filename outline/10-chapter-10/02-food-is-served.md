## Beats

1. **Seated** (Varley). Tor is seated at the table a ways down from the king and queen, beside Karina's sister, Varley. He knows her at once (she was at dinner in chapter 5, scene 3, and in his room in chapter 9, scene 2), but some of her features remind him of someone else he can't place (Rolph, by family likeness); the nobles all seem to be related to one degree or another, so that isn't surprising (user, November 8, 2026).
2. **Introductions.** Varley starts a "formal" introduction as a court game, as Veronica, her given name; Tor plays along: his name is Torrance, but his friends call him Tor. She tells him to call her Varley, as the family does (invented; accepted by the user, November 8, 2026).
3. **The plates.** Varley is happy to talk to him, since it's his fault the summer is bearable: he gave her two cooling plates. She confesses her sister almost didn't give them to her, but realized they were so good she didn't even need more than two.
4. **The warning.** She warns Tor about her sister: Karina will likely just try to use him for more magic trinkets.
5. **Doretta.** She's very friendly, and Tor, remembering the story of Doretta (chapter 8, scene 1), realizes he has to be friendly back, and not distracted and aloof, as he apparently comes across.
6. **School.** So he talks to her of school. She goes to school at the palace; they bring in tutors for her.
7. **Flying.** She wants to learn to fly. The council hasn't forbidden her to fly as it has the king, but there aren't enough rigs after the military; nobody else is getting any. That's why everyone is so envious of the gifts he gave the Morgans. Then a little light flirting, plainly a bid, to see if he has any extra he could give her.
8. **The left-hand rig.** Tor admits he has no more, but says he needs to make some new ones, steered more by the left hand than the right, so you could use your right hand for a weapon of some sort. He makes a lot of hand gestures as he says it, and the people around him can clearly hear him and understand he's talking about flying rigs (who will make sure the king hears, invented).
9. **Earmarked.** Varley promises to pay for the rig. Tor thinks that's ridiculous, since she's his best friend's little sister, and promises to earmark one for her. He also promises her and her sister shields, since he has extra of those, and she'll need one if she's going to fly.
10. **Gossip.** Varley keeps flirting, moves closer, and tells him court gossip.
11. **Demanding, not using.** Tor thinks Karina certainly isn't using him; she's just demanding things from him, which he sees as a separate kind of interaction. He hopes Varley doesn't start acting like that with him; that would be disappointing.
12. **Courses.** The meal has lots of courses, and he and Varley both eat only bits of each, being much smaller than the average noble: he because he's a commoner, she because she's still young.
13. **Varley at work.** Varley is skilled at dinner conversation and makes a point of talking with each person around them. A lot of people want to talk to Tor, and a lot of them want to talk about their problems.
14. **The mildew.** One woman (Ward's mother; to Varley "the dowager from the fen country, whom nobody had seen in town since the spring"; Tor doesn't know) has mildew and mold in her home because it's far too damp. Tor realizes it would be simple to dry the air, even easier than the drying field or the river fields, since it's only a little water in the air. He declines to do anything at once, but says he'll definitely work on it and send her something. She tries to pass it off; Tor insists she send him a note about it so he can be sure to get it to her. (Her note can arrive in a later chapter as the hook for the drying build.)
15. **Flirting.** Some of the other women around him flirt, which makes him very uncomfortable: he doesn't know them, and they're much older or married.
16. **Through her mother.** Varley helps a bit: all proposals have to go through her mother (her brother made that a rule, at the Debri house), and her mother has a high bar and has already turned down her older daughter: Karina asked, half seriously, if she might have Tor. Varley thinks it's funny.
17. **Playing along.** Tor is flabbergasted, but plays along, as if the queen were the proper person to manage offers of marriage for a country boy. He misses what Karina's asking means.
18. **Dessert.** It finally arrives: shaved ice so cold it isn't melting, so fine it melts on the tongue. The queen looks down the table at him and seems to be thanking him for it. Tor is sure he had no hand in it, though maybe his new cold box helped a bit (chapter 9, scene 5).
19. **The border.** After dessert, another shouting match erupts between two men seated near each other. Tor is too far away to hear exactly, but it sounds like a border dispute: where a county line is drawn, and what's on each side of it.
20. **The strangling.** Tor wants to ignore it, but then one man grabs the woman with the other man and starts strangling her. Reddish sparks are coming off him, and the air round him is hot as a forge; his grip doesn't tire, and Priscilla's natural shield only just holds. Tor is shocked nobody intervenes, but he gets the feeling at least one of them is in a combat rage, so the people backing away are likely protecting themselves from the combat aura, whatever this one is. The guards hold a ring and wait, as they always do with a rage.
21. **Him, then.** If anyone is going to save the woman, it will have to be him. He checks his shield once as he leaves his seat. He stumbles over, checking it again on the way, and the second check turns it off. She's already turning blue. He drops his walking stick, so he won't have a weapon, and hits the man in the kidney as hard as he can, which isn't hard enough for the man to notice.
22. **Again.** Tor thinks this will probably get him executed, but tries again, because he has to do something to save her.
23. **The kick.** Finally he kicks the man (awkward, with one leg in a splint). The man drops the woman, turns and tries to kill him instead. Tor has to goad him a few times to keep his attention. Pale gold sparks fly from the air at Tor's sides and back, out past where his shield would have held them; he never sees them. Near Varley, Lady Arden says under her breath that it's "the old gold," the color nobody has thrown since the first king's line ended; Varley half remembers the lesson from her tutors and doesn't finish the thought (invented; accepted by the user, November 8, 2026). Only Varley and the few round Lady Arden hear it; it travels later as gossip. He doesn't tire on the splinted leg and takes blows that should break him, and the air round him presses on people the way the duke's does. The man finally runs out of steam after about twenty minutes.
24. **Coming to.** The man seems suddenly to come to, and asks Tor if he's all right: he was discussing border issues with Baron Rochester, and the next thing he knew he was hitting Tor.

25. **The joke** (Rolph). Tor, sure he's going to be killed since everyone saw what he did, makes a joke, shaking: they had a disagreement about a lady, and he's afraid she's going home with him (the joke is on the situation, not on Priscilla; user, November 8, 2026). He smiles at the man, who looks too dazed to follow, and like a killer headache is coming on.
26. **The shield to Rolph.** Rolph has walked up beside Tor; Tor sighs and hands his shield over. After a couple of minutes of nothing happening, he realizes his punishment will likely come after the party, or at least not in front of the guests.
27. **The garden.** He limps out to a side garden; Rolph follows. Two royal guards come out too, looking a bit nervous.
28. **"Something you need?"** Rolph asks if there's something he needs, still holding Tor's shield amulet uncertainly.
29. **The guard's question.** The guard asks Tor if he intends to pursue this further. Tor doesn't understand. The guard needs to know whether he's satisfied or there will be further conflict with the duke; the king's forces will need to know how to respond.
30. **Rolph settles it.** Rolph intervenes, formally, speaking for the crown: the matter is over as far as Tor is concerned; it was never personal, and he only meant to protect Lady Priscilla when he saw her attacked. Rolph doesn't believe Duke Winchester means harm to Tor or to Priscilla; it was a disaster narrowly avoided, and these things happen. Let them know if the duke changes his mind, though Rolph wouldn't fancy the duke's chances against Tor; that's for the duke's honor to decide. But the duke is normally cool-headed and just, so they should expect it to be truly over. He hands the amulet back to Tor and motions him to put it on. (Content from the user's draft of the speech; the prose keeps its content and none of its wording, user, November 8, 2026.)
31. **Shaking.** Tor is shaking with fear.
32. **Connie.** Connie comes out after a bit and hugs Tor. Lady Priscilla asked her to thank him: luckily her natural shield came on enough to keep her alive until Tor came and distracted the duke. Connie commends his bravery. (Priscilla has gone home with Rochester.)
33. **"Why haven't I been arrested?"** Tor, still confused, asks.
34. **The crest.** Rolph, back in his own voice, asks if he's joking: he's still wearing the king's crest over his heart (on the breast of Burks's blue coat). That means Tor wasn't the one doing anything; as far as everyone is concerned, it was the king. Which explains the guard's question. A good thing Tor doesn't hold grudges.
35. **The rage they saw.** Does Tor understand? People saw him go into a battle rage himself, sparks and all, and worried he wouldn't recover at all (that can happen the first couple of times). And the duke hasn't the manpower, or the magical help Tor has or could give the king's army; even his army of 20,000 men would be no match for Tor if he were determined.
36. **The shield off.** Rolph and Connie are a bit confused about why Tor turned his shield off: the sparks flew free of it. Tor is confused again: he felt as if he had a shield on. It worked just like his normal one; he could feel the field and how the forces moved round him, draining into the ground. He says so.
37. **Back in.** Tor just wants to go back to his room, if he's still welcome in the house. The queen: of course he's welcome, but he has to go back to the party to show he's in control of himself. It will terrify those smart enough to understand what's happening. So he has to go in, smile, and pretend nothing happened.
38. **The hush.** He walks back in and the room goes quiet. At once several people converge on him: the duke he just fought; Tovey, to stand at his shoulder in case the duke's rage comes back (he knows rage from the inside, and says nothing); and another big man, even taller than the king. They're much faster than Tor, hobbling on his splinted leg.
39. **The knee.** The duke reaches Tor first and, instead of resuming the fight, goes down on one knee and asks forgiveness for the rage and for attacking him.
40. **No debts between friends.** Tor bows and tries what he did with Tovey (chapter 3): it was nothing, and should be nothing, since they could be friends, and there are no debts between friends, as long as he's asked forgiveness of the person who was actually hurt.
41. **To Priscilla.** The duke thanks him, rises, and goes to see to that directly.
42. **Count Ford.** The big man looks satisfied and holds out his hand to shake Tor's. He's Count Ford, and wants to thank Tor for all his help: the river already made, and, as he heard from the queen, Tor's plan to make up some of the lost crops by giving away more of his own work, all without asking anything of the count (chapter 9, scene 4). If there's anything he or his people can do for Tor, he's at Tor's service.
43. **The king's river.** Tor tries to turn the praise to the king; he's just happy he could play a small part.

44. **The rounds** (Trice). Connie has sent Trice to him. Tor has to stay another couple of hours making the rounds and being introduced. Luckily Trice is beside him, keeping track of everyone, since Tor is certainly not going to remember them all.
45. **Leave to go.** The queen finally indicates he may leave.
46. **His station.** Walking toward the door, Trice tells him she hasn't forgotten what they talked about earlier (scene 01): they just need to get Tor an improvement in his station.
47. **The hand.** Tor blushes, but tries to be courtly and gallant, bends over Trice's hand and kisses it. It's Trice's turn to blush, and she walks away, leaving Tor on his own.
