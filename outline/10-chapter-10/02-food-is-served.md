# Food Is Served

- **Summary:**
- Tor is sat at the table a ways down from the King and Queen. He discovers that he is sitting next to Karina's sister, Varley, or more properly, Veronica.  She looks familiar, but then the nobles all seem to be related to one degree or another, so that isn't surprising, and she was in his room the other day.
- they do a 'formal' introduction to each other; Tor says his name is Torrance, but his friends call him Tor.  Varley is happy to talk to him since it is his fault that the summer is bearable -- he has given her two cooling plates after all.  She confesses that her sister almost didn't give them to her, but realized that they were so good that she didn't even need more than two.
- She warns Tor about her sister -- that she will likely just try to use him for more magic trinkets.
- She is being very friendly, and Tor, remembering the story of Doretta realizes that he has to be friendly to her as well, and can't be to distracted and aloof, like he apparently comes across as.
- So he talks to her of school. He discovers that she goes to school at the palace -- they bring in tutors for her.  She also talks about wanting to learn to fly -- the council hasn't forbidden her to fly like they have the king, but there just aren't enough rigs after the military -- nobody else is getting any.  She explains that is why everyone is so envious of the gifts he gave to the Morgans. Then tries a little light flirting to see if he has any extra ones he could give her.
- Tor admits that he has no more, but claims that he needs to make some new ones --ones that are more controlled by the left hand rather than the right hand so that you could use your right hand for a weapon of some sort.
- he is making a bunch of hand gestures as he says this, and people around him can clearly hear him and understand that he is talking about the flying rigs.
- Varley promises to pay for the rig -- Tor thinks this is ridiculous since she is his best friend's little sister. he promises to earmark one for her.  He also promises her and her sister shields, since he does have extra of those, and she will need one if she is going to fly.
- Veronica/Varley continues to flirt with Tor, and also moves closer to him telling him court gossip.
- Tor thinks Karina is certainly not using him -- she is just demanding things from him, which he sees as a separate kind of interpersonal interaction. And he hopes that Varley doesn't start acting like that with him. - that would be disappointing.
- The meal has lots of courses, and he and Varley both only eat bits of each one as they are much smaller than the average noble -- he because he is a commoner, and she because she is still young
- Tor notices Varley is quite skilled at dinner conversation and makes a point to have conversations with each person around them. Tor found out that a lot of people wanted to talk to him. And a lot of them wanted to talk about problems that they had.
- One woman had a problem of mildew and mold in her home because it was far too moist. Tor realized it would be simple enough to dry the air -- even easier than the drying field or the river fields since it was just a small bit of water in the air.  He also declined to do anything immediately, but said he would definitely work on it and send something to her. she tries to pass it off, but Tor insists that she send him a note about it so he can be sure to get it to her.
- Some of the other women around him try flirting with him, which makes him very uncomfortable since he doesn't know them and they are either much older or married
- Varley helped a bit, mentioning that all proposals had to go through her mother -- her brother had made that a rule. she also mentioned that her mother had a high bar and had already denied her older daughter.
- Tor is flabbergasted but plays along, as if the queen is the appropriate person to manage offers of marriage for him, a country boy.
- Dessert finally arrives, and it is an extremely cold shaved ice dish -- so cold it isn't melting, and so finely shaved that it melts on the tongue.  The queen looks down the table at him and seems to be thanking him for the dish; Tor is sure he didn't have any hand in preparing it, although maybe his new cold box could have helped a bit.
- after dessert, another shouting match erupts between two men seated near each other.  Tor is a bit too far away to hear exactly what is going on but it sounds like some kind of border dispute about exactly where a county line is drawn and what exactly is on each side of the line.
- Tor wanted to just ignore it, but then he sees one of the men grab the woman with the other man and start strangling her.  Tor is shocked nobody is intervening, but he also gets the feeling that at least one of them is in a combat rage, so the people backing away are likely protecting themselves from the combat aura, whatever this one is.
- he decides that if anyone is going to save the woman it is going to have to be him
- he stumbles over to the two men
