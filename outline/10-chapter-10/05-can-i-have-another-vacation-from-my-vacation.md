# Can I Have Another Vacation From My Vacation

- **Summary:**
- Tor starts making a list of the things he can work on; first there is the left-handed flying rig -- something like that could make a difference, particularly against the Austran's who had machines that could fly so fast they made a vast roaring sound, or at least so Kolb had said.
- Tor, of course, gets distracted by the first thing on the list. As long as he is switching from right to left hand, he could make some other modifications too -- improve the lines of force to make it go faster; change the way the turning works to make that less cumbersome; change the way the air around you flows to keep it fresh, but not too strong, so the shield doesn't need to absorb all the force -- he thought he had felt it warming up on the last fast flight he had taken.
- He really didn't want to think about the Sorvey problem, so instead of moving on, he made a hundred copies of the new flying rig from the template he designed.  He was working -- nobody could fault him for ignoring people if he was working, right?
- he was glad that Meredith had the Sorvey name -- imagine if she had a different last name, but was still connected to the family -- he might have said yes and then he would be in a fine mess.  Nothing good could come from being related to Dorgal
- Continuing his working streak, he started designing the cargo floats, and after a half week he had a hundred copies of those too.
- Rolph had been spending the time with Sara; Tor couldn't blame him, she was pretty great.  But then, just when Tor was finished with the cargo plates, and was taking a break before figuring out what to work on next, Rolph dropped by to visit
- Rolph wanted to know if Tor had finished saving the kingdom all by himself? (a joke) He suggested that maybe Tor should not work all the time, but instead see some of the people who wanted to talk with him.
- For instance the new potential in-laws.  They are scheduled to come tomorrow at 9 for dinner.  They seem to be very happy with Tor, so it should be quite a good dinner -- no fights.
- Tor tentatively agrees that maybe that would be a good idea.  Which reminds him that he needs to get the drying fields together -- he asks Rolph to pick up the template from Debri house -- he would, but the Queen has asked him to stay in, and he isn't in the habit of ignoring the queen's orders.
- Rolph thinks this is a great idea -- another excuse to go see Sara.  He winks at Tor and claims that it should be quick, well, no more than 4 hours.
- Since Rolph had the template back before dinner, Tor just chalks it up to city travel times. he thinks he really has to get on figuring something out that would be easier getting around the city instead of slow miserable carriages.
- By the next evening, Tor has 50 more cargo haulers and 100 drying fields ready to go. After Tor wonders about a better strap than his hemp string for the flying devices, Burks is able to find somehow enough leather straps to fit 50 of the flying rigs as well.  Tor had asked Burks where he could get a chest, and the man had come through again with a large but ornate wooden box.  The chest has very fancy carvings of flowers and birds on it.  Another priceless treasure in itself as far as Tor can tell.  He puts the 200 different devices into the trunk.  Rolph comes at 7pm to pick him up for their dinner, and Burks and another man, help him load the chest onto the carriage. Luckily Rolph is big enough that he isn't worried about getting it off the carriage at their destination.
- When he gets to the Morgan estate, Tor doesn't notice how small it is, being merely a large mansion instead of the imposing edifice of Debri house.  Rolph would never mention it either.
- The door is opened by the house butler (unnamed), and they bring the box into the sitting room.
- Without warning Tor is accosted by Trice, moving at near combat speeds. he is so shocked he can't even respond. But it turns out well, with Trice kissing him and telling him she loves him, that this is so much better.  Rolph chuckles politely at his friends obvious surprise and confusion at this reception. Until Trices father, politely breaks them up inviting them in to dinner.
