# Can I Have Another Vacation From My Vacation

- **When:** from the afternoon of the eleventh day (straight on from scene 04, Tor escaping to "find a table") to the evening of the Morgan dinner, about a week later: the rig copies, then half a week on the cargo floats, then Rolph's visit, then one more day (invented arithmetic from the user's notes; see Questions).
- **Summary:** Kept in the palace with nothing to do, Tor makes a list of things to work on and gets no further than the first item: the left-handed flying rig, which matters against the Austrans, whose machines fly so fast they roar (Kolb). Switching hands, he improves the rest of it too (faster lines of force, easier turning, fresh air flowing round the rider so the shield doesn't take the whole wind), and then, so as not to think about the Sorvey problem, makes a hundred copies. He's glad Meredith carries the Sorvey name, since nothing good could come of being related to Dorgal. He goes straight on to cargo floats, and after half a week has a hundred of those too. Rolph, who has been spending his time with Sara, drops in as Tor finishes, asks whether he's saved the kingdom single-handed yet, and tells him to see people instead: his potential in-laws, the Morgans, expect him to dinner tomorrow at the ninth bell, and they seem very happy with him. Tor agrees, and sends Rolph to fetch the drying-field template from Debri House, since the queen wants him kept in; Rolph winks that it will be quick, four bells at most, and takes the excuse to see Sara. Tor puts Rolph's slow return down to city traffic and resolves to build something better than carriages. By the next evening he has fifty more cargo haulers and a hundred drying fields; Burks finds leather straps for fifty rigs and a carved chest, and two hundred devices go into it. Rolph collects him at the seventh bell. ## Beats

1. **The list** (Tor alone). Tor starts making a list of the things he can work on. First, the left-handed flying rig (promised to Varley, chapter 10, scene 2): something like that could make a difference, particularly against the Austrans, who had machines that could fly so fast they made a vast roaring sound, or at least so Kolb had said.
2. **Distracted by the first item.** Tor, of course, gets distracted by the first thing on the list. As long as he's switching it from right hand to left, he could make other changes too:
   - improve the lines of force so it goes faster;
   - change the way the turning works so it's less cumbersome;
   - change the way the air flows around the rider to keep it fresh, but not too strong, so the shield doesn't have to absorb all the force. He thought he had felt it warming up on the last fast flight he took.
3. **The template.** He designs the new rig as a template (a novel build; invented: a short working trance, seen as lost bells and dried ink).
4. **Not the Sorvey problem.** He really doesn't want to think about the Sorvey problem, so instead of moving on, he makes a hundred copies of the new rig from the template. He's working: nobody could fault him for ignoring people if he's working, right? (He mutters it; invented.)
5. **The name.** He's glad Meredith has the Sorvey name. Imagine if she'd had a different last name but was still connected to the family: he might have said yes, and then he'd be in a fine mess. Nothing good could come of being related to Dorgal. (Dramatic irony, invented: names hide connections all round him, Rolph and Burks among them, and the reader knows it.)
6. **The cargo floats.** Continuing his working streak, he starts designing the cargo floats (the flying cart for Mercy's fruit, chapter 9, scene 5; `codex/tension-ledger.md`, "Mercy's orchards"). After half a week he has a hundred copies of those too.
7. **Rolph and Sara.** Rolph has been spending the time with Sara. Tor can't blame him; she's pretty great.
8. **A break.** Just as Tor finishes the cargo plates and is taking a break before working out what to do next, Rolph drops by.

`* * *`

9. **Saving the kingdom** (Rolph). Rolph wants to know if Tor has finished saving the kingdom all by himself (a joke). He suggests Tor not work all the time, but instead see some of the people who want to talk with him.
10. **The in-laws.** For instance, the new potential in-laws. They're expecting him tomorrow at the ninth bell for dinner. They seem very happy with Tor, so it should be quite a good dinner: no fights.
11. **Tentatively.** Tor tentatively agrees that maybe that would be a good idea.
12. **The drying fields.** Which reminds him that he needs to get the drying fields together. He asks Rolph to pick up the template from Debri House.
13. **The queen's orders.** He would go himself, but the queen has asked him to stay in, and he isn't in the habit of ignoring the queen's orders.
14. **Another excuse.** Rolph thinks this is a great idea: another excuse to go and see Sara.
15. **Four bells.** He winks at Tor and says it should be quick; well, no more than four bells.

`* * *`

16. **Before dinner** (Hale). Rolph has the template back before dinner (that same day), and Tor just chalks it up to city travel times.
17. **Carriages.** He thinks he really has to get on with working out something easier for getting around the city than slow, miserable carriages (the next build; out loud, invented).
18. **The next evening.** By the next evening Tor has fifty more cargo haulers and a hundred drying fields ready to go.
19. **Straps.** Tor wonders aloud about a better strap than his hemp string for the flying devices, and Burks somehow finds enough leather straps to fit fifty of the rigs.
20. **The chest.** Tor had asked Burks where he could get a chest, and the man has come through again: a large but ornate wooden box, with very fancy carvings of flowers and birds on it. Another priceless treasure in itself, as far as Tor can tell.
21. **Packing.** He puts the two hundred devices into the trunk (fifty strapped rigs, fifty cargo haulers, a hundred drying fields; assumed from the counts).
22. **Seventh bell.** Rolph comes at the seventh bell of the evening to pick him up for dinner, and Burks and another man help load the chest onto the carriage. Luckily, Rolph is big enough that Tor isn't worried about getting it off the carriage at the other end.

`* * *`

23. **The Morgan house** (Rolph). When he gets to the Morgan house, Tor doesn't notice how small it is, merely a large mansion instead of the imposing edifice of Debri House. Rolph would never mention it either.
24. **The butler.** The door is opened by the house butler (unnamed), and they bring the box into the sitting room.
25. **Trice.** Without warning, Tor is accosted by Trice, moving at near-combat speed.
26. **Shocked.** He's so shocked he can't even respond.
27. **"So much better."** But it turns out well: Trice kisses him and tells him she loves him, that this is so much better.
28. **Rolph chuckles.** Rolph chuckles politely at his friend's obvious surprise and confusion at this reception.
29. **Dinner.** Until Trice's father, Duke Eric, politely breaks them up and invites them in to dinner. End.
