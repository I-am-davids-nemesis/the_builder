# Home Sweet Home

- **When:** arrival at Two Rivers, straight on from scene 02's four-hour flight. Scene 02 ends at noon with the party hovering over the village before landing: Tor stops in the air, "That's it. That's home." The landing belongs to this scene. Scene 02 also sketched the village from above (two rivers meeting, a square chapel tower, a green, the bakery chimney smoking too much for noon); invented, and still an open question in scene 02's outline.
- **Summary:** They haven't even finished landing when the mayor comes out to meet them, speaking the local dialect, and Tor has to translate both ways. The mayor recognizes Tor and wonders if he's working for the lords as their personal baker; Tor laughs and introduces his friends, and, saving the best for last, his fiancée Patricia. At the bakery three of his younger siblings burst out to hug him, then go wary at the strangers; his father comes out in his apron, and, since Tor's mother can't leave her berry pies, offers cider at an outdoor table. In the cellar, his father teases Tor about the "merchant girl" and asks only whether he loves her: love makes odd matches, he says; he married Tor's mother, after all. Tor's mother comes out, hugs him, and walks toward the table, looking younger and more regal with every step. Duke Eric stares, stands: "Laurie?" "Hello Eric. Mercy. How have you been?" Mercy runs to hug her: "We all thought you were dead!" End of chapter, on the cliffhanger. From the writer's chapter notes (user, November 10, 2026); canon unless marked.
- **POV / mode:** rotating third; not yet chosen (see Questions). Tor isn't alone, and the reunion isn't the kind of trauma that gives him the viewpoint (AGENTS.md §3). Proposed (invented): **Trice** for the landing, the yard and the table, since she is meeting her future family, knows her parents' faces and heard Laurali named in scene 02; a `* * *` break to **Tor's father** for the cellar, the one stretch Trice can't witness; back to Trice for the ending. Tor's mother, as the count's daughter, is not an Ancient, but she keeps the viewpoint closed anyway: her secret is the chapter's hook.
- **Location:** Two Rivers (`codex/locations/two-rivers.md`): the landing on the green (invented), the walk to the bakery, the bakery door and yard, the cellar under the house (canon: warm in winter, cool in summer, the butter kept there), and an outdoor picnic table (user). The place squeezes Tor's past (§6). This scene puts the bakery in the village itself, near the middle, settling the location file's "village or homestead" question (invented, from scene 02's smoking chimney).
- **Present:**
  - **Landing:** Tor; Rolph; Trice; Sara; Mercy, Duke Eric and Tovey (user, November 11, 2026); the mayor, **Tom** (user's notes; that Tom is the mayor's name is assumed).
  - **Bakery:** the above, less the mayor (assumed); Tor's brother **Terry** and sisters **Tara** and **Taman**, the youngest (user; see Questions on the codex's sibling list); Tor's father; Tor's mother ("Laurie").
  - **Cellar:** Tor and his father only.
- **Reader learns:**
  - Two Rivers speaks its own dialect, fluid enough that the nobles can't follow it, and Tor translates both ways (user).
  - Tor's family is real, warm and busy; the bakery runs at noon (user).
  - Tor introduces Rolph as "my roommate," and Mercy by her barony (user's notes: "the Morgans (Duke and Baroness), Count Tovey, and his roommate Rolph, as well as merchant Sara").
  - Tor's father asks only whether he loves her; Tor does, and says the marriage was Trice's idea (user).
  - "Love can make odd matches": his father married Tor's mother, a hint that something strange happened there (user).
  - Tor's mother is **Laurie**, known to Mercy and Eric from their youth, and believed dead (user). The reader, who heard in scene 02 that the count's daughter **Laurali** was at school with the parents, and that her story was "another story," can make the connection; nobody says it aloud (invented).
- **Hidden (and how):**
  - **Who Laurie is, why she left, and why everyone thought her dead:** the chapter ends on Mercy's cry; the scene cuts before any answer.
  - **How much Tor's father knows:** only his one line in the cellar, said lightly, with no explanation (user).
  - **Tor's clone secret** (`codex/characters/supporting/green.md`): untouched. A reader who links Laurie to Laurali will likely take Tor for the count's grandson, which explains the likeness to Burks and the middle name Green on its own and covers the real secret (invented reading; ask).
  - **How Laurie looks younger:** seen only from outside, as a change as she walks; not explained (user; mechanism open).
  - **Why Eric takes minutes to know her:** shown, not explained. The noble condition (`codex/canon-notes.md`: nobles don't easily recognize people who are too small) may be part of it (assumed).

## Beats

1. **The landing.** They haven't even finished landing when the mayor comes out to meet them. Nobody in Two Rivers has ever seen a noble (canon); seven people and ten trunks come down out of the sky onto the green (invented detail). He greets them in the local dialect. (user)
2. **The dialect.** The dialect is fluid, constantly changing with who is talking, their mood, and who they're talking to. There is supposed to be a linguistic underpinning that makes it a better base for learning other languages; Tor is smart and adaptable enough that this isn't really apparent from his abilities, but perhaps it helped a bit (it fits his shifting accents, `codex/characters/tor.md`). The first meetings should be a bit awkward in dialect, and Tor has to translate. (user)
3. **"Lords and Ladies."** Tor translates the mayor: "Welcome Lords and Ladies to Two Rivers. We are happy to have you." (user)
4. **The personal baker.** The mayor stares at Tor and suddenly recognizes him, and wonders if he's working for the lords as their personal baker. (user)
5. **Introductions.** Tor laughs, and says they're his friends from school and, if the mayor can believe it, his fiancée and her parents. He introduces the Morgans (Duke and Baroness), Count Tovey, his roommate Rolph, and merchant Sara; and then, saving the best for last, his fiancée Patricia. (user)
6. **Both ways.** He has to translate it all back to the nobles, and tell Tom he's looking for his parents, to introduce them to the Morgans. (user)
7. **The luggage.** They head over to the bakery and make a pile of their luggage. (user) The luggage plates set the trunks down by the door (invented).
8. **The children.** Just before Tor opens the door, three kids burst out and grab him in a big hug: his brother Terry, his sister Tara, and his youngest sister, Taman. (user) Tor is still on his stick with his leg splinted (scene 02), so the hug nearly knocks him over (invented).
9. **Wary.** As soon as they see the strangers, they pull back, suddenly wary, wondering who they are. (user)
10. **Father.** Tor starts to explain, but his father comes out, wearing his apron, with a bit of flour over his clothes. Tor introduces his friends and his fiancée's family. (user)
11. **The pies.** His mother is just finishing some berry pies, and can't come out yet or they'll burn. His father, everything in a thick accent, offers cider and leads everyone to an outdoor picnic table, and he and Tor go to fetch the cider from the cellar. (user)
12. **The merchant girl.** While they get the drinks, his father teases him about bringing home a merchant girl from the city to marry. Tor denies she's a merchant: she's a *ducharina*. He asks his father not to tell her she shouldn't marry a simple country boy like him (user's note reads "not to tell them not to marry"; see Questions). His father just laughs, and asks only whether he loves her. (user)
13. **Trees.** Tor says yes. Her name fits in well there, since *Trice* sounds a bit like *trees* in the local dialect. He's friends with her, and he loves her enough to marry her. She's the one who had the idea, but it seems pretty great to Tor. (user)
14. **Odd matches.** His father agrees that love can make odd matches: he's married to Tor's mother, after all. A hint that something strange happened there. (user)
15. **Mother.** They bring the cider and glasses to the table, and Tor ends up standing between the bakery and the table, so he's the first person his mother sees when she comes out. She hugs him and tells him the family has been missing him. (user)
16. **The walk.** Tor starts to introduce her to his friends, but she looks over at the table and starts walking toward it, and as she walks she seems to grow visibly younger and more regal. (user) Her speech may leave the dialect as she comes, so that her first words reach the nobles without Tor (invented; see Questions).
17. **"Laurie?"** Duke Eric looks at Tor's mother oddly for a few minutes, then stands. "Laurie?" (user)
18. **"Hello Eric."** She replies: "Hello Eric. Mercy. How have you been?" (user)
19. **"We all thought you were dead!"** Mercy cries "Laurie!", runs over to hug her, and says, "We all thought you were dead!" (user)
20. **Cut.** End of chapter, on the cliffhanger. (user)

**Questions:**

1. **Viewpoint:** who holds the scene?
   - Default: Trice for the landing, yard and table; Tor's father for the cellar; Trice again for the ending.
   - Option: Sara throughout, continuing scene 02, with the cellar told afterward in Tor's translation or skipped.
   - Option: Tor's father throughout, so the hint and the ending come from a man who knows something.
   - Option: Rolph, who heard "Laurali" as "another story" and may know more than he said.
2. **The siblings:** how do Terry, Tara and Taman fit the codex, which has Terlee and Tilda older, twins, Tam, Tessa (youngest, nine) and Teira younger?
   - Default: Add Terry and Tara as younger siblings; Taman is the full name of the chapter 5 draft's "Tam"; Tessa stays the youngest, so the note's "youngest sister" means the youngest of the three.
   - Option: Taman replaces Tessa as the youngest sister; rename Tessa in the earlier drafts.
   - Option: Terry and Tara are the twins.
3. **The rest of the family:** where are Terlee, Tilda, Teira and the others when Tor arrives?
   - Default: At work or on errands round the village; they don't appear in this scene and turn up in chapter 12.
   - Option: Some are in the kitchen with their mother, and come out behind her at the end.
4. **The mayor:** is the mayor's name Tom, and does he stay for the bakery?
   - Default: Yes, Tom; he leaves them at the bakery to spread the news.
   - Option: "Tom" in the notes is someone else, a neighbor who runs to fetch the parents.
5. **Laurie and Laurali:** is Tor's mother the count's daughter Laurali from scene 02?
   - Default: Yes; nobody says so in this scene, and the reader makes the link from the name.
   - Option: Yes, and Rolph or Trice says "Laurali" under their breath as Mercy runs, to make it unmistakable.
   - Option: Not decided; keep "Laurie" ambiguous for now.
6. **Grandson or clone:** if Laurie is Laurali, is she Tor's birth mother, given that Tor is a clone of Green?
   - Default: Book 1 never says; the reader is left to think Tor is the count's grandson, which covers the clone secret.
   - Option: She is not his birth mother, and his father knows it; leave the hint for book 2.
7. **Younger and more regal:** what makes her look younger as she walks?
   - Default: She lets go of something she has held for twenty years (a field that makes her look plain and older); unexplained in book 1.
   - Option: Nothing magic: posture, the apron coming off, the village stoop dropping, seen through someone who knows her.
8. **Her voice:** does Laurie drop the dialect when she greets Eric and Mercy?
   - Default: Yes; she speaks to them in clean Hill speech, which is the first thing that tells Tor something is wrong (invented).
   - Option: She speaks dialect and Tor, out of habit, starts translating "Hello Eric" before he hears what he's saying.
9. **Why Eric is slow:** why does it take Eric minutes to know her, and why him before Mercy?
   - Default: She was presumed dead and looks like a village baker's wife until she changes; Eric is facing the bakery and Mercy has her back to it.
   - Option: The noble condition, if Laurie is small, plays a part.
10. **The father's line:** does Tor's father know who his wife is?
    - Default: Yes, and he has always known; "odd matches" is said lightly to his son, who misses it.
    - Option: He knows she was someone, not who.
11. **"Not to tell them":** what does Tor ask his father in the cellar?
    - Default: Not to tell Trice, or her parents, that she shouldn't marry a simple country boy.
    - Option: Not to tell the village yet, before her parents have met his.
12. **Rolph's introduction:** does Tor introduce Rolph only as his roommate, hiding that he's the prince?
    - Default: Yes, out of habit and to spare the mayor; Rolph is delighted to be nobody for once.
    - Option: Tor forgets the prince entirely; nobody corrects him.
13. **Tor's leg:** how does Tor manage the cellar stairs and the cider on a stick?
    - Default: His father carries the cider and Tor carries the glasses on a luggage plate, which his father eyes and doesn't remark on.
    - Option: Tor flies down the cellar stairs on his rig.
14. **The village from the air:** does scene 02's picture (two rivers, square chapel tower, green with a cow, the bakery near the middle) stand?
    - Default: Yes; they land on the green, and the bakery faces it.
    - Option: The bakery sits at the edge of the village by the river, with the picnic table under a tree.
