# Home Sweet Home

- **When:** arrival at Two Rivers, straight on from scene 02's four-hour flight.
- **Summary:** People had even completely landed when the mayor came out to meet them.  He greets them in the local dialect. (Note: the idea behind the dialect is that it is a bit more fluid, constantly changing depending on the person who's talking, their mood, who they are talking to, etc.  There is supposed to be a linguistic underpinning that makes the dialect a better base for learning other languages from; unfortunately, Tor is smart enough, and adaptable enough, that isn't really apparent from his abilities, but perhaps it helped a bit.  in any case, the initial meetings should be a bit awkward in terms of dialect, requiring Tor to translate)
- Tor translates the mayor as saying "Welcome Lords and Ladies to Two Rivers.  We are happy to have you"
- The mayor stares at Tor, and suddenly recognizes him.  He is wondering if he is working for the lords as their personal baker.
- Tor laughs, and says that they are his friends from school, and, if he can believe it, his fiancee and her parents.  He introduces the Morgans (Duke and Baroness), Count Tovey, and his roommate Rolph, as well as merchant Sara.  And then saving the best for last, his fiancee Patricia.
- Then he has to translate the conversation back to the nobles and tell Tom that he is looking for his parents to introduce them to the Morgans
- So, they head over to the bakery, and make a pile of their luggage.
- Just before he opens the door, three kids burst out and grab him in a big hug -- his brother Terry, his sister Tara, and his youngest sister, Taman.
- But as soon as they see the strangers, they pull back and are suddenly pretty wary, wondering who they are.
- He starts to explain, but his father comes out, wearing his apron, and a bit of flour over his clothes.  he then introduces his friends and his fiancee's family
- Apparently his mother is just finishing up with some berry pies, and can't quite come out yet or they will burn. So his father (again, everything in a thick accent) offers cider, and leads everyone over to an outdoor picnic table, and he and Tor go grab the cider from the cellar.
- While they are getting the drinks, his father teases him about bringing home a merchant girl to marry from the city.  Tor denies she is a merchant, calling her a ducharina. He asks his dad not to tell them not to marry a simple country boy like him, and his father just laughs. he just asks if he loves her.
- Tor agrees -- her name fits in well there, as Trice sounds a bit like trees in the local dialect. But he is friends with her and loves her enough to marry her.  She is the one who had the idea, but it seems pretty great to Tor.
- his father agrees that love can make odd matches -- he is married to Tor's mother after all (which is a hint that something weird happened there)
- So they get the cider and glasses to the table and Tor ends up standing between the bakery and the table, and is the first person his mother sees when she comes out of the bakery.  She gives him a hug and tells him that the family has been missing him.
- Tor starts to introduce her to his friends, but her mother looks over, and starts to walk over -- as she walks over she seems to get visibly younger and more regal as she walks over
- Duke Morgan looks at Tors mom oddly for a few minutes, and then stands: "Laurie?" he asks
- She replies: "Hello Eric. Mercy. how have you been?"
- Mercy yells out, "Laurie!" and then runs over to hug her, saying "we all thought you were dead!"
- end of chapter cliffhanger
