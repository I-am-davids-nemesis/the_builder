# Meet the Parents

- **Summary:**
- They get to a back gate in another giant white wall. There are guards in purple and black — this is the uniform of the royal guard. Tor recognizes that they are royal guards and some part of his brain is really frustrated that he doesn’t see the obvious consequence. The head gate guard is George who greets Rolph as “ young sir”. Rolph code switches and starts speaking like a noble— more like Tovey than a merchant like Sara. Tor thinks this is quite funny and puts on a noble accent himself as a joke, making a joke about staying in the “small house” that he can see the roof of above the wall — small only in comparison to the massive building that is quite a bit further away, but still behind the wall. Rolph thinks the accent is actually quite appropriate for his house and advises tor to keep that accent and maybe develop another one for out in the city. This is a good chance to see Tors adaptive behavior by having his word choice and diction evolve to suit the people he is around. Just to be clear the reference to staying in the small house is a joke meant to invoke the idea of having to stay in a outhouse as that is the kind of small house that they have in Two Rivers

They get through the gate with a warning from the guard that they have a list of everyone allowed inside

Rolph takes them to the ‘small house’ which is much bigger than any building tor has been in. Rolph tells him they will have dinner later and to get cleaned up. 
Tor is astonished at his suite of rooms — bigger than he could imagine and very fancy. He feels too dirty to even walk in them. He immediately goes to the bathroom and washes himself as clean as possible. Then he steps out of the bath  and starts sweating; he walks over to his trunk to get clean clothes and realizes the has a cooling plate.  He immediately takes one out and turns it on, and puts it on the center of a giant table with a nice green table cloth. The copper matches the green.
he cools off; when he turns around he sees a girl in his room, realizing he doesn't have his shirt back on yet.  She says "your not Alphie... sorry to catch you dressing..."
he figures out this must be Rolph's sister, and introduces himself; She responds "oh, Alphie's friend from school, but I guess you all call him Rolph there. I'm Karina". She steps into the room and immediately appreciates the cool; Tor has finished dressing by now.  She thinks Tor must be important to already get cooling plates -- she doesn't have one in her room yet.  She immediately makes plans to spend as much time as possible in Tor's room.  Tor is immensely pleased, because he is prepared.  He feels a bit of shock coming from Karina, like she thought he was plotting something.  Anyway, he gives her four of the heat exchange plates, two for her and two for her sister.  Karina is pleased, and a little worried to be getting such a grand present.  Tor is happy because "I brought them as gifts for Rolph's family. (although, I guess I need to start calling him Alphie, or Alphonse now that we are in the city)". She appreciates them, and does some minor flirting "nobody has two, except of course my parents, and obviously you..."  She thanks him and leaves; then shyly sticks her head back in and asks where Alphie is.  Tor is confused a bit because the sense coming off her (off her field) was oddly sly -- not evil, but more self-focused.  Tor suddenly realizes that he is going to have to walk around, and wishes that he could carry the cool with himself.  He suddenly realizes that this is a good idea, and starts to figure out how to make a personal temperature field that keeps a person at the perfect temperature.  Cool in the hot weather, and warm in cold.  If he is clever enough, he figures he can just steal the heat from the ground.  He starts working on it until Rolph comes in and shakes him for dinner, breaking his concentration.  "Mothers here, but Father couldn't make it"
Rolph drags Tor off to dinner, away from his sketches of the new device.  Tor is suddenly worried that his brown clothes aren't good enough, particularly since Rolph's sister was in such a pretty dress.  Rolph tells him not to worry about it: part of the reason they are there is to get comfortable and find some appropriate clothes.  Rolph thinks some of his old clothes might fit Tor (since he is so much smaller).
