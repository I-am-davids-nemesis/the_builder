# Testing the Field

- **Summary:**
- Tor makes an extra batch of the personal cooling devices, just in case.  Then he has to walk around the house, inside and out, to test them.
- Around noon, a man comes to the door with an armful of clothing.  The man is slightly taller than Tor, but looks strangely familiar.  Even his voice has a certain quality that is familiar.
- “Sir? Master Alphonse suggested that these clothes be brought to you, for your outing later this afternoon?” The man walked into the room and seemed surprised that the bed was made. At least he stared in that general direction, for several moments.
* tor shows off his personal cooling device that he made the night before instead of sleeping.  Tor offers one to the man to help test for him. The man takes the amulet and puts it on (Tor had some hemp string he used as a necklace to hang it on).  It doesn't seem to do anything in the cool room; Tor suggests he goes out and tests it, maybe in the kitchen.  The man agrees, but asks if Tor wants to have breakfast before a bath.  Tor decides to have a bath first, and the man leaves to get the food ready.
* Tor takes a bath, but finds the water oddly cool -- not cold, but not hot either.  Then he realizes he is wearing the personal cooling device and takes it off to protect the string.
* Tor gets out of the bath, and finds that there is a nice breakfast spread laid out for him.  The man reports that he tested the device, outside, and in the kitchen and reports that the device works perfectly. "most effective, sir"
* Tor finally introduces himself to prevent the man from calling him 'sir', and asks who the man is.  The man introduces himself as Burks.
* Burks tries to return the amulet, but Tor insists that it is a gift and he should keep it.
* after breakfast, the man helps Tor choose and wear one of Rolph's old clothes sets.  Afterwards, Burks leaves Tor
