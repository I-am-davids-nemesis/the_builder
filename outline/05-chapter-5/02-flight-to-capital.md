# Flight to Capital

- **Summary:**
- when they go to leave, Tovey has a coach ready to go, but Tor shows up and tells everyone they are flying.  Trice makes a big deal about Tor having to carry all three of her trunks then.  Tor pulls out his trunk plates and gets everybody's trunks all tied on, and hands out the copper follow buttons.  His trunk is quite heavy because he has brought a lot of copper plates with different magic devices on them to give Rolph's mom as presents, and to give to other people in case they are needed.
- Tovey is worried about going to fast to breathe, or even see (he's tried it and found that it gets quite cold and hard to breathe when you go to fast, and then his eyes watered and he couldn't see where he was going). Tor has a solution in a new shield design that has a teardrop shape that blocks wind at the front.  Teardrop shaped because it will go through the air better -- like a drip from a faucet but turned on its side.
- it takes about 10 hours to fly, but everyone wanted to stop and eat at some of the nicer cities along the way, and to use the facilities.  Tor is a bit shy around all the people he doesn't know. He also feels like a country bumpkin staring at all the new places.  People stare at them quite a bit -- not too many flying people drop by.  Some people are even worried that its an Austran invasion, but they calm down when they see Count Thompson and Rolph.
- The last part of the flight takes a bit longer as they need to follow a river to get to the right place -- and they had to figure out if it was the right river (by finding a well maintained road beside it with mile marker to capital).
- Tor starts worrying that his "homemade trinkets" will be a worthy enough present
- Everyone assures him that he will be a hit as long as he doesn't forget to talk to people.
- As they come in view of the city, it is 10x larger than anything Tor has seen before, with a thick white wall surrounding it. Most of the buildings are white stone, but there is color everywhere.
- As they are flying in, a group of men met them with military grade force lances in their left hands, and Tors flying device in their right hand, held on by sturdy leather strapping.
- the men are wearing city guard uniforms in red and white. one of them comments on how cool the flying trunks are before the leader silences him.
- Soldiers make an odd salute with their left hands (since obviously their right hands are occupied).  The nobles nod, and Sara bobs in an awkward mid-air curtsey.  Tor decides to copy the nobles and nods and says greetings.
- The guards demand identification (politely) The count identifies everyone, calling Rolph 'Alphonse -- but we all call him Rolph at school and when traveling... for safety, you understand'
- The guards welcome them to the capital (without giving away that Alphonse is the prince, but just barely)
- The guards inform them that flying is not allowed in the city -- its too new for appropriate rules.
- guard captain Curtis leads them to the gate to await a carriage, and explains that there have already been accidents.
- there are already carriages ready when they get to the gate, one for the people and a couple for the luggage.
- its super hot, even in the shade, but they endure the carriage ride to a huge house; Tovey dismisses it as his third house for when he has to go to the capital in the fall for the council of counts.
- Tovey claims that flying in was much superior to riding a carriage in, and is sure all the nobles will want to do it.
- Servants aren't quite ready for Tovey to be back -- they thought it would take at least a week to ride the carriage in.
Tor feels bad that the servants are not prepared, since he insisted they all fly in.
Tor can feel that the head servant is grateful, even though he doesn't make any outward sign; Tor realizes that he can pick up this strong feeling 'by magic'.  Its not super odd, since he has heard about it before in theory class, but he has never experienced it himself since he has focused on building devices that have physical effects.
The servants bring cool water, and the ice even has chunks of ice in it.  Tor is very impressed and thinks that would be a neat effect to make an ice machine, as that would go over very well in a place as hot as the capital.  Tor's mind starts to drift off into how he could make it, and conversely an oven. Trice has to physically distract him from going off into a trance.
Tor wonders if all noble women are as violent as Trice; most of his sisters are relatively meek, except Teira, and they didn't hit him nearly as much as Trice did. (even if it is mostly in combat class).
Trice reminds them that they are all going out to their own places now, and will meet up the next day at Debri house for luncheon (mid afternoon)
