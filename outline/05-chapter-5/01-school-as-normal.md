# Scene 1

- **Summary:**
- Tor goes back to being a student for a while.  He distributes flying devices to his friends, and gives a few to Kolb, since he suggested it.
- Half-way through spring, its a hot day, Tor is sweating in his room working on coming up with a new design, and Rolph comes in, laughing at him because he forgot to use the heating plate to cool the room.  Rolph comments that the rest of the school is very happy in their cool rooms but Tor is sweating away after having made the cooling device.
- Rolph also suggests that they visit the capital over summer break.  Tovey has to go back 'for work', and is expecting Tor to come to stay with him.  Rolph suggests that he has to stay with his family, because Tor is basically family since he and Tor are best friends.  Tor decides that he is not needed at Two Bends since he has plenty of brothers and sisters to help out in the bakery, and his youngest sister is now old enough to help out with the baking, freeing up two people (her to help, and the one who was watching her before).
- Tor agrees, thinking that of course they will fly, but he doesn't mention this to anyone.  He immediately realizes that he needs to have extra new devices to fly their luggage with them as well, so he starts designing that while Rolph is talking about the visit, and about how much better his parents place is than Tovey's, since it is a bit more central to the capitol.
- A few days later Sara comes to him and asks if he can help out her mother -- one of the cities she is trading with has had an issue where their sewage system has broken, and spilled into their drinking water.  Tor grumbles about it, thinking it is not that big of a deal since it is a common type of magic (water purification), at least for personal sized fields.  He makes a design that can work for larger systems, packs a bunch of food and goes off to the isolation chambers to build a template and 10 copies.  While he is at it, he builds the luggage plates to follow people around.

Tor never holds the viewpoint (not alone, nothing traumatic). Camera: the same four sections, from outside. (invented; pending the user's review)
- **Location:** The practice yard; Tor and Rolph's room; the east-stair workroom; the undercroft (isolation chambers).
- **Present:** Tor throughout; Kolb (and a first-year raking); Rolph; Sara; Bet Haskins.
- **Reader learns:** The flying belts are out in the world (friends, and three for the school). Tor's own room was the last in the school to be cooled. Tovey expects Tor for the summer; Rolph claims him first; Tor talks himself out of being needed at home. Tor means to fly to the capital and is building luggage plates for it, though he never says so. The Ashford purifier: a jug-sized field on the pipe, ten in a row, instead of one big field in the cistern.

  - **That Tor means to fly:** never said aloud; Rolph sees the trunk drawing and takes it for a present for his mother. The reader sees what it is.
  - **Who runs the Debri house:** kept intact. Sara says "the house" asked; nobody names who wrote the letter (`sara.md`). The outline's "her mother" is honored offstage only.

## Beats

1. **The belts.** After combat, Tor brings Kolb a sack: three flying belts and controls for the school, since it was Kolb's idea. The cord-on-the-wrist rule; Kolb says it'll go in the rules. Tovey has flown one a little and says it isn't the same as "the other way." (canon, user; details invented)
2. **A student again.** Nearly a month of ordinary classes; Rolph counts nineteen days without a trance. (canon, user; the count invented)
3. **The hot day.** Halfway through spring the heat comes overnight. Rolph finds Tor sweating at the desk under his own plate and laughs: the whole school has turned their plates round to cool, and Tor forgot his. Tor turns it, and apologizes to it. (canon, user)
4. **The invitation.** Tovey is going home "for work" and expects Tor to stay; Rolph asked first, and Tor is practically family: "Brothers beat friends." (canon, user)
5. **Not needed at home.** Tor counts his family on his fingers: Tessa is old enough to knead, which frees Tilda too. They'll miss him, but they don't need him. He says yes. (canon, user; names invented)
6. **Trunks.** Rolph mentions trunks; Tor starts drawing luggage that follows its owner while Rolph talks about the house being "right in the middle." Rolph thinks it's a gift. (canon, user)
7. **Ashford.** Three days later Sara brings a letter under the Debri seal: sewage in a river town's cistern, flux, deaths. Tor grumbles that a purifier is a second-year sigil, then sees the answer: a small field on the pipe, ten in a row. "Children." Then he's angry: you don't put a sewer by the water; the purifier is "a bucket under a leak," and he isn't sure it will last more than **10 or 20 years**, so the town must dig up and move the sewer. He makes Sara promise to put it in the letter "in those words," then apologizes for nearly shouting. (canon, user; the design and wording invented; the anger and the 10–20 years, user, October 23, 2026)
8. **He packs.** For once Tor packs his own food for a trance. "Tell her I packed *properly*." (canon, user; Trice's line invented)
9. **The undercroft.** Two days later he comes out with the template and ten purifier sheets, and ten luggage plates following him up the stair like ducklings. Bet Haskins will have something to say at breakfast. (canon, user; details invented)
