# Scene 1

- **Summary:**
- Tor goes back to being a student for a while.  He distributes flying devices to his friends, and gives a few to Kolb, since he suggested it.
- Half-way through spring, its a hot day, Tor is sweating in his room working on coming up with a new design, and Rolph comes in, laughing at him because he forgot to use the heating plate to cool the room.  Rolph comments that the rest of the school is very happy in their cool rooms but Tor is sweating away after having made the cooling device.
- Rolph also suggests that they visit the capital over summer break.  Tovey has to go back 'for work', and is expecting Tor to come to stay with him.  Rolph suggests that he has to stay with his family, because Tor is basically family since he and Tor are best friends.  Tor decides that he is not needed at Two Bends since he has plenty of brothers and sisters to help out in the bakery, and his youngest sister is now old enough to help out with the baking, freeing up two people (her to help, and the one who was watching her before).
- Tor agrees, thinking that of course they will fly, but he doesn't mention this to anyone.  He immediately realizes that he needs to have extra new devices to fly their luggage with them as well, so he starts designing that while Rolph is talking about the visit, and about how much better his parents place is than Tovey's, since it is a bit more central to the capitol.
- A few days later Sara comes to him and asks if he can help out her mother -- one of the cities she is trading with has had an issue where their sewage system has broken, and spilled into their drinking water.  Tor grumbles about it, thinking it is not that big of a deal since it is a common type of magic (water purification), at least for personal sized fields.  He makes a design that can work for larger systems, packs a bunch of food and goes off to the isolation chambers to build a template and 10 copies.  While he is at it, he builds the luggage plates to follow people around.
