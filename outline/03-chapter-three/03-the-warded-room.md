# The warded room

- **Summary:** Campbell takes Tor to the basement of the administration building, a corridor that seems empty and won't hold his attention. Tor drops into a deep trance to focus and sees a door; Campbell is surprised but takes him to it and opens it with an access token. Inside, a roaring fire doesn't warm a freezing room, and one side won't be seen. Deeper in trance, Tor sees who's sitting there: Trice, the headmaster, and Wensa, Rolph's accounting instructor.
- **POV / mode:** Rotating third: Campbell (user, October 17, 2026), whose surprise makes the trance visible from outside. Camera: the narration itself skids off what the wards hide (see staging notes).
- **Location:** The basement of the administration building: a warded corridor, and a warded room. See `codex/locations/the-administration-basement.md`.
- **Present:** Tor, Campbell; then Trice, the headmaster, Wensa.
- **Reader learns:** The school has warded spaces that hide doors and people from attention; access tokens; Tor's deep trance sees through wards, which surprises adults; in trance he's less reactive and less subservient.
- **Hidden (and how):** Why the room is warded, and what Wensa really is (she's an accounting instructor until scene four); what it means that Tor can see through the wards (Campbell's surprise and Wensa's look are all the reader gets).

## Beats

All beats canon (user, October 17, 2026) unless labeled.

1. **The basement.** Campbell drags Tor down to the basement of the administration building. It's **very cold**. It feels like an **empty corridor**, but wrong: Tor can't focus.
2. **Trance.** Tor goes into a **deep trance** to steady his mind. In it he can **see doors**. He's **less reactive** than before, and **less subservient**.
3. **The door.** When Tor focuses on a door, **Campbell is surprised**, but takes him to it anyway.
4. **The token.** Campbell presses an **access token** to a **pad** by the door. For a moment the door is no longer impossible to focus on; Campbell opens it and they go in.
5. **The cold room.** A **roaring fire** in the fireplace, and the room still **bone-chillingly cold**. **One side of the room is impossible to focus on.**
6. **Deeper.** Tor drops further into trance and sees who's there: **Trice**, **the headmaster**, and, oddly, **Rolph's accounting instructor**, Wensa.
7. **Suspicion, both ways.** Wensa sees Tor pierce the wards on the room and on the **chairs**, and reads him as an unknown and dangerous person. Tor finds her suspicious too: she's behaving very oddly for an accounting instructor.

**Staging notes** (invented):

- **The wards act on attention, not sight.** The corridor isn't dark; it's just impossible to keep in mind. Show it as sliding: Tor's eyes keep landing somewhere else; he starts a sentence about the walls and finishes it about the floor. In camera mode, the narration can do it too: the description of the corridor goes vague and repetitive ("a wall, and more wall") until Tor fixes on the door, and then the door is suddenly vivid. The same trick for the room's far side: the narrator describes the fire, the cold, the near chairs, and slides past the rest, until Tor names who's sitting there.
- **The trance is the reverse of chapter one's.** There it was timeless, inward and days long. Here it's minutes, chosen, and pointed outward: a tool he picks up because he's frightened.
- **Less subservient is a big signal; spend it carefully.** In trance Tor stops apologizing. He looks straight at people, speaks plainly, maybe doesn't add "sir." It's the first hint that his meekness can be switched off (`tor.md`, the mental controls; the reading is ours, assumed). Nobody comments. The reader should feel the change without being told it matters.
- **Campbell's surprise is the measure.** A dean who has walked this corridor a hundred times watches a student look straight at a door nobody should find. One beat: he stops, looks at Tor, looks at the door, decides it's above his pay, and gets out his token.
- **The token and pad** are new objects; describe them concretely. Invented suggestion: a disc on a cord, pressed to a plate set in the stone, and a sound like a held breath let out.
- **The cold room is description work.** Fire roaring, logs shifting, heat on the face with no warmth in it, breath showing. The cold may be part of the wards; don't explain it.
- **The reveal of the three is an odd lineup**, and Tor reacts to the oddest: why is the accounting instructor here? Trice, swollen-faced (see `04-the-hearing.md`), may wave.
- **The chair wards drop.** Once Tor has seen through them, the headmaster switches them off ("There's no point now, is there?"), so Tor can come out of trance and still see the room. The contrast is the point: in trance he's calm and plain; out of it he's apologizing within a breath. (invented, October 17, 2026)
- **Wensa's suspicion is physical.** She's seen a stranger walk through wards built to stop strangers, and her job is Rolph's safety. She shifts in her chair, a hand going to her belt. The reader doesn't yet know what's there.
