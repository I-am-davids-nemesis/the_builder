## Scenes

After tor hits to tovey and gets his attention , he tries to follow his training and run away. He’s too clumsy and slow under the effects of Toveys battle rage. Tovey gets a good hit on him that sends him flying. When he lands he is only bruised, but he is far enough away that he can get up and run. Tovey is loud on his heels but slowly starts to fall behind. After a couple of minutes the sound of running behind him stops and he risks a look back where he sees Tovey start to levitate off the ground and start flying forward. He redoubles his running and tries to find a local forest to hide in where the flying doesn’t give Tovey the advantage. At dawn Tovey seems to come to himself and take off towards school. 

At this point the problem becomes clear: striking a noble is a high crime and can be punished by death. Tor understands this and is terrified— but also determined to prevent Trice from suffering that fate. He also discovers that he is still carrying the bag of drying fields. He would have wanted to drop them earlier— anything to help him get away from the rampaging noble.  He determines to go back to the dorm and make sure Rolph has the drying plates for his mother and then turn himself in and try to take the blame so that Trice is not blamed. 
When he gets back to his room he finds the dean of students in his room with a sleepy looking Rolph. The dean is wearing all black and looking pretty grumpy. (Last name Campbell). Tor tells Rolph that his mother’s order is in the bag and asks him to sell the rest and give the money to his family (because he thinks he is going to die). Campbell drags him away before Rolph can respond. 

The dean drags Tor to the basement of the administration building.  It is very cold and Tor feels that he is in an empty hallway, but it also feels very wrong to him, like he can’t focus.  He goes into a deep trance state so that he can focus his mind, and in this state he can see doors — but he is not quite as reactive as before — and he is not quite as subservient.  When he focuses on the door, the dean is surprised, but drags him over anyway. He presses an access token to a pad by the door, and the door briefly is no longer impossible to focus on, and he is able to open it and go inside.

Inside is also weird — there is a roaring fire in the fireplace, but the room is still bone-chilling cold.  In addition, one side of the room is impossible to focus on.  As Tor drops into a deeper trance to focus, he sees Trice, the headmaster, and oddly, Rolph's accounting instructor.
