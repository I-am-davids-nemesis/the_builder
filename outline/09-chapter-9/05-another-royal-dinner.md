# Another Royal Dinner

- **When:** the tenth day, from just after lunch with Connie (a little past the noon bell, scene 04) to dinner at the ninth bell of the night (user, November 7, 2026). Tor has all afternoon and evening for the cold box, the kitchen test and dressing.
- **Summary:** Back in his room, Tor finds the ice box Connie's two fingers ordered and builds ten cold plates for it; the test ices a pitcher solid. Burks takes one to the palace kitchen while Tor starts on the flying cart, then dresses him for dinner, slitting and lacing the trousers over the splint, and finds a box for the Morgans' present and a walking stick. The "family" dinner turns out to be about a hundred guests and a herald. The herald doesn't know Tor and announces him as plain "Tor", and the room falls silent. Tor queues, greets the king and queen properly, hands the present to the king for the Morgans, and is sent toward Alphonse, who is walled in by large people. He ends up beside Tovey and his date, Collette, steps back, bumps into Trice, and she walks away without seeing him, plainly hurt. Facts from the user's notes; canon unless marked.
  1. **The ice box.** Tor goes back to his room and finds a nice large ice box in it, with a pitcher of cool water in the middle.
  2. **The field.** He sits down and works on a good field to keep it cold. He's a bit disappointed not to have a box beside it to pour all the heat into, for an oven (the paired idea from scene 04), so he just dumps all the heat into the ground.
  3. **Ten.** He makes a template and a batch of ten in a couple of hours, puts a cold plate in the box and turns it on.
  4. **Nothing.** He doesn't feel anything and wonders if he failed. It happens (not to him, of course, but he's definitely heard other students complain about it).
  5. **The amulet.** Then he realizes he put his temperature equalization amulet back on and isn't feeling any temperature at all. He grabs the pitcher, and the water, super-cooled, ices up the second it's disturbed.
  6. **"I wish Burks was here."** Now he just needs to test it, and says so out loud. As if by magic, Burks walks out of the wall from behind a cleverly hidden panel and asks what he needs.
  7. **The kitchen test.** Tor needs to test the cooling box somewhere busier and more useful than his room. Could Burks take one of the cooling plates to the kitchen and have it tested there? Burks agrees and takes off.
  8. **The flying cart.** Burks is gone a rather long time, but Tor doesn't mind; he starts on the flying cart problem, looking for a bigger field that flies in a way less like a flying rig (the Debri house's transports, `outline/06-chapter-6/03-luncheon.md`).
  9. **Installed.** Burks eventually comes back: the device is installed in the kitchen. It took a while because they decided to screw it to the inside of one of their bigger ice boxes.
  10. **Nothing to wear.** Tor suddenly realizes it's getting close to dinner and he has no clothes to wear. Burks tells him not to worry and goes out for some.
  11. **The trousers.** Burks comes back and tries to dress him, but the trousers won't go over the splint. He expertly cuts the leg from hem to nearly hip, they get the trousers on, and he laces up the side with thin silk ribbons so convincingly that it looks stylish rather than a slipshod repair.
  12. **The present.** Tor remembers he promised a shield and flying rig for the Duke and Duchess Morgan (scene 04). Has Burks anything to carry it in? Burks goes out and is back in a couple of minutes with a finely carved wooden box; Tor finds the workmanship superb. He puts the shields and flying rigs in and ties it up in beautiful purple silk and ribbon.
  13. **The walking stick.** Burks also finds him a nice walking stick to help him down the hall. Tor heads out to dinner with some vague directions from Burks and an initial helping hand from the door guard.
  14. **Follow the crowd.** In a busier hall, the guard points him to just follow the crowd.
  15. **No small family dinner.** Close to the dining room, Tor realizes it's a large event with maybe a hundred guests. A man with a deep, loud baritone announces people as they enter; they walk a few feet into the room, pause to be seen, and go on.
  16. **The couple ahead.** The couple in front of Tor turn out to be another duke and duchess, from a different part of the kingdom. People glance over, but they apparently don't rate much attention.
  17. **The herald is stumped.** When it's Tor's turn, the announcer doesn't recognize him and is stumped until a guard steps over and whispers to him.
  18. **"Tor."** He announces him simply as "Tor". Tor walks (limps? settled: a careful walk, and the limp starts in the line) in the requisite few steps and pauses, awkward, walking stick in one hand and the present under the other arm. He suddenly realizes that *everyone* is looking at him; the room falls silent for a moment before conversation starts up again.
  19. **The line.** People are walking up to a line to pay their respects to the king and queen, seated at a welcoming table. Waiting, Tor watches closely how people greet the royals.
  20. **Checking the amulets.** He surreptitiously checks that he has his full complement of amulets (shield and temperature equalization), since the last time he dined with royalty (the Ward dinner, chapter 7) things did not turn out well. His leg is really starting to hurt, and holding the box is becoming a real pain.
  21. **The greeting.** At the front of the line he greets the king and queen most properly, then asks the king to hand his present on to the Duke and Duchess Morgan, since his leg is bothering him so much.
  22. **The suspicious guard.** Somewhat surprised, the king agrees and at once hands the present to a guard, who looks at Tor suspiciously for handing the king a package directly.
  23. **Alphonse.** The king points out his son, Alphonse, so that Tor has someone to talk to and can hopefully meet more people; that's what these dinners are for.
  24. **The wall of people.** Tor slowly makes his way over, but his friend is surrounded by very large people, and with his injury he can't get close.
  25. **Collette.** Luckily he sees Tovey close by and stands near him, hoping to be noticed. Tovey's date sees him first and introduces herself as "Baronetta Collette Coltress." She wouldn't normally be invited to such a thing and is very excited to be standing so close to the heir to the kingdom. Tor agrees that Alphonse is pretty great and asks her to get Tovey's attention, so they can barge in on Alphonse and chat.
  26. **Stepping back.** Tor says hi to Tovey, but has to step back so as not to stand too close to his date, and bumps into someone.
  27. **Trice.** He turns round, sees Trice and greets her at once. She doesn't seem to see him; she simply apologizes and walks away, but Tor can see she's extremely sad and hurt. Cliffhanger: the scene ends as Trice walks away.

## Beats

1. **The ice box** (Tor). Back in his room, a large ice box has appeared, with a pitcher of cool water in the middle. He hasn't been asked twice; Connie's two fingers did it.
2. **Heat into the ground.** He settles to the field. No box beside it to take the heat for an oven, a small disappointment, so the heat goes into the ground. A template, then ten, in a couple of hours.
3. **Nothing.** The plate goes in; he feels nothing. Failures happen, to other students. He mutters about it.
4. **The amulet.** He finds the cord at his neck. He lifts the pitcher, and the water turns to ice in his hands.
5. **"I wish Burks was here."** Said aloud, to nobody. A panel in the wall swings out and Burks steps through, asking what he needs.
6. **The errand.** A busier test than his room: would Burks take a plate to the kitchen? Burks goes.
