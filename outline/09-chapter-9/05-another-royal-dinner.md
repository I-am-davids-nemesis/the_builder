# Another Royal Dinner

- **Summary:**
- Tor goes back to his room, to discover a nice large ice box in it, with a pitcher of cool water in the middle.  He sits down and focuses on making a good field for it to stay cold; he is a bit disappointed that he doesn't have a box beside it to pour all the heat into for an oven, so he just dumps all the heat into the ground.  He makes a template and a batch of 10 in a couple of hours.  He puts a cold plate in the box, and turns it on.
- He doesn't feel anything, so he wonders if he failed; it happens (not to him of course, but he has definitely heard other students complain about it).  Then he realizes that he put his temperature equalization amulet back on and isn't feeling any temperature.  he grabs the pitcher of water, and since it was super-cooled, it immediately ices up the second it is disturbed.
- now he just needs to test it.  "I wish Burks was here", he says out loud.  And like magic, Burks walks out of the wall from behind a cleverly hidden panel.  Asking what he needs.  Tor explains that he needs a test of the cooling box in a place that is more trafficked and useful than his room. He asks if Burks can take one of the cooling plates to the kitchen and get it tested there.  Burks agrees, and takes off.  He is gone a rather long time, but Tor doesn't mind, he just starts working on the flying cart problem.  Looking for a bigger field that flies in a way that is not so much like a flying rig
- Burks eventually comes back and tells him that he got the device installed in the kitchen -- it took a bit because they decided to screw it into the inside of one of the bigger ice boxes they have.
- Tor suddenly realizes that it is getting close to dinner and he doesn't have any clothes to wear.  Burks tells him not to worry and he goes out to grab some clothes.  he comes back and tries to get Tor dressed, but they find the pants won't go over the split; So Burks expertly cuts the leg of the pants from hem to nearly hip, and they are able to get the pants on -- then he uses some thin silk ribbons to lace up the side of the leg in such a convincing way that it looks stylish rather than like a slipshod repair.
- Then Tor remembers he promised to bring a shield  and flying rig for the Duke and Duchess Morgan; he asks Burks if he has anything to carry the present in -- Burks goes out and comes back in a couple of minutes with a finely carved wooden box; Tor finds is to be of superb workmanship.  he puts the shields and flying rigs in, and gets some beautiful purple silk and ribbon to tie it up in.
- Burks also finds him a nice walking stick to use to help him walk down the hall, and he has to head out to dinner with some vague directions from Burks, and an initial helping hand from the door guard.
- Once he gets to a more trafficked hall, the guard points him to just follow the crowd.  As he gets close to the dining room he realizes that this is no small family dinner but a large event with maybe a hundred guests.  As people enter the room a man with a deep, loud, baritone voice announces them -- they walk a few feet into the room, pause for people to see them, and continue on.  The couple in front of Tor turns out to be another Duke and Duchess of a different part of the kingdom.  People glance over, but apparently they don't rate much attention.
- When it is Tor's turn, the man announcing people doesn't recognize him. stumped and unsure until a guard steps over and whispers something to him.
- The man then simply announces him as "Tor".  Tor walks (limps?) in the requisite few steps to stop and pause, awkwardly with his walking stick in one hand and the present tucked under his other arm.  He suddenly realizes that *everyone* is looking at him, and the room fell silent for a moment before conversation started back up.
- Tor realizes that people are basically walking up to a line to pay their respects to the King and Queen sitting at a welcoming table.  As he waits in the new line, paying close attention to how people are greeting the royalty, he surreptitiously double checks that he has his full complement of amulets (both shield and temperature equalization) -- since the last time he was at a dinner with royalty things did not turn out so well.  He also realizes that his leg is really starting to hurt and holding the box is really starting to be a pain.
- He eventually gets to the front of the line where he greets the King and Queen most properly.  Then he requests the king to hand off his present to the Duke and Duchess Morgan since his leg is bothering him so much;
- though somewhat surprised, the king agrees and immediately hands of the present to a guard who looks at Tor suspiciously for handing the king a package directly.
- The king then points his son, Alphonse, out to Tor so that he can have someone to go talk to and hopefully meet more people -- that's what these dinners are all about.
- Tor slowly makes his way over to Alphonse, but as he gets closer he finds that his friend is surrounded by very large people and he can't really get close, with his injury.
- luckily is sees Tovey close by and goes stands near him hoping to be noticed.  Tovey's date, sees him first, and introduces herself as “Baronetta Collette Coltress.”  She apparently wouldn't normally be invited to such a thing, and is pretty excited that she is standing so close to the heir to the kingdom.  Tor agrees that Alphonse is pretty great, and asks Collette to get Tovey's attention so that they can barge in on Alphonse and chat. Tor says hi to Tovey, but has to step back so as not to be too close to his date, and bumps into someone
- He turns around, and sees Trice, who he immediately greets. Trice however, doesn't seem to see him, simply apologizing and walking away -- but Tor can see that Trice is extremely sad and hurt -- cliffhanger ending as Trice walks away
