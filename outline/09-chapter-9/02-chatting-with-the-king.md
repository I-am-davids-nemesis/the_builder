# Chatting With the King

- **Summary:**
- The King comes to visit Tor. Rolph comes with him, and an older man, who is wearing cream and yellow robes -- he is mostly bald with a short ring of white hair around the back half of his head.  Karina, the princess comes in too, with a smaller girl that has to be a sister given how much she looks like the queen, who walks in beside her.
- Tor greets everyone enthusiastically, but only semi-formally. He immediately asks how everyone from the tanner's yard is doing, specifically the two kids.
- the king replies: Both kids are doing well. The boy was actually trying to rescue the girl who had fell in -- one of his sisters friends.  The king tells Tor that people are very impressed.
- Tor misreads the kings statement to imply that people are impressed with the boy trying to save the girl, not Tor saving both of them -- of course an adult would try to save a child, but children are not known for their bravery, normally.
- Rolph laughs at the interplay and asks what the birds are for -- they are not distributed about in a way that is very attractive for room decoration.
- **Where the falcons came from** (user, November 7, 2026): the king or Rolph mentions it here: they stood on the old gate before the wall was raised. (Scene 1 never says; the king sent them without a word.)
- Tor laughs, and says the he needs something to hold the new river. The one for County Ford.  One needs to go at the coast, one needs to go at the head of the river, to raise the water level, and one needs to go in-between to keep the field going.
- The queen is quite put out by the idea -- Tor should not be working, he should be resting.  Tor doesn't want to go against the queen, but he is very concerned about the county and the problems the drought will cause.
- he also thinks that he has to rest anyway, and being deep in the meditation state required to get the field on the birds will be better than drugs for distracting himself from the pain.
- The queen relents after making him promise not to stress himself.
- The main in the cream/yellow robes bows to him, and the queen then notices him and introduces him to Tor: Smythe of Westland. He apparently is the head of the military for the kingdom, like the joint-chief-of-staff.  Second only to the king.  Smythe states that he had a few things he wanted to discuss with Tor, and asked if he could speak with him privately later, when he wasn't busy saving a county from drought.  Tor, of course, agrees.  He does hope he isn't being drafted because his battle cry "run away" would probably not go over well in the military.
- Karina then barges up and says how brave Tor was going into the "hole" (since she is too polite to name the place he went down).  She then asks if Tor really went into a combat rage, like everyone is saying.  Everyone else winces as this is socially very poor manners to talk about such a thing; and her sister basically hits her for being so rude, saying "you can't ask people that..."
- Tor is very confused. he thinks the story is already growing beyond what happened -- he claims that he just did a bit of magic at the end.
- Rolph contradicts him -- he could see that he had a shield going, even without his amulet, and he could feel the battle rage, which is why he called for everyone to run, and he saw the direct effect of throwing all the dirt into the sky, pulling out the girl.
- Tor admits to being a bit upset at the dirt, so maybe, but combat rage is a noble trait, not a trait that a commoner like himself would have.
- The king laughs, and tells him not to worry about it -- if he needs anything let him or his staff know.  He then leaves with his military advisor.
- The queen and the kids stay around.  The girls start teasing and flirting with Tor, while Rolph/Alphonse gets super embarrassed. Connie laughs and tells him that is what he gets for bringing someone home with him.  After a bit more flirting and teasing they all leave him alone.

## Beats

1. **The visit** (Rolph). The door opens on a crowd: the king, Rolph, Smythe in cream and yellow, the queen, Karina, Varley. Tor, in bed, greets them all warmly and only half properly.
2. **The children.** Tor's first question is about the tanners' yard: the two kids. The king: both well; the boy went in after the girl, his sister's friend, who'd fallen in. People are very impressed.
3. **The misreading.** Tor agrees: of course they are; a grown man would go in after a child, but a boy that age doing it is brave. Rolph laughs.
4. **The falcons.** Rolph asks what the birds are for, standing about the floor like that. The king says, dryly, where they came from: the old gate, before the wall was raised. That's all he says about having sent them.
5. **The river.** Tor laughs: they're to hold the new river for Ford. Coast, head of the river to raise the water, one between to keep the field going.
6. **The queen.** He should be resting. Tor won't cross her, but Ford is drying out; he has to rest anyway, and the trance is better than any draught for the pain. She relents once he promises not to strain himself.
7. **Smythe.** The man in cream and yellow bows; the queen notices and introduces him: Smythe of Westland. He'd like a word privately, later, when Tor isn't busy saving a county. Tor agrees, and privately hopes he isn't being drafted: "run away" would make a poor battle cry.
8. **Karina.** She barges up: how brave he was, going into the "hole" (too polite to name the midden). Is it true he went into a combat rage, like everyone says? The room winces; Varley swats her: "You can't *ask* people that."
9. **"A bit of magic."** Tor, bewildered: the story is already growing; he only did a bit of magic at the end.
10. **Rolph contradicts him.** The shield, without his amulet; the rage he felt, which is why he shouted for everyone to run; the dirt in the sky and the girl coming out of it.
11. **"A noble trait."** Tor allows he was a bit upset at the dirt, so maybe; but combat rage is for nobles, not commoners like him. The king laughs: don't worry about it; anything he needs, ask. He leaves with Smythe.
12. **The teasing.** The queen and the three stay. Karina and Varley tease and flirt with Tor; Rolph goes scarlet ("Alphie"). Connie laughs: that's what he gets for bringing someone home. A little more, and they leave Tor alone with the falcons.
