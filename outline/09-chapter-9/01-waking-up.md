# Waking Up

- **Summary:**
- Tor wakes up in a room that is even fancier than the one in his guest house, where the bed is even larger -- or at least it seems that way to him
- he realizes that his leg is in a split -- strips of metal down the side, and bandaged tight, with enough padding to keep it comfortable (well, as comfortable as it can be -- the leg is a constant throbbing pain).  He also notices that he is dressed in light loose linen.  The room is cool and he sees a cooling plate on the wall.
- he decides he is thirsty and needs to use the bathroom -- he sees neither water nor a restroom in his current room, so he has to figure out how to hobble over to the door
- When he opens it up he sees two royal guards standing outside -- and for a minute he can't tell if he is a prisoner.  the guards are startled to see him; one of them starts to say "Coun---" cuts himself off and says "Master Baker"; they are there to prevent anyone from disturbing him. Tor asks for water and a restroom; the guards are happy to help (so tor realizes he is not a prisoner).
- Tor asks if he can see King Richard, about the drought.  The guard says the request will be relayed, but he can't promise that it will happen.
- Tor waits for a bit, then asks the guards to get his working materials from his room -- it takes a while as they have to ask someone else to get it as they can't leave their station.
- The door is left open as he is waiting, and he starts talking about his project to fix the drought in County Ford.  He says he needs something big that isn't going to be easy to move around, that people won't mess with.  he does say that the king asked him to get this done. the guards don't respond, but a couple hours later, three large bronze falcons are brought into the room, amusingly, using Tor's own lifting plates -- it looks like each bronze falcon requires three plates under it to keep it off the ground, and even then, they are only a couple of inches off the ground.  Tor is surprised they could even get the things into the room.
- A royal guard named Finely comes into the room with them, and asks if they are going to be appropriate.  Tor is quite impressed, and claims they will be great if the king allows the project.  This will be the most interesting thing he has ever put a field on, and their size and weight will make anyone think twice before they think about stealing it -- of course, you would have to be pretty daft to steal anything with a giant sky river attached to it.
