# Waking Up

- **Summary:** Tor wakes in the palace after the rescue at the midden (chapter 8, scene 3), hurt, and starts work on the Ford water line from bed. Facts from the user's notes; canon unless marked.
  1. **The room.** Tor wakes in a room even fancier than his rooms in the guest house, in a bed that is even larger, or seems so to him.
  2. **The leg.** His left leg is in a splint: metal strips down the side, bandaged tight, with enough padding to keep it as comfortable as it can be. It throbs constantly. Tor doesn't really focus on it, and nobody volunteers how bad it is; the reader finds out later how bad it is and how it heals (user, November 6, 2026). He's dressed in light, loose linen. The room is cool; there's a cooling plate on the wall.
  3. **Thirsty.** He needs water and a privy and sees neither, so he has to work out how to hobble to the door.
  4. **The guards.** Two royal guards stand outside. For a moment he can't tell whether he's a prisoner. They're startled to see him up; one starts "Coun—", cuts himself off, and says "Master Tor." (He knows Count Lairdgren by sight, and Tor looks very much like him: Tor is the count's clone, a series secret. Tor doesn't notice, and the reader is told nothing; user, November 6, 2026; `codex/characters/supporting/green.md`.) They're there so nobody disturbs him. Tor asks for water and a privy, and they're glad to help, so he isn't a prisoner.
  5. **The king.** Tor asks whether he can see King Richard, about the drought. The guard will pass the request on but can't promise anything.
  6. **His materials.** After a while Tor asks for his working materials from his room. It takes a while: the guards can't leave their post and have to send someone.
  7. **Talking at the open door.** The door stays open while he waits, and he talks (to the guards, or to himself; they don't answer) about the project for Ford County: he needs something big that won't be easy to move and that people won't meddle with. He mentions that the king asked him to get this done.
  8. **The falcons.** A couple of hours later, three large bronze falcons are brought in on Tor's own lifting plates: three plates under each, and even then they ride only a couple of inches off the floor. Tor is surprised they got them through the door.
  9. **Finely.** A royal guard sergeant named Finely (senior to the door pair; he recurs as Tor's minder in the palace; user, November 6, 2026; `codex/characters/supporting/finely.md`) comes in with them and asks whether they'll do. Tor is very impressed: they'll be perfect if the king allows the project. They're the most interesting thing he's ever put a field on, and their size and weight will make anyone think twice about stealing one; though you'd have to be pretty daft to steal anything with a giant sky river attached to it.

## Beats

1. **Waking** (Tor, alone). Ceiling first: too high, painted. The bed goes on and on. He tries to sit up and the leg answers.
2. **The splint.** Metal strips, tight bandage, padding; the throb with every heartbeat. He looks at it once and moves on. Linen he doesn't own. Cool air, and a cooling plate on the wall; he checks it's one of his.
3. **Water.** Dry mouth, a full bladder, no jug, no privy door. He mutters himself through it ("Think, Tor") and makes the trip to the door by furniture, chair to bedpost to wall.
4. **The door** (the guard). It opens on the two of them. The patient is up, gray-faced, holding the frame. "Coun— Master Tor." Their orders: nobody disturbs him.
5. **Prisoner?** Tor is very polite, which the guard reads as nerves. Water, please, and a privy. Both arrive at once, and Tor visibly relaxes.
6. **The king.** Could he see King Richard? It's about the drought. The guard says it'll go up; no promises.
7. **His things.** Later: could someone fetch his working materials? The guards can't leave the door, so they send a page, and it takes time.
8. **Thinking aloud.** The door left open; Tor in a chair by it with the leg up, talking: something big, heavy, that people won't touch; the king asked him to do it. The guards say nothing. The guard notices the other one is listening too.
9. **The falcons.** Two hours on: three bronze falcons, each floating a couple of inches up on three of Tor's own lifting plates, steered through the door with a hand's breadth to spare.
10. **Finely.** "Will these be appropriate?" Tor is already on his good knee beside one, hand on the bronze. The best thing he's ever put a field on, if the king allows it, and nobody will steal it; though you'd have to be daft to steal anything with a river attached. The guard decides he likes him.
