# Building a River

- **Summary:**
- it takes tor 6 days to build the field on the bronze falcons.  it is perhaps the strongest field he has ever made.
- over the 6 days, the palace staff had to come in to feed him and lead him to the restroom.
- each day he gets more and more withdrawn, into the fields he is pushing on to the birds.  He forgets the pain by the end of the second day. The third day he doesn't even see anyone around him. the last two days are just locking the fields into place, where the only thing in his mind is the fields
- afterwards he just wants to sleep and eat, and sleep, and eat.  He finally is ready to do something at the end of the seventh day, when he sends the falcons out to do their job -- very clear instructions about where to put each one. The first has to go far up the river so it gets plenty of water and everyone downstream can siphon off the water for their irrigation canals.  the second has to go half way to the ocean to make sure that the water stays the course and keeps in the air.  The third needs to be as close to the ocean as it can be without being washed away.
- A group of royal guards come in to take it out, and accompany the riggers out to each location as a sign of how important the job is.
- Tor also gave them instructions on how to start the river -- just like the clothes dryer or fruit dryer, it just has to be tapped, but people don't seem to understand quite what he means that tapping it anywhere would work, so he tells them to be sure to tap the bird on the center of the shield it is holding.
- It took more than a day, even using the luggage floats to move the birds around, but after they were in place, by the tenths day, there was a large stream of perfectly pure water flowing into the river, making its way downstream to all of the farms.
- while it added quite a bit of water, it didn't cause any flooding, and Tor was quite thankful about that -- it was one of his chief worries that he would add too much water and cause more problems than he solved.  But the drought had already reduced the river enough that his addition just brought it up to a bit over normal.
- If the weather turned, and a it started raining, they could get the guards at the beach to turn it off -- but Tor hoped they wouldn't have to do that much
