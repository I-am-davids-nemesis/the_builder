# Dinner With the Queen

Split from scene 03 (user, November 7, 2026).

- **Summary:** On the tenth evening, with the river running, Burks helps Tor bathe, and Tor sees his ankle for the first time. At dinner Connie wants the river called the River Tor, and Tor won't have it. Connie's sister Mercy arrives, Trice's mother, worried about her orchard crop. Tor, introduced only as Torrance Baker, offers her food dryers and flying rigs in return for half of what would have spoiled, for Ford. Facts from the user's notes; canon unless marked.
  1. **The bath.** Once it's all done, Tor badly needs a bath and can't manage it alone. Luckily Burks is there. He helps Tor take off the splint and bandages, and Tor sees the bruising and the horrible rope burn looped round his ankle. Burks is very kind: he helps him get clean and dresses him in very loose silk trousers and a tunic.
  2. **Family style.** Tor has dinner in one of the smaller dining rooms. Connie comes to eat with him "family style," sitting close enough to talk.
  3. **The River Tor.** Connie mentions the new river: it's new, so it needs a name. She calls it the River Tor.
  4. **The Falcon River.** Tor definitely doesn't want it named after him and struggles for another name. He suggests the Falcon River. The King's River is already taken, by the one past the capital. The falcon is the king's symbol, though, and this river is held up by falcons. It would probably go over well with the people who hear about the sky river the king made to end the drought, much better than naming it after some student.
  5. **Connie refuses.** It was Tor who heard of the problem and went straight to fixing it.
  6. **Tor demurs.** Connie was in the room when the king said they'd need a river of water to fix it.
  7. **The crush.** Tor feels awkward and suddenly wonders whether he's developing a crush on the queen. It would be very awkward, even if she does look just like Rolph's older sister.
  8. **Changing the subject.** He switches at once to going out to inspect the river, how he'd get there on a broken leg, and whether he should start on the transports he promised the Debri house (the flying cart, `outline/06-chapter-6/03-luncheon.md`).
  9. **Connie's anger.** She won't let his injury become his excuse to work himself to death.
  10. **The water box.** To head her off, Tor asks for some water. Connie takes a cool pitcher out of an insulated box on the table. Tor chuckles, remembering his idea for paired cooling and heating boxes, the oven and ice-box together.
  11. **"Just to check something."** He asks for a box like that in his room: not for work, exactly, only to check something.
  12. **Two fingers.** The queen is a little intrigued, because she doesn't quite believe him. She does the royal thing: she raises two fingers and says nothing. Tor has seen it before and gathers that now it's simply going to happen.
  13. **Mercy arrives.** A new woman walks into the dining room, and Tor struggles to his feet. She looks familiar, but he can't place her at first. The queen hugs her and gives her air kisses.
  14. **Just in.** The woman says they've just arrived. They wanted to see the new magic river and had to taste the pure water, in case it has healing powers.
  15. **Her oldest.** She asks whether Connie has seen her oldest. The queen thinks they're down in the market looking at fabrics from Afrak, with Alphonse playing footman for her daughter, Karina, Varley and her friend Sara.
  16. **Names, please.** Tor is still half on the ice box, but he realizes there are two beautiful women in front of him and he ought at least to know their names. At a lull he interrupts, awkwardly, hoping for some clarity.
  17. **Introductions.** Connie introduces her sister: "Sitting Baroness, Merciful Thorgood Morgan." Then she introduces him as Torrance Baker, with nothing added. Tor isn't going to claim anything for himself either.
  18. **Listening.** Tor goes on listening to their conversation.
  19. **The orchards.** Mercy is a little circumspect, but she gets to the point. Her barony has a great many orchards, pears and apples, and the crop is very good this year. She's afraid it will all rot on the trees before it can be picked and shipped. She hopes she has some connections with the Debri house, which is apparently busy with something more lucrative and has no time to make food dryers.
  20. **Connie's suggestion.** Connie suggests she ask her son, who has been getting quite close to Sara lately, and perhaps Mercy's own daughter, Patricia, since she and Sara are such good friends.
  21. **"You're Trice's mother!"** Tor suddenly realizes who Mercy is, blurts it out, and says how very glad he is to meet her. Connie teases him lightly: he isn't even that polite to Richard. The women carry on talking.
  22. **The conversation winds** from the apple, peach and pear crops (their superb quality, their worrying future) to the flying river, the flying guards, and how Mercy's husband, Duke Eric, is very keen to try flying himself.
  23. **Tor's offer.** Tor butts in: he has a couple of spare flying rigs and shields, so Eric could try it; or he could just ask his daughter for one.
  24. **Mercy is shocked.** He'd part with one of the fabulously expensive Tor flying rigs, even as a loan? She plainly doesn't connect Torrance with Tor, or either with the young-looking boy at the table. She's also surprised that Patricia can already fly.
  25. **Presents.** Tor waves her worry away, while Connie hides her laughter: he brought extras as presents. He jokes that the councilors won't let Rich fly, and Connie is afraid of heights, so she won't even try. Trice, on the other hand, is already a very accomplished flyer.
  26. **Teachers.** Rolph (oops, Alphonse) could teach her, since he's very good, or Tovey (Count Thompson, he means), who is already one of the best flyers. Though Tovey might be busy running his county, instead of being lazy like Tor.
  27. **The bold suggestion.** Tor could perhaps get her the food dryers, and maybe some flying rigs to deliver the fruit before it spoils, but he wants half of what would have spoiled. It isn't for himself, he insists: Ford County is already starting to suffer from the drought, and food would go a long way toward sparing its people.
  28. **"Could you really?"** Mercy, perhaps not believing this child could do all that, does a good impression of flirting and asks whether he really could get it all done.
  29. **The queen.** Connie switches to regal queen mode: if Tor says he can do something, at least where magic is concerned, then he most certainly can.
  30. **"Oh, *that* Tor?"** Mercy looks at Connie, astonished. "Yes, that Tor."
  31. **The wheat.** Mercy can't necessarily agree on her husband's behalf, but if he can manage all that, then perhaps he could also get a fraction of the wheat crop (an eighth? a tenth?). Wheat keeps better.
  32. **Not tonight.** Tor almost wants to start right away. He decides it would go much faster if he first got the template from the Debri house (the food dryer's, shipped there in chapter 4), and besides, he wants to work on his cold-box idea.
  33. **Excused.** He's excused, and the queen tells him he has to come to dinner at nine.

## Beats

1. **Out of the splint** (Tor). Burks comes in as if he'd been sent for. He undoes the bandages and takes the iron strips away; Tor sees the ankle for the first time: bruising, and the rope burn looped round it like a second strap. He looks once, and keeps looking.
2. **The bath.** Burks gets him into the water and clean, kindly and without fuss, and dresses him in very loose silk trousers and a tunic. The splint goes back on (invented: Burks binds it as neatly as Dr. Marsh did).
3. **Family style** (Connie). The smaller dining room; Tor at the table with the leg on a stool. Connie sits beside him, not across, close enough to talk.
4. **The River Tor.** She names it. He protests, struggles, offers the Falcon River (the king's bird; the King's River taken; better than some student). She refuses: he heard of the problem and fixed it. He: she was there when the king said they'd need a river.
5. **The crush, from outside.** Tor goes red and stops halfway through a sentence; Connie, watching, takes it for tiredness.
6. **The leg as an excuse.** He swerves to inspecting the river, getting there on a broken leg, and the Debri transports. Connie is angry: his leg is not his excuse to work himself to death.
7. **Water.** He asks for some; she lifts a cool pitcher from the insulated box. He chuckles. Could he have a box like that in his room? Not for work. Just to check something. She doesn't believe a word, and raises two fingers. He knows what that means.
8. **Mercy.** A woman comes in; Tor struggles up; he almost knows her face. Hugs, air kisses. They're just in; they had to taste the magic river in case it heals. Where's her oldest? At the market among the Afrak fabrics, with Alphonse playing footman for the girls and Sara.
9. **Names.** At a lull, Tor, red, asks. "Sitting Baroness, Merciful Thorgood Morgan." "Torrance Baker." Connie adds nothing, and enjoys it.
10. **The orchards.** Mercy, circumspect, comes round to it: pears and apples, a splendid crop, rotting on the trees; the Debri house too busy for dryers. Connie: ask Alphonse, who's close to Sara lately, or Patricia.
11. **"Trice's mother!"** Tor blurts it and is very glad to meet her. Connie: he's never that polite to Richard.
12. **The winding talk.** The crops; the flying river; the flying guards; Duke Eric longing to fly.
13. **Spare rigs.** Tor offers his spares for Eric, or Trice could lend hers. Mercy is shocked at the loan, and at Trice flying. Tor: they're presents; Rich isn't allowed, Connie won't; Trice is very good; Rolph, Alphonse, could teach, or Tovey, Count Thompson, if he's not busy running a county, "not lazy like me." Connie hides her face.
14. **The bargain.** Dryers and rigs, for half of what would have spoiled, for Ford. Mercy flirts: could he really? Connie, regal: if Tor says so, about magic, he can. "Oh, *that* Tor?" "Yes, that Tor."
15. **The wheat.** Mercy can't speak for Eric, but perhaps a tenth of the wheat as well; it keeps better.
16. **Excused.** Tor wants to start now, then thinks better: the template from the Debri house first, and the cold box. He's excused. "Dinner at nine," says the queen.
