- **Summary:** One day Kolb suggests Tor build a device to make a person fly. Tor insists it isn't possible. Kolb counters: the Austrans do it, and it's their chief advantage over Noram; and didn't Tor just see Tovey flying? Tor admits it must be possible, and drifts off into a fugue working out how.
- **POV / mode:** Proposed rotating third: Kolb (he sets the idea in motion and watches it land). Camera: the practice yard. (invented; to approve)

- **Present:** Tor, Kolb; classmates, perhaps Trice and Sara in black.

## Beats

All beats canon (user, October 18, 2026) unless labeled.

1. **The suggestion.** Kolb suggests Tor come up with a device to make a person fly.
2. **"It isn't possible,"** Tor insists.
3. **Kolb counters:** of course it is. **The Austrans do it**; it's their chief advantage over Noram.
4. **And Tovey.** Didn't Tor just see Tovey flying?
5. **Tor admits it must be possible,** and goes off into a **fugue state**, working out how.

**Staging notes** (invented):

- **Kolb's voice:** short, unimpressed, quietest when he means it most (`kolb.md`). He says the impossible thing as if asking Tor to fetch water.
- **Tor's "impossible" is honest and technical:** a field that pushes things away from you can't push *you* without something to push against. Kolb answers with facts, not theory, and Tor can't argue with the sheep field.
- **The Austrans** tie back to chapter three's "Austran spy." Kolb may say it the way soldiers say the name of an old enemy. Nobody explains how they fly.
- **The fugue lands visibly:** Tor stops mid-sentence, looks at the sky, then at his own feet, and walks off the yard without being dismissed. Kolb lets him. "Nice and loose," perhaps, to nobody (`kolb.md`, chapter two drafts).
- **Kolb's hidden aspect** (training vs. punishment) leans further toward training: he's feeding Tor problems, deliberately.
