# The first flight

- **Summary:** Tor goes out alone at dawn to test the flying device, turns back for his forgotten shield, and in the sheep field fumbles the controls (on, off, on) until a gesture of frustration throws him into the sky. He learns to move slowly, flies to Hollin in a couple of minutes, takes a long time learning to turn, races home, and spends two minutes landing. The chapter ends with him on the grass, alone, and a rumor starting in Hollin.
- **POV / mode:** Rotating third: Tor (he's alone, and it's the one scene where only he can hold it; see `AGENTS.md` §3 on when Tor holds the viewpoint). Camera: follows Tor, from the ground and then the air. (POV approved by the user, October 18, 2026)
- **Location:** The sheep field below the school (the chapter-three chase); the sky over the valley and Hollin; back at school. (canon, user)
- **Present:** Tor, alone.
- **Reader learns:** The flying device works; it answers the hand, and has to be moved slowly; turning and landing are hard; speed takes his breath.
- **Hidden (and how):** That Hollin saw something: a rumor starts in the town that Tor never hears of (user, October 18, 2026).

## Beats

All beats canon (user, October 18, 2026) unless labeled.

1. **Alone, early.** Tor goes out alone, early in the morning, to test his flying device.
2. **The shield.** Halfway there he realizes he doesn't have his shield. He goes back for it: without it, this could be a disaster.
3. **The sheep field.** He goes back out to the sheep field.
4. **On.** He turns the device on. Nothing happens.
5. **On again.** He tries again. Nothing happens, and he realizes he's just turned it **off**. He turns it on again. Nothing happens.
6. **Up.** He throws up his hands in frustration, and **shoots high into the air**.
7. **Down, nearly.** He starts to bring his hand down quickly, and stops himself before he crashes into the ground.
8. **Slowly.** He realizes he has to be very slow, and slows down all of his movements.
9. **Forward.** He slowly pushes his hand forward until he's moving so fast he can hardly breathe.
10. **Hollin.** In a couple of minutes he's over the town, five miles away.
11. **Turning.** He slows down and tries to work out how to turn. It's much harder than he expects; it takes him a long time to turn around.
12. **Home, faster.** When he manages it, he tries to get back to school even faster.
13. **Landing.** Back at school, he realizes landing is going to be a problem. He takes **two minutes** to lower himself slowly to the ground.
14. **Hollin saw something.** A rumor starts in the town. (user, October 18, 2026)

**Staging notes** (invented):

- **The forgotten shield is the first joke and the first lesson:** the test taught him to fear the moment before it's on, and he still nearly leaves it behind. He trots back up the hill, cord in hand, muttering.
- **On, off, on** is a small, perfect comedy of a builder who can make anything and can't tell which way a switch goes. In camera mode the reader can see it before he does.
- **The control answers the hand.** Throwing up his hands sends him up; bringing it down fast nearly puts him into the turf. Show the field from outside (grass flattening in a ring, sheep scattering) and from inside (his stomach left behind).
- **Slow is the rule.** The whole flight is Tor doing everything at a quarter speed, which looks ridiculous from the ground and feels like the most careful thing he's ever done.
- **Too fast to breathe** is the shield again: it passes air, not wind, or the wind takes the air. Another note for a later version. (The engine's next idea may live here.)
- **Hollin from above:** the well where Kolb's runners slap the stone, the chapel, the mill road, the marker stone, the carter's cabbages, all tiny. Five miles in a couple of minutes; Kolb's ten-mile run, done before breakfast.
- **The rumor** (user, October 18, 2026): someone in Hollin (the carter with the cabbages, a boy at the well, the chapel's bell-ringer) sees something in the early sky. By the end of the day it's a story in the town. Tor never hears of it; the reader can enjoy it alone, in a line or two, or in a later chapter.
- **Turning is the hardest thing.** A long, slow, wobbling circle over Hollin, getting it wrong in every direction.
- **Two minutes of landing** is endurance comedy, like Kolb's twenty: inch by inch, the grass coming up, his feet finally on the ground, and Tor lying down in it.
- **The celebration is private** (the old scene 10's proposals moved to scene 8, user, October 18, 2026). Lying in the wet grass among the sheep, Tor laughs out loud, alone: the first time in the book he lets himself be proud, and nobody hears it. It's news, not a boast, even to himself (`tor.md`). Then the sheep, and the bell, and breakfast.

- **Alone is the danger.** Every other test in the chapter had Kolb, Wensa, a crowd. This one has nobody to stop a fire or pull him down.
- **The rig** (user, October 18, 2026): an etched **copper plate worn on a belt** carries the field, like the shield on its cord; the **handheld control** is separate.
- **Early spring** (user, October 18, 2026): dew, mist in the low ground, lambs in the sheep field.
- **The handheld control** should be in his hand from the first line, and the reader should know what happens if he drops it.
- **The sheep field at dawn** would echo chapter three's chase: the place he ran from a flying count, now the place he learns to fly. (invented suggestion)
