# The introduction

- **Summary:** After dinner, Dorgal corners Tor outside the dining room with two henchmen, Marco and a frightening black-eyed man slightly too old to be a student. They accuse him of having an older girlfriend on the faculty. Tor decides to set his two threats on each other: he goes back in, asks Wensa to come and explain to them why she's following him, and introduces everyone very properly, Wensa as a royal guard and the others by the titles they hold in his eyes. Then he walks away and hopes they don't decide to gang up on him.
- **POV / mode:** Rotating third: Dorgal (wounded pride, honestly held; he doesn't know about the black-eyed man's influence, so he hides nothing). Camera: outside the dining room, then following Tor away. (POV approved by the user, October 18, 2026)
- **Location:** Just outside the cafeteria: a passage, a stair, the yard by the kitchen door; then back into the dining room and out again. (invented)
- **Present:** Tor, Dorgal, Marco, the black-eyed man; Wensa; the cafeteria crowd.
- **Reader learns:** Dorgal has turned on Tor, and has muscle; one of them is strange; Tor can turn his enemies against each other; Dorgal's group now knows Wensa is a royal guard.
- **Hidden (and how):** What the black-eyed man is, and his influence on Dorgal (a glance before Dorgal speaks, a crueler line than Dorgal's usual; nothing more); what passes between Wensa and Dorgal's men (never shown).

## Beats

All beats canon (user, October 17, 2026) unless labeled.

1. **Cornered.** After one dinner, Dorgal corners Tor, with **Marco** and a frightening man slightly too old to be a student, whose eyes are nearly all black except for the irises.
2. **The accusation.** They accuse Tor of having a girlfriend: an older one, a member of the faculty.
3. **The decision.** While they threaten him, Tor decides to push the two threatening groups together and hope they clash.
4. **Back inside.** He goes back into the dining room and asks Wensa to come out and explain to Dorgal and his henchmen exactly why she's following him around.
5. **She comes.**
6. **The introductions.** Tor introduces them to each other, very properly. He introduces Wensa **as a royal guard**, aloud, and gives the others titles that suit his view of them rather than their station. (royal guard: user, October 17, 2026)
7. **He walks away**, hoping they don't decide to gang up on him.

**Staging notes** (invented):

- **Dorgal's motive is wounded pride** (user): the workroom night, the perfect painted copy that did nothing, Tor's wandering finger that worked anyway. In his viewpoint, the resentment is honest and a little ashamed of itself. He's polished and theatrical (`dorgal.md`), "My dear Baker."
- **The black-eyed man's influence** (user; revealed much later): in Dorgal's viewpoint, Dorgal finds himself saying something crueler than he meant, and glances at the man without knowing why. The eyes are described exactly, through Dorgal, who is used to them and still doesn't like to meet them.
- **The accusation is Tor's own joke come back.** In chapter three he told Rolph it was a pity Wensa wasn't younger. Dorgal makes it an insult: Baker's being walked out by a schoolmistress. Non-sexual mockery only (`AGENTS.md` §9).
- **Tor's decision is visible:** a glance from Dorgal to the dining room door, a small "oh," the polite "Would you excuse me a moment?" Dorgal, baffled, lets him go.
- **Wensa coming out is the surprise.** She comes because Dorgal's men are now the thing she can't explain near Rolph's friend (`wensa.md`).
- **The titles.** Exactly correct manners, wrong categories. Suggestions: "Mistress Wensa, of the royal guard, who is following me. Master Dorgal Sorvey, who would like to frighten me. And his friends, whose names I don't know, who are helping him." The black-eyed man gives **no name** and says nothing (user, October 18, 2026).
- **"Royal guard" out loud** is Tor's literal honesty used as a weapon, and a big step: it blows her cover to these three, after Rolph's warning. Wensa's face goes cold. The comedy is best if Tor realizes it a beat too late, walking away: a stop, a wince, "Oh, *Tor*," and he walks faster.
- **The exit:** Tor doesn't look back. The camera stays with him. What happens behind him is never shown.
