# The template

- **Summary:** With the design finished, Tor sets out to make ten shields, and as always he builds a template first. It takes two days in an isolation chamber, over the rest day and into a class day; he asks Rolph to get his absence excused for the second. That night he comes back to the room and announces it's done. Rolph is mortified, thinking Tor drove himself this hard for the food drying field; Tor laughs and points at the lonely wooden template on the desk, finished days ago. Rolph gets him food and water, and Tor sleeps through the night.
- **POV / mode:** Rotating third: Rolph (secret-keeper rule). Camera: the chamber door and slate, then the room. (POV approved by the user, October 18, 2026)
- **Location:** An isolation chamber (Bet Haskins's undercroft); the building office or the headmaster's office, for the excuse; Tor and Rolph's room.
- **Present:** Tor, Rolph, Bet Haskins; whoever excuses the absence.
- **Reader learns:** Tor always makes a template first; a template is a trance build; ten shields, for his friends; Tor can ask for help now; the food dryer is done.
- **Hidden (and how):** Who the ten are for (he doesn't say yet; see scene 4); what the shield is worth (nobody prices it yet).

6. **Tor laughs** and points at the lonely **wooden template** on the desk: the food dryer, finished **a couple of days ago**. He's been working on the shield ever since.
7. **Food and water.** Rolph gets him food and water.
8. **Sleep.** Tor sleeps through the night.

**Staging notes** (invented):

- **The template is a trance, like chapter one's,** not like chapter two's copying. Show it from outside: Bet Haskins's slate (*BAKER, T. — In: …*), her checks every bell, food left at the door and not touched, the rest day's quiet in the undercroft.
- **Asking Rolph is a quiet change.** In chapter one Tor feared expulsion for missed classes. Now he asks, mid-build, without looking up, perhaps through the chamber door. Rolph going to the office for him is friendship in action, and a little funny: Rolph explaining to a clerk that his roommate can't come to class because he's busy being brilliant.
- **The ten copies.** Copying from the template is fast for Tor (fifty drying fields in a night). Whether the ten are made in the chamber or back in the room is a drafting choice; the user's beats have him finishing "the shield build" on the second night.
- **Rolph's mortification pays off chapter three,** where he told Tor to take his time on the food dryer. He thinks he did it again, worse.
- **The laugh is the reversal.** The thing Rolph feels guilty about was done days ago, alone, without fuss, and has been sitting on the desk unnoticed. The image: one plain wooden board, alone on the desk, and ten copper plates on cords. The joke is on Rolph's guilt, not on Tor.
- **Bet Haskins tells breakfast.** Her reaction rule (`bet-haskins.md`): sees something remarkable, tells no one, then finds she has something to say at breakfast. That's how the crowd at the test knows to come.
- **Food, water, sleep** is Rolph's care made physical: a cup held, bread torn, boots pulled off. Tor asleep mid-sentence.
