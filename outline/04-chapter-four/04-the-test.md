# The test

- **Summary:** After an ordinary morning of classes and lunch, Tor and Rolph walk to the testing ground with people trailing after them, and find a crowd already there. Tor announces he's ready, and Wensa attacks with three royal-guard rods almost before he can activate the shield: one blows up the pell, one bows and cracks the wall behind it, one lights a fire around Tor that she ends before the air runs out. Kolb beats on the shield for twenty minutes, then swings his cutter. Nothing gets through. Kolb laughs that it was meant to be tested on a pell; Wensa says a builder should have faith in his equipment. The headmaster wants some for the school; Sara asks if the deal still stands; Trice suggests selling the template. Then the two of them have to explain to Tor how many people will want this, and he still doesn't connect it with money. He's already thinking about version 2.
- **POV / mode:** Rotating third: Sara (she prices it, and she's the one who has to explain the scale; she hasn't held a viewpoint yet). Camera: the testing ground, wide. (POV approved by the user, October 18, 2026)
- **Location:** The testing ground: the practice yard (pell, the old wall), with a pond nearby. (testing ground canon; its being the practice yard is assumed)
- **Present:** Tor, Rolph, Kolb, Wensa, the headmaster, the building instructor, other faculty, Sara, Trice, Tovey, a crowd of students.
- **Reader learns:** The shield works against everything; Kolb never meant to test it on Tor; Wensa's rods; her cover is gone; the shield's commercial (and, by hint, military) worth; Tor's naivety about scale; version 2.
- **Hidden (and how):** Why Wensa is happy (she can't say; probably Rolph, who now has a shield of his own); what the crown will make of the shield (the headmaster's request and Wensa's pleasure only hint); who gets the 10% (still ambiguous).

## Beats

All beats canon (user, October 17, 2026) unless labeled.

1. **Morning.** Regular classes; lunch together.
2. **The walk.** Tor and Rolph head to the testing ground, with several people following along.
3. **The crowd.** Already there: the headmaster, several faculty including **Wensa** and the **building instructor**, **Sara and Trice**, and **Tovey**.
4. **Ready.** Tor announces he's ready for the test.
5. **Wensa attacks** with special magic rods almost before he can activate the shield.
6. **The first rod** seems to have no effect, until she turns it on the **pell**, and it **explodes**.
7. **The second rod** has no effect, until she points it at the pell's remains, and the **wall behind bows out and cracks**.
8. **The third rod** sets **fire** all around Tor. Tor realizes it's actually dangerous: the fire could burn up all the air, and he has no way to breathe once it's gone.
9. **Wensa stops it** before it becomes a real problem. He comes out unharmed.
10. **Kolb claims the right to test,** picks up a practice weapon, and beats on the shield for **twenty minutes**.
11. **The cutter.** When that does nothing but **crack the ground under Tor's feet**, Kolb draws his cutter and swings it at Tor. Nothing.
12. **Kolb laughs:** the test was supposed to be the shield on a **pell**, not on Tor. (Tor misunderstood all along.)
13. **Wensa counters** that a builder needs faith in his equipment. She seems **happy**, for some reason.
14. **Kolb is satisfied.**
15. **Suggestions.** Several people suggest other things to try (drowning Tor in the nearby **pond**). Nobody tries anything.
16. **The headmaster is enormously pleased,** and suggests Kolb beg Tor for a few for the school's use. Tor doesn't realize how valuable they are.
    - **Who gets the ten** (user, October 18, 2026): Tor keeps his own; one each goes to his immediate friends, **Rolph, Trice, Sara and Tovey**; the **rest go to the school, for Kolb to manage**. Tor can hand them out here, or the split can be settled in a line; either way the headmaster's request is answered on the spot.
17. **Sara asks** whether the shields are still in their deal. Tor agrees, still at **10%**. He isn't sure how he'd spend the money.
18. **Trice:** sell the **template** to the Debri house, and collect royalties without doing the work (though Tor is much faster than other builders).
19. **The scale.** Tor is very naive about how many people will want this. It has to be explained to him, and even then he doesn't connect it with how much money he stands to make.
20. **Version 2.** Every way he now sees it could have failed is already turning into the next version.

**Staging notes** (invented):

- **Tor walks on ready to die politely.** He's believed since chapter two that Kolb would test it on him. He may have said goodbye to Rolph at lunch, or given him instructions again (chapter three's will, reprised small). Kolb's punchline lands on all of it.
- **The rods are unexplained magic.** Each "nothing happens" beat is the shield working; the pell and the wall show what it stopped. The wall is the practice yard's old wall, "something older than the school" (`canon-notes.md`): it bows and cracks, which should worry someone.
- **Wensa attacks before he's ready.** It reads, to Tor, as an attempt on his life, and he'll go on believing she wants him dead. In Sara's viewpoint, Sara sees a guard testing a guard's tool, and sees her face afterward.
- **The fire is the one serious beat.** A hand to his throat, a held breath; then Wensa ends it. This is version 2's air problem, landing visibly.
- **Kolb's twenty minutes** is endurance comedy: the crowd sitting down, someone fetching water, Kolb sweating and grinning, Tor inside the field apologizing for the inconvenience. The ground cracking under his feet is version 2's second lesson.
- **The suggestions are a cheerful chorus** of students and faculty proposing ways to kill Tor more thoroughly. The pond is version 2's third lesson.
- **In Sara's viewpoint, the price comes first.** She's pricing it before Kolb has finished: what it's made of, how long it lasts, who'd buy it (`sara.md`). Her father's rule about not pricing some things aloud can apply. When she explains the scale (every guard, every soldier, every merchant on a road, every noble's child at the special academy), she does it gently, and watches it not land.
- **The military weight is hinted, never raised.** The headmaster's request, Wensa's pleasure, Tovey's silence. Nobody says the word "army."
- **The 10% stays ambiguous.** Sara and Trice may exchange a look.
- **End on Tor not getting it,** muttering about air and ground and water, while his friends do the arithmetic.
