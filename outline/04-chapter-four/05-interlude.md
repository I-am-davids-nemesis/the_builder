# Interlude

- **Summary:** After the test, life goes on: Tor goes to his classes and works on new versions of the shield, each one fixing something the test taught him. He makes a few copies of the different devices he has. And his templates are shipped off to the Debri house.
- **POV / mode:** Proposed rotating third: Sara (she arranges the shipment; she sees what the templates are worth as they leave) or Tor. Camera: a montage. (invented; to approve)
- **Location:** The building room, the room, the dormitory desk; the school gate or the carter's yard for the shipment. (invented)
- **Present:** Tor; Sara (and perhaps Trice) for the shipment; Rolph in passing.
- **Reader learns:** Shield versions exist; Tor took Trice's advice and sold the templates; the drying field and shield are going out into the world.
- **Hidden (and how):** What the Debri house pays, and how much Tor will make (nobody tells him in a way he hears); what the crown thinks (offstage).

- **The copies** are for the friends who were to get shields, and perhaps drying fields for the washing house again. Which devices, and for whom, is an open question.
- **The shipment** should be concrete: a crate, straw, a Debri clerk or carter, Sara's seal on the lid, Tor watching it go as if he's sending his own bread to market. The templates are the first things he's made that leave Lairdgren without him.
- **Tor's box** rhymes with Rolph's lockbox from chapter one: the lock Tor copied for their door, now on his own box of coin. A baker's son with a strongbox. He may look at it as if it belongs to someone else.
- **Wensa,** if present, is now openly a guard (her cover went at the test). She may watch the crate go.
