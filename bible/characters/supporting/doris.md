# Doris, meditation instructor

Named **Doris** (canon, user; earlier notes called her "Sophia"). Teaches the daily **meditation classes**, which train lighter versions of the building trance. (canon) Her sensing Tor's mental structures is an (invented) hook tied to his canon mental controls. A candidate for expansion to the full main-cast template.

- **Barely talks at all.** Each day she brings in a **new item** for the class to focus on. She doesn't react much to Tor's absence or his explanation. (canon, user)

Other personality details (assumed).

- **Role:** Trains students' mental discipline; the first to sense something odd inside Tor's mind.
- **Internal tension:** Duty to report what she senses vs. fear of what would happen to Tor if she does.
- **External tension:** Pressure from whoever built Tor's hidden mental structures to look away.
- **Reaction:** Senses a wall in Tor's mind → stops the session, calmly, and never explains why.
- **Voice:** Almost none. When she speaks, it's a word or two. Her silences and her objects do the talking. (canon, user: barely talks)
