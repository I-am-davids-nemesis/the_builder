# The Builder — Character Bible

The bible lets you drop a character into any scene and predict both **what they do** and **how they sound**. It is the source of truth for characters, places and series tension; the manuscript draws on it, never the other way around.

Split from `builder_character_bible_handoff.md` (October 9, 2026). Where the two disagree, the bible wins.

## Labels

Every fact carries one of:

- **(canon)** — in the source text or verified by research.
- **(assumed)** — our guess about what canon says; verify before relying on it.
- **(invented)** — our own addition.

Unlabeled profile content is (assumed) unless the file says otherwise.

## Profile template

**Main cast:**

- **Role** — who they are to Tor and to the plot.
- **Want vs. need** — what they chase, and what would actually fix them.
- **Internal tension** — two things they believe that can't both win.
- **External tension** — the person, faction or rule pushing on them.
- **Hidden aspect** — what the reader or other characters don't know yet.
- **Reaction rules** — if/then triggers for predicting behavior.
- **Voice** — diction, rhythm, tics, and words they never use.
- **Sample lines** — calibration for the voice.
- **Series slice** — what book 1 resolves and what it leaves open.

**Supporting cast:** role, one internal tension, one external tension, one reliable reaction, and a voice recognizable in a single line. Expand to the full template if a character becomes a larger player (Count Mississippi and Sophia are the likeliest).

## Tension rule

Each book resolves one small, concrete piece per character (a question answered, a relationship shifted) while the root conflict survives. If a book resolves a character's root conflict, that character's arc is over. See `tension-ledger.md`.
