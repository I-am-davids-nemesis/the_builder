# Reader knowledge

What the reader can know vs. what each character knows, by chapter. Use it to keep dramatic irony deliberate (`AGENTS.md` §3).

## Chapter 1

| Thread | What the reader is shown | What Tor knows | Who knows more |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rolph's family | A magic lockbox full of gold in their room; "among nobility," said like an insider; crop losses as a topic at home; a mother who'd want new magic for its prestige. Hints only, no admission. | That Rolph is from a merchant family; finds none of the hints strange | Rolph; possibly Sara, a real merchant's daughter (invented hook) |
| The invitation home | Rolph invites Tor to his family home in the capital over the break. In the rotating third, Rolph privately notes his mother's eager questions about Tor. The house is "big, draughty, full of forks," big enough to "lose a regiment in." | Thinks it's a kind invitation; worries only about being a burden; unaware of the family's interest in him | Rolph and his family |
| How nobles see Tor | Tovey is curious and wants a gift for his mother, not disdainful | Believes all nobles despise commoners; is afraid of Tovey | Tovey |
| The copies | Tor silently takes on making drying fields for everyone | Assumes he must | Nobody else; no one asked for that |
| The building instructor | Dunks Tor in a tub (comedy); a bystander is appalled. States his reason: the field has to be shown safe around people. | The stated reason; takes it as a fair test | The instructor alone knows he already expected it to be safe; the reader never learns it, though they may hear the vitality-field theory elsewhere and infer it |
| The quality of Tor's work | Nobody comments on it; the instructors' silence | Hears silence; doesn't know his work is better than average | The instructors assume it's top-notch; never said |
| Expulsion | Tor raises it; others treat it as a poor joke and move on. Nobody says why it isn't a risk. | Believes he might be expelled for missed classes | Everyone else: one builder like him per cohort is a triumph for the school |
| Tor's mental controls | Nothing yet | Nothing | — |
