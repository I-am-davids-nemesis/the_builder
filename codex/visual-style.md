# Visual style

How every picture of this book should look. The editor adds this to each image prompt it prepares; an empty section is left out. What a character or place looks like belongs in its own codex entry, not here.

## Medium

A cinematic 35mm film photograph: natural textures, shallow depth of field, real materials.

## Palette

Muted, earthy colors: weathered stone, brown wool, aged wood, dull copper; pale stone and soft greens in the capital.

## Period and setting

A pre-industrial world: the school is old stone in the hills, the capital a white-stone city with early nineteenth-century dress. Water comes from pumps, wells and buckets; light from windows, lamps, candles and fires.

## Light

Natural light only: soft gray daylight indoors, low sun outdoors, lamp and firelight at night.

## Framing

Eye level, unhurried compositions; places shown whole enough to find your way around them.

## Avoid

- text or lettering
- taps, faucets or modern plumbing
- electric lights
- modern clothing or objects
