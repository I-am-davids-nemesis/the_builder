# Patricia ("Trice")

Patricia sees the political board more clearly than anyone Tor's age, and she can't decide whether he's a friend or her best move.

## Description

Trice is the taller of the pair she makes with Sara. Her hair is dark. She wears it pinned up close and plain, and lets it down, past her shoulders, perhaps twice a term and never without a reason; in a hot room it comes loose. Her face, eyes and coloring are otherwise undescribed, except for the night Tovey knocked her down: for days afterward the whole right side of her face was swollen, purple and yellow, the eye on that side shut. When she's moved, the tips of her ears go pink, and that's the only sign she gives.

She wears the special school's black, and in the practice yard she sits with Sara on the bench by the water butt. She carries a small knife that appears and vanishes "the way it had come," a pouch of white powder, and a short pushing rod with a thumb catch. She fights dirty and fast, opening against boys with a kick straight up, and she turns into a wheel kick "like a door on its hinge." Off duty she has worn a shawl, which she folds into a precise small square when she needs something to do with her hands.

She moves without hurrying and without stopping, and crowds make room for her without deciding to. She sits very upright, or curls sideways in a big chair with her boots tucked under her, or folds her hands in her lap. Her face is usually calm; she watches people the way she watches a game board, files looks away, and counts what's owed. Her tells are small: a hand that shakes after a fright, a look at the sky "innocent as a cat," ears going pink or warm.

### Open questions

- Eye color, skin tone, face shape and features are never described.
- Height is only relative (taller than Sara); her build is unstated beyond a fighter's speed and kicks.
- Her age isn't given anywhere (she's been Sara's friend nine years, and a Lairdgren student).
- Pink ears are her tell, but Sara is also given pink ears once (after the mid-air curtsey, "Flight to Capital"). Keep the tell hers alone?
- Where she carries the knife, the powder pouch and the rod is never shown; a picture would have to place them.
- For a picture: hair up (her usual) or down (rare and deliberate)? The prompt uses up.
- Proposed fixed trait: dark hair, pinned up close and plain (down only rarely, past the shoulders).
- Proposed fixed trait: taller than Sara.
- Proposed fixed trait: the special school's plain black uniform.
- Proposed fixed trait: upright, unhurried, composed bearing; a calm face.

## Profile

- **Role:** A sharp noble student fluent in court maneuvering; Tor's guide to power and occasionally its instrument. (assumed)
- **Want:** Leverage. To be the person who saw Tor's value first.
- **Need:** To trust one person without calculating the return.
- **Internal tension:** Real affection for Tor vs. a worldview in which affection is a liability. She tells herself the fondness is part of the strategy.
- **External tension:** A family that expects her to deliver an advantage, and rivals (Tovey's house, possibly House B) who see Tor as a prize to be claimed.
- **Hidden aspect:** Whose interests she ultimately serves. Even she may not be sure.
- **Hidden from herself: high noble blood.** Trice has a hereditary field, and hers **drives other nobles into a rage**. It triggers rarely enough that she hasn't noticed she's doing it. Something triggered it in the courtyard and sent Tovey into a blind rage against her. (canon, user, October 9, 2026) Her parentage is the secret behind the secret; not revealed in book 1's early chapters. Who her blood comes from is not yet defined. (Chapter three makes her openly a duke's daughter, so what's hidden is the field and wherever it comes from, not her rank.)
- **Hidden from Tor: the special school** is a **spy school, with a side of assassination training**. (canon, user) **Not revealed at all in chapter two**: the black uniform, the knife and the powder are all the reader gets. In chapter three, Rolph tells Tor it trains people who might be assassination targets (canon, user). Both are true; Rolph gives the polite half. (user, October 17, 2026)
- **Hidden from Trice: her field caused the courtyard.** In chapter three she's the victim, everyone treats her as one, and she doesn't suspect otherwise. (canon, user)

## Reaction rules

- **Something new and valuable appears** → gets it priced, and knows who to ask. (canon seed: has Sara value the field)
- **New information** → files it, reveals none of it, and asks one more question than seems polite.
- **Tor is naive in public** → rescues him smoothly, then lectures him in private.
- **Caught in a manipulation** → doesn't deny it; explains why it was the right call.
- **Genuinely moved** → gets brisk and changes the subject to logistics.

## Voice

- **Diction:** precise, courtly; vocabulary of trade and debt ("owe," "cost," "position").
- **Rhythm:** clipped statements; rhetorical questions that are really instructions.
- **Tics:** calls Tor "Master Baker" with ironic formality; "Who benefits?" ("Think, Tor" is Tor's own canon self-talk, so it's not hers.)
- **Never:** says "I don't know." She says "Not yet."

## Series slice

Book 1 establishes that Patricia will protect Tor when it counts. It leaves open whose interests she serves, and whether she'll ever stop calculating.
