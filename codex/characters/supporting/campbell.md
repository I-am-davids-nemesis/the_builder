# Campbell, dean of students

The school's dean of students, sent to fetch Tor at dawn. (canon, user, chapter three)

- **Facts:** Surname **Campbell**. Waits in Tor and Rolph's room with a sleepy Rolph when Tor comes back after the courtyard fight; **wears all black** and looks grumpy. Drags Tor away before Rolph can answer, to the basement of the administration building. Is **surprised** when Tor, in trance, focuses on the warded door, but takes him to it anyway; opens it with an **access token** pressed to a **pad**.
