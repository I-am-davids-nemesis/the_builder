# Douglas Sorvey, merchant

A merchant who feels dangerous to Tor before he's said anything dangerous.

## Facts

- Named **Douglas Sorvey**; **Dorgal's father.** (canon, user) Tor doesn't realize it; nobody else thinks it worth mentioning, since it's common knowledge.
- Waits in the **building instructor's office**, brought in to "do business" with Tor. (canon, user)
- Offers **two gold** per clothes drying device, and to pay for any new projects Tor comes up with. Agreeable when Tor says Sara represents him. Leaves his **card** for future contact. (canon, user)
- **Tor can feel that he's dangerous.** (canon, user)

## Profile

All (invented) unless labeled.

- **Role:** The first outsider with money to want Tor's work; the trade face of the Sorvey family, and Dorgal's father (canon) and so the weight behind Dorgal's need to win (`dorgal.md`, assumed).
- **Internal tension:** A merchant's patience vs. whatever makes him dangerous; he'd rather buy than take, and knows he could take.
- **External tension:** Rival houses (Debri) and a school that guards its builders.
- **Reaction:** Refused → agreeable, unhurried, leaves a card. He's planning to come back.
- **Voice:** Courteous, low, few words; treats two gold as a generous offer, and says so.
  - "Two gold a piece, Scholar Baker, and I'll take every one you make. Keep the card."
