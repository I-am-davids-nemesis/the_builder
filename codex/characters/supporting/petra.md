# Petra

Combat classmate who is kind to Tor.

- Tall, dark-skinned, bright smile. (canon)
- Beats Tor in combat class, but kindly. (canon)
- Tor can't recall her name. (canon) This fits his rule of assuming a girl's friendliness is kindness or a mistake.
- Tensions and voice still to be developed.
