## Description

Petra is tall and dark-skinned, with a bright smile (canon); the manuscript adds almost nothing to her looks. What it shows is how she moves through the practice yard. She stays late when Kolb keeps her back to work on her guard. She once lifted the heavy iron practice bar for a dare in her first year, and put it down again quickly. She has beaten Tor eleven times since then. When he can't let go of the bar, she simply takes it out of his hands. She gives her own name plainly, without fuss, when he forgets it, and she smiles at him easily, which surprises him. She's good-natured about being forgotten: "it was hard to mind, when he was so sorry about it."
