# Maria

Minor antagonist; the root of Tor's romantic self-doubt.

- In Tor's first year he asked her out, **to a school dance** (user, October 18, 2026); she screamed that he was poor, ugly and above his station, **in the cafeteria, in front of most of the student body** (user, October 31, 2026). He hid in his room for weeks. (canon) It's why, in chapter four, he can't believe Sara and Trice mean their proposals.
- The rejection can reach the page through gossip, through Rolph, or through Maria herself, since Tor's thoughts aren't narrated.
- She left the school after the dance (user). In chapter 6 she reappears as **Count Ward's wife**, much shorter than him, on his arm at the royal dinner: the chapter's cliffhanger (user, October 30, 2026).
- At school she was **no taller than Tor**; she was younger then and has grown a good deal since, since nobles grow into their size slowly (user, October 31, 2026).
- **About Rolph's age** (user, October 30, 2026); Ward, who is middle-aged, married a younger woman. **She's the brains of the pair**; Ward isn't very bright (user, October 30, 2026). How they met, and whether Ward knows about the dance, is open.
- **At the Ward dinner** (invented; accepted by the user, October 31, 2026): she snubs Ursula publicly, the first move in Ward's defense. She steers him with a hand on his sleeve and murmurs between his lines, and leads him out of his rage and the room. She knows the tale of Wylde and Doretta, and the troubled feeling under her smile at Tor is fear that he's a Wylde. Tor thinks she doesn't recognize him. The reader is steered toward her as the brain behind the brooch, but it's never stated.
- **From the chapter 6 draft** (invented; pending the user's review): slim, much shorter than Ward, in deep green, very carefully dressed in a way that doesn't look careful; her eyes take the measure of every person in the room and stop on Tor. She was at Lairdgren in Rolph and Tor's year and left before the end of the first.
- **After the cafeteria** (canon, user, November 1, 2026; told by Rolph in chapter 8): she ran a rumor campaign against Tor for weeks, and the staff feared for him. Her family recalled her before the end of the year; Rolph doesn't know why. She was in Tor's **theory class** and sat by the window (user, November 1, 2026). From the chapter 8 draft (invented; pending the user's review): Tor asked her at lunch; she screamed for about six minutes, and two fourth-year girls walked her out; she left a week before exams.
- **Ward's mother sent away** (user, November 8, 2026): now that Ward has a wife, his mother has been sent off to a damp dower house in County Ward, on low wet ground by the coast, which Maria found for her (invented; accepted by the user, November 8, 2026); Maria needed her out of the way to be sure she controls Ward. Tor meets the mother at the queen's dinner (chapter 10, scene 2) as a lady with mildew in her house, and doesn't learn who she is; Varley knows her only as "the dowager from the fen country," so the reader can't connect her yet (user, November 8, 2026).
