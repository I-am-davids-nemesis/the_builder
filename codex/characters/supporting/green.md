# Green ("Burks")

The Ancient who has run Noram for three thousand years, met by Tor as a man who brings him clothes. Every scene with him should be funny on the surface and enormous underneath.

## Facts

- **Green** is an **Ancient**, one of the long-lived, color-named humans (`canon-notes.md`). He has been **running Noram for the last 3,000 years**, and he **invented the system of magic the continent uses**. (user, October 27, 2026)
- In chapter five, scene 4, he comes to Tor's room in the small house as **"Burks"**, with an armful of Rolph's old clothes, and Tor takes him for a servant. Who he is **doesn't come up yet**: it's a funny episode. (user, October 27, 2026)
- **Slightly taller than Tor**, so small for someone of rank (nobles are big). This is a **deliberate hint about Burks** (user, October 27, 2026). Looks strangely familiar to Tor, and something in his voice is familiar too (user): **he looks and sounds a bit like Tor** (user, October 27, 2026).
- **The palace and its staff know him as Count Lairdgren**, the same name as the school (user, October 27, 2026). (Spelled as the school is in the codex: Lairdgren.) "Burks" is the name he gives Tor.
- Tor's middle name is **Green** (`tor.md`, canon).
- **Tor is a clone of Count Lairdgren** (Green) and looks very much like him (user, November 6, 2026; series-long secret). **Not discussed in book 1: tiny hints only**, and the reader is told nothing. Hints so far: the familiar face and voice (chapter 5); a palace guard who knows the count almost calls Tor "Count" and turns it into "Master Tor" (chapter 9, scene 1). Tor never notices.

- **From the chapter five drafts** (invented; pending the user's review): to the household he is a small, neat, quiet man in a plain dark coat, who has come and gone for all of Quavel's twenty years and looks no older; nobody quite knows what he is, and "when he was in the house, the house knew it." Uses a servant's voice as Burks. Studies the amulet's etched line before touching it, then wears it and tests it in the kitchen; comes back dry and calls it "Most effective, sir." Says "Thank you, Tor" once, and takes the amulet as a gift. Ties Tor's stock and stands behind him in the glass.
- **Supplies Tor's materials, unseen** (user, November 1, 2026): while Tor is at the Ward dinner, a pile of copper squares appears on his desk; Burks saw to it. Tor doesn't notice that anyone has started supplying him.
- **From the chapter 10, scene 5 draft** (invented; pending the user's review): copper keeps appearing in the orangery; fifty oiled leather straps with brass buckles and rings appear after Tor wishes aloud ("a man in the lower town, sir… He had a good many"); he kneels and buckles the rigs on with Tor; a long carved chest of dark red-lit wood (flowers, wrens, finches, a heron on the lid, branch handles) appears while Hale is out of the room; he carries one end down the stair backward without a breath, with a big man in a leather apron nobody in the palace knows. In the yard Rolph steps back and half bows to him; he doesn't look at Rolph.
- **Never holds the viewpoint.** No narration from any Ancient's point of view; he's seen only from outside (user, October 27, 2026; `AGENTS.md` §3).
