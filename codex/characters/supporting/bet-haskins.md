# Bet Haskins, keeper of the isolation chambers

All details (invented; accepted by the user, October 9, 2026). She first appears in chapter one's opening, where Tor comes out of his trance.

## Facts

- Keeper of the **isolation chambers** for **thirty-one years**. Unnamed in the camera-mode draft.
- Looks in on each occupant **every bell**, day and night, and keeps a **tally on a slate** hung by each door. Students chalk their own times in and out. Tor's entry: *BAKER, T. — In: late, fourth day* / *Out: first bell, rest day*, followed by *Thank you*.
- Knows the bell intimately: which stroke is the clapper and which the echo off the chapel roof.
- Can read students by how they come in: the ones out in an hour with nothing, the ones who sit a day and a night for a half-working lamp-square.
- Knows the **fast-drying sign**: she has seen paint dry like that "perhaps a dozen times" in thirty-one years.
- As a girl she once touched the poured courses at the bottom of the bell tower, and they felt warmer than they should.
- Tor thanks her every time he signs the slate; no other student ever has.

## Profile

- **Role:** The school's quiet witness to building. She sees more novel work begin and end than any master, and the school's gossip about it often starts with her.
- **Internal tension:** Pride in what she's seen vs. the conviction that nobody listens to a keeper.
- **External tension:** Her place is low. Masters and students alike treat her as part of the furniture of the undercroft.
- **Reaction:** Sees something remarkable → tells no one directly; then finds she has something to say at breakfast. She's how the school learns, by the rest-day breakfast, that Baker came out with a board and went to the washing house.
- **Reaction:** A student says something foolish (e.g., Tor fearing expulsion) → says nothing.
- **Voice:** Few words, plain and practical; talks to herself as much as to anyone.
  - "Well, then."
  - "You'll want feeding."
  - "Sign the slate."
