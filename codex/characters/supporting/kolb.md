# Kolb, head combat instructor

Named **Kolb** (canon, user; earlier notes called him "Carl"). **Head instructor** of combat, which takes up the afternoons. (canon, user) Combat class is mixed boys and girls. (canon)

## Facts

- **Tall**, **middle-aged looking**, **bald**, with a **scar on his face**; a lot of corded muscle without being overly bulky. (canon, user) The scar is **pale and runs from the corner of one eye down into his jaw**. (invented; accepted by the user)

- At midday, at the end of chapter one, he **confronts Tor in the cafeteria** and **quietly promises extra intensive workouts** for daring to miss his class. (canon, user)
- Rolph backs Tor as far as he can, but nothing is going to stop Kolb from taking his "revenge." (canon, user)
- Chapter one ends with Tor heading to Kolb's combat class, afraid; the class opens chapter two. (canon, user)

- **Chapter two:** relaxed, even chatty, with Tor at the pell. Hits it hard enough to shake the yard; says one such blow could kill someone without armor or a shield device; cuts the pell clean through with a **four-foot cutter**. (canon, user)
- **Sets the shield assignment:** two weeks to build a shield that survives both kinds of attack for at least twenty minutes. (canon, user)
- **Always tells Tor his best way to survive a fight is to run away.** Sends him on a ten-mile run to the nearest town "to warm up"; next day, three times as far, then the pell with an **iron bar** until Tor can't lift it. (canon, user)
- Pairs Tor with Trice and Sara for unarmed sparring. When they complain Tor has a shield, he **laughs**: Tor is working on the project, and now that Kolb knows it'll likely work, he'll come at it even harder. (canon, user)
- Talks with the **head of the special school's department** about Tor; tells him Tor can do it, or he wouldn't have set the task (as overheard by Trice). (canon, user)
- **Chapter three:** arrives at the warded room with Tovey. In the stand-off with Wensa, **laughs** and says he taught Tor all of that, and Tor takes direction well: he ran away from Tovey, as taught. Taught Tor the **defensive stance** Tor falls into. (canon, user, October 17, 2026) It's the first time he praises Tor in public, if sideways. (invented reading)
- **Chapter four, the test** (canon, user): claims the right to test; beats on the shield with a practice weapon for twenty minutes (cracking the ground under Tor), then swings his cutter. Laughs that the test was meant to be the shield **on a pell**, not on Tor. Satisfied. Never meant to test it on Tor; Tor misunderstood (user, October 17, 2026).
- **Chapter four, the shields** (canon, user, October 18, 2026): manages the school's share of Tor's shields: the rest of the first ten, after Tor's friends have theirs, and the further copies Tor makes for the school.
- **Chapter four, later** (canon, user, October 18, 2026): suggests Tor build a device to make a person **fly**. When Tor says it's impossible: the **Austrans** do it, their chief advantage over Noram; and didn't Tor just see Tovey flying? Feeding Tor problems on purpose (invented reading).
- His "revenge" turns out to be a build assignment. That leans the hidden aspect toward training, though Tor still hears it as a death threat. (invented reading)

- **From the chapter two drafts** (invented; accepted by the user): wears the cutter's hilt on his belt every day. Smiles rarely; Sara has never heard him laugh aloud until the sparring. Punishes Rolph with two hundred cuts at the pell for speaking twice. Watches Tor leave on the run and says "Nice and loose" to nobody. Calls Trice and Sara "Black." Keeps Petra back late to work on her guard. "That's my homework you're complaining about."

## Profile

- **Role:** A retired soldier who judges students only by what they can actually do. (assumed)
- **Internal tension:** Contempt for nobles who play at war vs. protectiveness toward the kids who'll one day fight one. (assumed)
- **External tension:** Noble parents who demand their sons win; and a builder student whose trances take him out of combat for days. (first assumed; second canon-adjacent)
- **Reaction:** A student misses his class → doesn't shout; makes a quiet, specific promise of what it will cost them, and keeps it. (canon, user)
- **Reaction:** Tor gets hurt → no sympathy in class, quiet extra lessons after. (assumed)
- **Hidden aspect:** Whether the "revenge" is punishment or training he thinks Tor needs. The user's quotation marks leave room for both; from outside, they look the same. (invented, undecided)
- **Voice:** Short imperatives, soldier's slang; quietest when most dangerous. (assumed)
  - "Again. Feet. You're small, so be fast. Again."
  - "You owe me an afternoon, Baker. I counted. Tomorrow we start paying it back."
