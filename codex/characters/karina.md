# Karina

Karina is a princess who has learned that the right object in the right hands is worth more than a title, and every scene with her should test whether she ever wants anything for its own sake.

A main character from chapter 5 on, with more plot to come later in the series (user, November 1, 2026).

## Facts

- **Princess**, the elder of Rolph's two younger sisters: **18**; her sister **Varley** is 16 (user, October 25, 2026). Daughter of King Richard and Queen Constance (`rolph.md`).
- Calls Rolph **"Alphie."** (user)
- **Walks into Tor's room on purpose** on his first evening at the palace, to meet the new guest, using "You're not Alphie" as her excuse. She finds him half-dressed by the cooling plate. (user, chapter 5)
- **Wants his devices for favor in her circle of friends** (user, October 25, 2026): to own what nobody else in the city owns. Tor gives her four cooling plates, two for her and two for Varley.
- **Tor feels her field** as sly, quick and self-focused, "not evil," like a cat that has found the cream (user; chapter 5). She is **startled that he can tell** when she's surprised.
- Wears an **old mechanical watch** on a chain (user, November 1, 2026).
- **Chapter 7:** knocks early to ask for a personal cooling amulet for her day out, fetches the acid and brushes, helps etch the plates in the yard, and gets one. She kisses Tor's cheek, pretends to blush, and scurries off (user). Tor decides nobody at home would ever believe it, and laughs it off.
- **From the chapter 5–6 drafts** (invented; pending the user's review):
  - Has practiced a shy little dip of the chin in a glass since she was twelve.
  - Times her entrances: not too soon, not too late, no knock, so she can always say she had the wrong door.
  - Measures everything against **Lady Arden**, who has one cooling plate in her morning room and receives in it every afternoon in a shawl. "Nobody has two, you know… except of course my parents. And obviously you."
  - At dinner, in blue, she announces she has two plates, and watches Tor "doing sums." Her mother: "Karina is going to be impossible."
  - Smirks at Tor's first-bite mistake.

## Profile

All (invented; pending the user's review) unless labeled.

- **Role:** The royal family's most worldly child, and the first person at court to treat Tor as currency. Through her the reader sees what Tor's work is worth in the capital's social market.
- **Want:** Standing among the young women of the Hill: to be first, to own what nobody else owns, to be the one everyone asks. (Favor with her friends, user.)
- **Need:** To value something, or someone, for what it is rather than what it gets her. Tor, who gives her things for nothing, is the first test of that.
- **Internal tension:** She plays the court's game better than anyone her age and enjoys it, *and* she's surprised to find she likes the boy she's playing it on. She tells herself the liking is part of the play.
- **External tension:** A princess's every move is watched and priced. She is younger than Rolph and outshone by him, and her mother sees through her ("Karina is going to be impossible"). Her friends' favor is only as good as her last coup.
- **Hidden aspect:** What she does with the favor she gains: whose circle it is, what it buys, and how far she would trade on Tor's name. (Open; more plot later in the series, user.)

## Reaction rules

- **Sees something new and rare** → wants it first, and works out who to show it to before she's finished admiring it.
- **Caught out** (someone reads her surprise, as Tor does) → goes still, denies it smoothly, and files the person as dangerous and interesting.
- **Given something freely** → is pleased, and a little uneasy: she doesn't know what's owed. Then she thanks prettily and finds a way to make it look like her idea.
- **Bored** → makes something happen, preferably to someone else.
- **Her brother is in trouble** → teases him in public, defends him in private. (assumed)

## Voice

- **Diction:** light, quick, polished; court phrasing worn lightly; says "Alphie."
- **Rhythm:** a breath of hesitation, practiced, before the line that matters; questions that are really bids.
- **Tics:** a shy dip of the chin; "Nobody has… except…"; the excuse ready before the entrance.
- **Never:** asks directly for what she wants in front of an audience, or admits she's been surprised.

## Sample lines

- "You're not Alphie. Oh, I'm *so* sorry to catch you dressing."
- "Nobody has two, you know. Except, of course, my parents. And obviously you."
- "I don't suppose you know where Alphie is?" (when she knows exactly)

## Series slice

Book 1 introduces her as charming, calculating and a little startled by Tor. It leaves open what her friends' favor is for, how far she'll trade on him, and whether she ever wants anything from him that isn't a thing.
