# Sara

Sara treats Tor as a person rather than a phenomenon, which is exactly why her secret, if she has one, would hurt most.

- Spelled **Sara**. (canon)
- Daughter of a **large merchant house**, perhaps the **third daughter**. (canon, user; birth order uncertain)
- Has **a lot of exposure to the capital** and to the best magic the continent has to offer. (canon, user)
- Usually found **with Patricia**; the two are a pair. (canon, chapter one)

He walked her through the **glass halls on the river** in the capital, where fine fields are sold set in ironwood with silver-inlaid lines. Carries a leather satchel; often has ink on her fingers. ## Profile

- **Role:** Tor's steadiest friend and the emotional center of his school life (assumed); the one who knows what magic is worth (canon).
- **Want:** A life that's hers, not decided by her family's rank or by the storm around Tor.
- **Need:** To stop shrinking herself to keep the peace. She and Tor share that flaw.
- **Internal tension:** Wanting to stay close to Tor vs. seeing that closeness to him is dangerous and draws attention she never wanted.
- **External tension:** Rich but not noble: a merchant's daughter among nobles (canon background; framing assumed), so she's the easiest person for Tor's enemies to pressure. A third daughter of a trading house may be expected to marry or trade well for the family. (assumed)
- **Hidden aspect:** What her family wants from the friendship, if anything, and anything she hasn't told Tor. (Not yet defined.) Possible hook (invented): as a real merchant's daughter, she may notice that Rolph's "merchant family" story doesn't fit.

## Reaction rules

- **Tor is overworking** → physically removes the tools and puts food in front of him.
- **Snubbed by a noble** → polite and unbothered on the surface; cries later, alone.
- **Tor is in danger** → runs toward it, not away, even without a plan.
- **Shown a piece of magic** → sizes it up by material, duration and market, almost without meaning to. (canon seed: prices the drying field)
- **Asked to choose between Tor and her family** → stalls, bargains, and tries to find a third option. That indecision is her main flaw.

## Voice

- **Diction:** warm, direct, everyday words; uses Tor's name often.
- **Rhythm:** gentle questions, then one blunt sentence.
- **Tics:** "Tor. Look at me."; teasing that never stings.
- **Never:** flatters or uses titles to gain ground.

## Sample lines

- "Tor. Look at me. When did you last eat something that wasn't a crust?"
- "You don't have to fix everyone. Some of us just like you."
- "I'm scared too. I'm still coming."

## Series slice

Book 1 makes Sara the reader's trusted anchor. It leaves open her family's position, the cost of standing beside Tor, and anything she hasn't told him.
