# The building instructor's office (canon)

- **What it pressures:** Where the school meets money: the instructor brings a merchant here to buy Tor's work.
- **Who it tests most:** Tor, the building instructor, Douglas Sorvey

## Description

The building master's office is on the first floor of the north side of the school, up a stair and along a corridor of bare boards from the long north building room. A bench stands in the corridor beside its door, where late students wait with their copy-work; early in the morning, before breakfast, the far end of that corridor is still dark. The door can be left ajar, and through the gap someone on the bench sees a slice of the room: the end of the desk, the window, whoever is standing there.

Inside it is a plain working room, small enough that the door stays close at the back of anyone who stops two steps in, and that a man standing up seems to take up all of it. It smells of linseed and cold ashes. There is a desk with the master's chair behind it, facing the door, and one other chair in front of it; papers and slips lie on the desk, and work brought in for inspection is set down on it. A window gives the room its light, clean and even in the morning, and the master spends much of his time standing at it, holding boards up and tipping them so the light runs across the paint. When nobody speaks the room goes completely silent, quiet enough that what is said in it carries to the bench outside.

### Look

A small, plain office with a single window letting in clean, even daylight. A wooden desk sits with its chair behind it facing the door, and one plain chair stands in front of it; papers and painted pine boards lie on the desktop. Outside the door, a corridor of bare floorboards with a wooden bench against the wall.
