## Description

The washing house stands at the back of the dormitory court, its door opening straight onto the court's stones. It is a long, low room of wet stone with flagstones underfoot and a drain running down the middle of the floor. One pump stands at the near end; a copper boiler sits in the far corner over a banked fire. Along the wall stands a row of old white tubs, glazed inside like dinner plates, each on four iron feet cast as an animal's paws. Nobody knows what animal, or why the school once had so many. There is at least one bench beside the tubs where washing is laid out, and at least one window. The door shuts tight, which makes the room the one place in the school where Tor can fill the air with straw smoke and nobody minds. A tub turned upside down serves as a seat.

On a rest day morning, a little after the second bell, the room is busy, low, damp and full of steam from somebody's copper. It smells of wet stone, ash, wool and other people's stockings. Early on a school day it stands empty, gray and dripping, and the only sound is water running into a single tub like a small stream. The other sounds are water slopping over a tub's rim, pattering on the flagstones and fanning toward the drain, and the copper ticking in its corner when the room goes quiet. The soap used here is gray, cracked and smells faintly of ash. In the cold snap the sheets won't dry, and the room gets one of Tor's warming plates.
