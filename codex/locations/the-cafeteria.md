# The cafeteria (canon)

- **What it pressures:** Public, everyday, and inescapable: everyone eats there, so whatever happens there has an audience. Chapter one ends as Tor leaves it for combat.
- **Who it tests most:** Tor, Rolph, Kolb, Wensa
- **Chapter four:** Wensa eats there whenever Tor does. Dorgal and his men corner Tor after dinner, just outside, and Tor goes back in to fetch Wensa. (canon, user)

## Description

The cafeteria (also "the dining hall", or just "the hall") is one big room of long tables with benches, enough of them that a girl can throw an apple at someone "from three tables away." Tall windows run along at least one side, and Rolph likes the end of a bench by them. At the other end is the kitchen door, with a long table beside it where Tor and Rolph sit in chapter four, and a hatch where diners take their plates and cups when they're done. There are at least two doors, and you can sit at the end of a table with your back to the wall and see both. One door opens onto a dim passage lined with coat pegs, lit by a lamp at each end, that runs down to the kitchen yard. Students can also walk straight out from the hall to the practice yard. The room is far enough from the kitchen that soup comes out steaming and is lukewarm by the time it reaches the tables on a cold day. Meals are breakfast, lunch at the noon bell, and supper.

It's loud. At midday it's "full and loud," and at supper it's "loud and steamy." Through the open door you hear its light and noise and the clatter of plates. The noise drops table by table when Kolb walks between them and rises again behind him. On the day of Tor's test the whole room goes quiet and eats slowly, eyes on its plates. The smells follow the menu: the same stew on the first day of each week, porridge at breakfast, fish at supper, and wet wool from the coat passage on a rainy evening. Rain at supper runs down the tall windows in sheets. What's on the tables is plain: bowls, plates, spoons and cups; eggs, bread, rolls and butter, cups of milk, apples, salt, and a tea Rolph calls dreadful. Hob works in the kitchens. The hall also carries every rumor in the school ("At supper the whole hall knew where Tor was"). Staff seem to eat at a staff table, but Wensa sits at the end of a student table.

### Look

A long, high-windowed hall filled with rows of long wooden tables and benches. Tall windows line one side; at the far end a kitchen door and a serving hatch are set in the wall. Plain crockery: bowls, plates, cups. Daylight comes in through the windows at noon; at supper, light pours out through the open door into a dim passage.

### Open questions

- Where is the staff table? "Haskins told the staff table at breakfast" suggests staff eat in a separate spot or at their own table, yet Wensa eats at the end of a student table. Is the staff table in this room?
- Size: how many tables and how many students? Nothing gives the dimensions or the ceiling height.
- Materials: the walls, floor, roof beams and table wood are never described. The image prompt assumes stone and scrubbed wood.
- Heating: the cold-snap outline mentions "the cafeteria hearth," but no manuscript scene shows a hearth or fireplace.
- Lighting after dark: the hall gives off "light" at supper, but its lamps, candles or light-squares aren't described.
- Where are the windows relative to the kitchen door, and which doors lead to the practice yard and to the coat passage? Are they the same door?
- How do students get their food: served at the tables (the cold-snap soup "reached the tables"), or collected at the hatch?
