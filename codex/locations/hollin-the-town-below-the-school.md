# Hollin, the town below the school

- **What it pressures:** Money and ordinary people; where Tor's work first reaches real lives. Five miles down a long slope; a square with a well (Kolb's runners slap its stone), a pump, a chandler's, a little chapel whose bell strikes the half hours, and a mill road out the far side. From the road the school's bell tower stands on the ridge like a finger. A mile short of town stands an old marker stone, gray and smoother than stone should be, with letters nobody can read. The east road from there runs into the trees toward Two Rivers, four days on foot. (invented; accepted by the user)
- **Who it tests most:** Tor, Miriam, Sara
