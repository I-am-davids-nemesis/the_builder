# Debri House

The Debri house in the capital, the merchant house Sara's family runs. Description from the lookbook image (`lookbook/gemini-generated-image-ttq8f4ttq8f4ttq8.jpg`; pending the user's review).

The house faces a wide, open forecourt paved in great pale flagstones, worn smooth and a little uneven, bright as a threshing floor in the afternoon sun. Everything is built of the same white stone as the rest of the capital, cut in big squared blocks with fine joints, warm and creamy where the light falls and gray-blue in the shade. It rises in tiers like a stepped hill: a plain, heavy base of stone along the forecourt; above it a long terrace with a pierced stone balustrade, its panels carved into a lattice of interlocking stars and crosses; then a second terrace; then a third, crowned by a row of small horseshoe arches open to the sky. Wings stand forward on either side like the arms of a chair, so the forecourt feels half enclosed and the house seems to come out to meet whoever arrives.

In the middle, a broad flight of shallow white steps climbs from the forecourt to the first terrace and the great door: a pointed horseshoe arch set in a tall square frame carved all over with shallow pattern, vines and knots and borders within borders. The door itself is a pair of enormous dark wooden leaves, studded and paneled in squares, with a smaller arched wicket cut into one of them for everyday use, so the great door needn't open for anyone less than a guest. Long cloth banners hang from poles on either side of the doorway and along the terrace, in faded saffron and rust, each with a dark emblem near the foot. The windows are all arches, horseshoe-shaped and in pairs, some deep and shadowed with heavy curtains of tawny cloth drawn half across against the heat.

It's a green house as well as a white one. Big terracotta pots stand along every terrace and at the foot of the stairs, crowded with palms, broad-leaved plants and small fan palms whose fronds spill over the balustrades. Striped canvas awnings in sand and rust are rigged out over the upper terraces to make shade, and a parasol stands on the first terrace to the left. The light is high, hazy summer light, white at the top of the house and gold lower down. It is a rich man's house that wants to be pleasant to sit in, built for the capital's heat: deep doorways, thick walls, shade wherever shade can be made.
