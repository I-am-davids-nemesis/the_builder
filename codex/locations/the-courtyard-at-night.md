# The courtyard at night (canon, chapter two)

- **What it pressures:** The school's public center emptied out; the bell tower overhead and nobody to see. A clouded moon, a lamp at the gate lodge with nobody awake, and the long hall's wall throwing sound back. (invented; accepted by the user) Where Tor finds Trice losing a fight to Tovey.
- **Who it tests most:** Tor, Trice, Tovey

## Description

The main court of Lairdgren is a wide open square of flagstones, wide enough that a voice at one side carries only as a tone to the other. The bell tower stands at its corner, square and plain, the bell hung open at the top under a little peaked roof; its upper courses are weathered, patched gray stone, and its lowest three are something older, smooth and seamless and gray, poured rather than cut, warmer to the touch than they should be and never touched by frost. Around the court stand the dormitory (its door crossed to on the diagonal), the door at the foot of the east stair with the workroom windows above it, the long hall, whose wall throws sound back across the stones, and the student store. Up the court toward the gate a lamp burns at the gate lodge (the porters' lodge), far off and with nobody awake in it. Past the corner of the long hall the ground drops to the lower lawn and the low wall of the kitchen garden.

At night the court is dark. On the night of the fight the early hours are clear and cold, with the bell tower standing black over the court and a dog barking down the hill. Later a moon sits behind clouds, giving just enough light to make the tower a black shape against a dark gray sky. Every dormitory window is dark but one. It's cold enough that breath shows as smoke, and the flagstones are gritty and wet underhand, every crack visible when light falls on them. The only light comes from the sparks off Tovey: short blue-white spills that light the stones around his feet, then a long crackling sheet that throws shadows jumping across the court. Then the clouds move off the moon, and a thin gray light falls over everything.

The court carries sound. A cry goes right across it and comes back off the long hall's wall; a dropped sack of copper hits the flagstones with a crash that rings all the way around. Heavy, uneven steps sound on the stones, and the sparks fizz and crackle like fat in a pan. From the tower the bell strikes the hours, its echo coming back off the chapel roof.

### Look

A broad square court paved with gray flagstones, ringed by plain old stone buildings with dark windows: a long hall, a dormitory, a stair door. At one corner rises a square, plain bell tower with an open bell under a small peaked roof; its upper stone is weathered and patched, its bottom three courses smooth, jointless poured gray. Far off by the gate a single lamp burns at a lodge. At night it is lit only by a clouded moon.

### Open questions

- **Size and layout:** never given. Where do the dormitory, the east stair door, the long hall, the student store, the chapel and the gate stand relative to each other and to the tower's corner?
- **Weather:** the night is “clear and cold” during the build, but cloudy by the fight; the stones are “wet.” Did clouds and rain or dew come in during the night? It should stay consistent.
- **Gate lodge or porters' lodge?** The camera draft says “porters' lodge,” the rotating-third drafts say “gate lodge.” The same building?
- **The lit dormitory window:** whose is it, and does it matter?
- **The chapel:** its roof echoes the bell, but it's never placed or seen in the court.
- **Frost:** there's frost on the court's flags in chapter one, and the fight night is cold enough to see breath. Is there frost that night, or only wet?
- Proposed fixed trait: gray flagstones over the whole court.
- Proposed fixed trait: a square, plain bell tower at one corner, bell open under a small peaked roof; weathered, patched upper stone; bottom three courses smooth, jointless poured gray, never frosted.
- Proposed fixed trait: a single lamp at the gate lodge, far across the court.
- Proposed fixed trait: the long hall's blank wall on one side; the dormitory and the east stair door facing the court.
