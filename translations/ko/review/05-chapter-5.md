# 제5장 검토 요청 (Chapter five: review notes)

검토해 주셔서 감사합니다. 이 번역은 초벌 번역입니다. 아래는 번역자가 특히 확신하지 못하는 부분들입니다. 각 항목마다 영어 원문, 현재 번역, 그리고 걱정되는 점을 적었습니다. 이 목록에 없는 부분이라도 어색한 곳이 있으면 자유롭게 고쳐 주세요. 말투(존댓말·반말)의 기준은 `glossary.md`에 정리되어 있습니다.

*Thank you for reviewing. This is a first-pass translation. Below are the passages the translator is least sure of, each with the English, the current Korean and the concern. Please also mark anything else that reads awkwardly. The speech-level choices for each character are in `glossary.md`.*

The chapter covers the end of spring term and the first day in the capital. Tor gives out flying belts, builds a water purifier for a plague town and luggage plates that follow their owners, flies his friends to the capital, and stays at Rolph's family's "small house" without realizing it's the palace. New speakers: Rolph's mother Connie, his sisters Karina and Varley, the head server Quavel, the gate guard George, the Thompsons' butler Dallow, a city-guard captain, and "Burks," a mysterious older man who serves Tor breakfast. Viewpoints: Kolb, Rolph, Sara, Bet (scene 1); Tovey, Sara, Rolph, Trice (scene 2); Rolph, Tor alone, Karina, Connie (scene 3); Tor alone, then Quavel (scene 4).

## Across the whole chapter

1. **Tor's borrowed "noble voice."** In English Tor imitates Tovey's accent. Korean can't carry an accent easily, so his imitation uses **Tovey's 하오체** with equals, servants and Rolph ("알겠소. 그대가 그렇게 생각한다면." / "물론이오. 먼저 가시오, 롤프."), and refined **하십시오체** with Rolph's mother at dinner ("안녕하십니까, 부인. 초대해 주셔서 정말 감사합니다."). When he forgets himself explaining the cooling plate, he slides back into his ordinary 해요체 ("지하 창고예요. 부인, … 아세요?") and back up again ("죄송합니다. 말이 길어졌군요."). Does the shift read as "a country boy putting on a posh voice," and is the slide audible?
2. **Rolph's home voice.** At the palace gate Rolph switches to a young lord's **하게체** with older servants ("조지. 잘 있었나." / "퀘이블, 이것 좀 학사한테 올려다 주겠나?"), distinct from Tovey's 하오체. Natural for a young noble to an old guard and a head servant?
3. ~~**Servants → Tor: 도련님.**~~ Answered by the author: keep 도련님. George, Quavel, the maid, the gardener and Burks all call Tor 도련님 ("sir"). Tor objects: "자꾸 저를 도련님이라고 부르시는데요. 저 아무것도 아니에요." Is 도련님 right for a young male guest of uncertain rank, or would 손님 be more natural?
4. **Units** (traditional, as before): 22 miles → 88리 (and the milestones 80, 72, 60, 48); twenty yards → 예순 자; every hundred yards → 삼백 자마다; a foot → 한 자; an inch → 한 치. Is 삼백 자마다 too clumsy for the towers on the city wall? (Alternative: 백 걸음마다.)
5. **Trice's 당신** again in scene 2 ("당신이 말해 봐요", "당신은 집사가 아니잖아요"). Same exasperation as in chapter 4.

## Scene 1: School as normal (Kolb, Rolph, Sara, Bet)

6. **"Two hundred, if that rake stops again."** → 그 갈퀴 또 멈추면 이백 바퀴다. Kolb to a lazy first-year.
7. **"Bless that boy."** → 그 녀석 복 받을 거야.
8. **"sweating like a cheese"** → 치즈처럼 땀을 흘리면서. Literal; is there a better Korean image?
9. **"your declared friend"** (Tovey formally declared Tor his friend) → 공언한 친구. Natural?
10. **"Brothers beat friends. That's the rule."** → 형제가 친구를 이기는 법이야. 그게 규칙이야.
11. ~~**Tor's sisters.**~~ Answered by the author: Terlee and Tilda are older (누나); Tessa and Teira are younger. Terlee and Tilda are taken to be older (털리 누나, 틸다 누나); Tessa (nine) and Teira are plain names. *(Author: please confirm who is older than Tor.)*
12. **"a bucket under a leak"** → 새는 데 받쳐 놓은 양동이. Tor's angry speech about the sewer; the anger should be plain and shocking, since he never raises his voice.

## Scene 2: Flight to the capital (Tovey, Sara, Rolph, Trice)

13. **"I heard *carry*. I'm holding you to *carry*."** → *든다*고 들었어요. *든다*는 말 책임지세요.
14. **The teardrop shield**: "like a drip off the pump spout, lying down" → 펌프 주둥이에서 떨어지는 물방울 … 누워 있는 거요. Clear?
15. **"Greetings."** Tor's over-formal addition to a nod → 인사드립니다. Should sound stiff and a little comic.
16. **"force lance"** → 역장창 (a field-lance). New word; does it read as a weapon?
17. **"Austrans!" / "Austrans, in brown?"** → 오스트라 놈들이다 / *오스트라* 놈들이 갈색 옷을 입고?
18. **"Welcome—" (the swallowed "home")** → 어서— … *돌아오신 것을*이라는 말이 입 안쪽에 있다가 … "어서 오십시오." The captain nearly says "welcome home" to the prince.
19. **"So I perceive, my lord. How very pleasant."** (the butler, unflappable) → 그런 듯합니다, 나리. 참으로 반가운 일입니다.
20. **"Are all noble ladies like this?" / "*Violent*." / "Only the ones who like you."** → 귀족 아가씨들은 다 이래요? / *난폭*해요. / 좋아하는 사람한테만 그래요. The last line is a half-confession Tor misses.
21. **"as though Tovey had pointed at a cathedral and called it a shed"** → 큰 성채를 가리키며 헛간이라고 부르기라도 한 것처럼. Changed from "cathedral" to keep religious words out.

## Scene 3: Meet the parents (Rolph, Tor, Karina, Connie)

22. **The "small house" joke.** Rolph's family calls their residence 작은 집; Tor laughs because at home the "small house" is the privy: "*뒷간*이에요. 우리 동네에선요. 작은 집은 *뒷간*이에요, 형." In Korean 작은집 usually means an uncle's household. Does the joke still land, given Tor explains it?
23. **"Thank you, sir" → George's "Sir."** Tor calls the old guard 나리 in his posh voice ("고맙소, 나리."), and George mutters "나리라." Does that carry the joke (a guest calling a gate guard "my lord")?
24. **Karina's "You're not Alphie … sorry to catch you dressing"** → 알피가 아니네요 … 옷 입는 중에 들어와서 미안해요. Should be embarrassing, never suggestive.
25. **"Nobody has two, you know … And obviously you."** → 아무도 두 개는 없어요, 알죠 … 그리고 당연히 당신도요. Karina's light flirtation; is 당신 right here, or too intimate?
26. **"like a cat that has found the cream left out"** → 내놓은 크림을 찾아낸 고양이처럼.
27. **The first bite.** "In this house … the first bite was the host's." → 첫 숟가락은 주인의 것이었다.
28. **"There's a T-name for every one."** → 다들 이름이 ㅌ으로 시작합니다. (토르, 털리, 탬, 틸다, 테사, 테이라 all begin with ㅌ, so the joke survives.)
29. **"He's going to kill me, I think, eventually. But fairly."** → 언젠가는 저를 죽이실 것 같습니다. 그래도 공정하게요.
30. **"something from a long way east, with flour on it"** → 먼 동쪽에서 온, 밀가루 묻은 무언가. Tor's country voice coming back.
31. **Connie to her son**, 반말, warm and dry: "먼저 우리를 좋아하게 두렴." / "카리나는 감당이 안 되겠다."

## Scene 4: Testing the field (Tor, Quavel)

32. **"the temperature of nothing. The temperature of not noticing."** → 아무것도 아닌 온도. 알아채지 못하는 온도.
33. **"Count Lairdgren"** → 레어드그렌 백작. The same name as the school. Quavel calls him 백작님; he speaks to Quavel in 하게체 ("내가 가져가지", "와서 문을 잡아 주게") and to Tor in a servant's 하십시오체 ("수고랄 것도 없습니다, 도련님").
34. **"Most effective, sir."** → 아주 효과적입니다, 도련님. Should be a dry understatement.
35. **"Burks"** → 벅스, and Tor calls him 벅스 씨. Tor calls Quavel 어르신, then 퀘이블 씨.

37. **"I look like somebody."** → 제가 뭐라도 되는 사람 같아요.
38. **The stock** (a man's neckcloth) → 목장식. Is there a better period word?
