# 제6장 검토 요청 (Chapter six: review notes)

검토해 주셔서 감사합니다. 이 번역은 초벌 번역입니다. 아래는 번역자가 특히 확신하지 못하는 부분들입니다. 각 항목마다 영어 원문, 현재 번역, 그리고 걱정되는 점을 적었습니다. 이 목록에 없는 부분이라도 어색한 곳이 있으면 자유롭게 고쳐 주세요. 말투(존댓말·반말)의 기준은 `glossary.md`에 정리되어 있습니다.

*Thank you for reviewing. This is a first-pass translation. Below are the passages the translator is least sure of, each with the English, the current Korean and the concern. Please also mark anything else that reads awkwardly. The speech-level choices for each character are in `glossary.md`.*

The chapter covers Tor's second day in the capital. Tor spots that Ursula's brooch is Austran technology, the king walks in, Tor learns Rolph is the prince and calls the king "Rick," the engagement is broken, and Tor offers to marry Ursula. He hands out cooling amulets, goes to a working lunch at the Debri house (where Sara's mother bows to Rolph, the house can't pay Tor, and proposes a match), and is dressed for a royal dinner wearing the king's falcon. The chapter ends when he sees Count Ward's wife, Maria. New speakers: the king, Ursula, Heather and Kris Debri, Count and Countess Thorgood. Viewpoints: Ursula, then Tor (scene 1); Connie (scene 2); Rolph, Sara, Rolph (scene 3); Tor alone, Quavel, Rolph, Trice (scene 4).

## Across the whole chapter

1. **The king's speech level.** King Richard speaks a mild, slow **하게체** to Tor, Ursula and guests ("앉게. 앉는 편이 낫겠어 보이는군." / "아가. 자네를 믿네."), **해라체** to his son ("이 *방*에서부로다, 알폰스.") and **해요체** to the queen ("콘스턴스. 문서로 남기도록 해 줘요."). The headmaster and Dorgal also use 하게체. Is this right for a king, and does he sound gentle rather than stern?
2. **Addressing royalty.** Tor calls the king 어르신 for "sir" and 폐하; the queen 부인 and 왕비 마마; the Debri house calls Rolph 전하. Is 왕비 마마 natural next to 폐하, or should the queen be 왕비 전하 / 폐하?
3. **Trice's aunt.** The queen is assumed to be Trice's father's sister, so "Aunt Constance" → 콘스턴스 고모 and "her uncle" → 고모부. *(Author: if the queen is her mother's sister, this becomes 이모 / 이모부.)*
4. **The falcon crest** → 매 문장 (crest) and 휘장 (badge), kept apart from 문양 (sigil). Is 문장 confusing so close to 문양?

## Scene 1: Spy (Ursula, then Tor)

5. **Tor's Hill voice slipping.** "Rolph, we've shared a room for *two years*. You told me about the dog." → "형, 우린 *두 해*나 한 방을 썼소. 개 얘기도 해 줬잖아요." The 하오체 starts and his own 해요체 "creeps in." Is the slide audible?
6. **"Rick" / "Rich" / "Icky Ricky."** → 릭 / 리치 / *끈적이 리키* (he'd fallen in the carp pond). The English rhyme is lost; is there a better childish taunt with 리키?
7. **"Sir. Your. Majesty. Sir."** → "어르신. 폐. 하. 어르신." Tor stumbling over the title.
8. **"the vaults beneath the river temple"** → 강변 신전 아래 지하 묘실. "Temple" is in the English; should it be neutral, as with 회당?
9. **"the noble condition … Faces slide."** → 귀족 증세 … 얼굴이 미끄러져. Clear?
10. **The proposal.** "I'll marry you" → "제가 결혼할게요." "I'm usually working." → "보통 일하고 있거든요." Should sound earnest and a little absurd, not romantic.
11. **"there's nothing wrong with bakers"** → 빵집 하는 게 뭐 어때서요.

## Scene 2: Tor-Everything (Connie)

12. **"*Tor*-shields? … That's a *terrible* name."** → "*토르* 방패요?" / "*끔찍한* 이름이에요." And "a Tor-something" → 토르 뭐뭐.
13. **"It's a bit warm. Here. In the capital. I don't know if any of you have ever noticed that?"** → 좀 덥잖아요. 여기. 수도는요. 혹시 여러분 중에 그걸 알아채신 분 있나 모르겠네요? Tor's deadpan; the whole room laughs.
14. **"Hm," which meant *yes*.** → "흠." … 그게 *그래*라는 뜻임을.
15. **"You weren't counting."** → 당신은 셈을 안 했어요. Ursula's 당신 here is wonder, not hostility. Right?
16. **"*Tor*," said Alphonse, and Ursula, and Connie, all at once.** → "*토르*." … 한꺼번에 말했다.

## Scene 3: The luncheon (Rolph, Sara, Rolph)

17. **"My father has never in his life been polite by accident."** → 우리 아버지는 평생 한 번도 어쩌다 예의를 차리신 적이 없어.
18. **Tor's mixed accent.** "every sentence started on the Hill and finished on the wharf" → 문장마다 언덕에서 시작해 부두에서 끝났고. His lines here are a mix of 하십시오체 and 해요체 ("제 트렁크에 있습니다. … 오늘은 못 드려요"). Does it read as a boy straining to sound posh?
19. **Rolph's cold formal voice.** "Is that the reputation the house wants?" → 그게 이 집안이 원하는 평판입니까? and "*Really*, Heather?" → "*정말* 그러십니까, 헤더?" This is his red flag (glossary). Cold enough?
20. **"one doesn't send the king a bill on the first of the month"** → 폐하께 월초에 청구서를 보내지는 않지요.
21. **Kris's "Mother"** (meaning "careful, where's the hook?") → "어머니." / *조심하세요. 낚싯바늘이 어디 있죠?*
22. **"I didn't even have to dress up."** → 차려입을 필요도 없었잖아요. Sara's slip; should land like a dropped glass.
23. **"within three steps of her station"** → 자기 신분에서 세 계단 안쪽으로. The marriage law; clear?
24. **"Is it— does everybody do this? … a city thing."** → 이게— 도시에서 하는 건 줄은 몰랐어요. Tor thinks proposals are a capital custom.

## Scene 4: Dinner distractions (Tor, Quavel, Rolph, Trice)

25. **"If you looked terrible, I would certainly tell you. How else would we fix it?"** → 꼴이 엉망이시면 저는 틀림없이 말씀드릴 겁니다. 그러지 않으면 어떻게 고치겠습니까? Burks's dry servant voice.
26. **"Like a taster."** → 음식 맛을 먼저 보는 시종처럼. (기미 상궁 was considered and dropped as too Korean-historical.)
27. **"Children. Babies, mostly. … Small babies."** → 어린애들이요. 주로 아기들이요. … 작은 아기들이요.
28. **"as if it were the sacrament" / "receiving a relic"** → 가보라도 받쳐 들듯 / 가보를 받아 드는. Religious words avoided, as in chapter 4.
29. **"Is the cat out of the bag?"** → 다 들통났어요? / "it's very hard to hide a whole palace" → 궁전 하나를 통째로 숨기기란 아주 어렵단다.
30. **Trice sticks out her tongue at the queen.** → 혀를 쏙 내밀었다.
31. **"Master Wizard"** → 대마법사, split as "*대*—" / "*마법사*요." Does the interruption work?
32. **"If he'd planned it … she'd eat her slippers."** → 실내화를 씹어 먹어도 좋았다.
33. **Units.** "hundreds of miles east" → 동쪽으로 천 리도 넘게; "for a mile" → 4리 안에서; "a hand above the road" → 한 뼘.
34. **The last line.** "someone across it had just called him a name." → 방 저편의 누군가가 방금 그를 놀리는 이름으로 부른 것이었다. Then "*마리아*." The cliffhanger rests on Tor's face, seen by Trice.
