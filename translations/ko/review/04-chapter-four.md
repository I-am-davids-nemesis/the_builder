# 제4장 검토 요청 (Chapter four: review notes)

검토해 주셔서 감사합니다. 이 번역은 초벌 번역입니다. 아래는 번역자가 특히 확신하지 못하는 부분들입니다. 각 항목마다 영어 원문, 현재 번역, 그리고 걱정되는 점을 적었습니다. 이 목록에 없는 부분이라도 어색한 곳이 있으면 자유롭게 고쳐 주세요. 말투(존댓말·반말)의 기준은 `glossary.md`에 정리되어 있습니다.

*Thank you for reviewing. This is a first-pass translation. Below are the passages the translator is least sure of, each with the English, the current Korean and the concern. Please also mark anything else that reads awkwardly. The speech-level choices for each character are in `glossary.md`.*

The chapter covers about a month of early spring: Tor builds and tests a magic shield while Wensa (a royal guard posing as an accounts teacher) follows him everywhere, then builds a heater and a flying device. It's the lightest chapter so far: physical comedy, a running gag of people throwing things at Tor, and two half-serious marriage proposals he takes for a joke. Viewpoints rotate: Wensa, Dorgal, Rolph, Sara, Kolb, Trice, and Tor alone for the last scene.

## Across the whole chapter

1. **Wensa's speech level.** She's a royal guard pretending to be a teacher, so she uses curt 반말 to students ("아니." "그러겠지." "베이커 군. 일어나.") and 해요체 to staff. To Rolph, whom she is secretly guarding, she also uses 반말 ("그 애가 한 일이라면 나도 알고 있어"). Does she sound cold and controlled, rather than rude? *(Author: keep 반말.)*
2. **Trice's 당신 in scene 9.** Scolding Tor while she spoons broth into him, she calls him 당신 ("당신은 엄청나게 골칫거리예요", "당신 외상이라고요"). Intended as exasperation. Does 당신 sound too intimate, or like a married couple quarrelling? Alternatives: drop the pronoun, or 토르 씨. *(Author: keep 당신 unless it reads wrong to you.)*
3. **"Open"** (feeding) → 아 해요, repeated a dozen times. Natural, or too babyish for an angry young woman?
4. **Units** (traditional, as before): four feet → 넉 자; six inches → 다섯 치; an inch → 한 치; a hand's breadth → 한 뼘; a mile → 4리; "a man's height, two" → 사람 키만큼, 두 길.
5. ~~**예배당, 간호 수녀.**~~ Answered by the author: neutral words, 회당 and 간호사, in every chapter. Is 회당 a natural word for a small town's chapel with a tower?

## Scene 1: The tests (Wensa)

6. **"Ask for one."** The scene's last line, Wensa's first note in nine days → *하나 달라고 할 것.* Is the memo style right?
7. **"the way he might have taken a burr off a sister's stocking"** → 누이의 스타킹에서 도꼬마리를 떼어 내듯이. Is 도꼬마리 a natural "burr"?
8. **"The air's smaller. Of course it is."** → 공기가 더 작은 거야. 당연하지. Tor's half-understood insight; should sound odd but not wrong.
9. **"cut-penny smell of the etching acid"** → 동전 자른 듯한 냄새.

## Scene 2: The introduction (Dorgal)

10. **"Baker's sweetheart has had the fish."** → 베이커 애인은 생선을 먹었네. Schoolboy mockery; should be silly, not crude.
11. **"She's practically marrying up."** → 그분 입장에선 거의 시집 잘 가는 셈이지. A class jibe (baker's boy vs. schoolmistress).
12. **The introductions.** Tor names everyone formally, with titles describing what they are to *him*: "이쪽은 도걸 소르비 도련님이십니다. 저를 겁주고 싶어 하십니다." / "이쪽은 근위대의 웬사 선생님이세요. 저를 따라다니고 계세요." The comedy is the stiff politeness. Does it land? Is 도련님 right for a rich merchant's son (not a noble)?
13. **"Oh, *Tor*,"** said to the door as he realizes he's blown her cover → 아, *토르*. Does a person scolding himself by name read naturally in Korean?
14. **The black-eyed man** is never named; narration calls him 세 번째 남자. His eyes: "잉크병 속 잉크처럼 고르고 젖은 검정 … 연못에 떨어뜨린 동전 두 닢처럼."

## Scene 3: The template (Rolph)

15. **"Oh, *Baker*,"** said to the ceiling → 아, *베이커*.
16. **Rolph's rush to the building master**, a single breathless run-on in 하십시오체 ("격리실에 있습니다, 선생님, 어제 다섯째 종부터 …"). Does it read as breathless rather than broken?
17. **"Busy being what?" / "I don't think there's a word for it."** → "바빠서, 뭐?" / "그걸 가리키는 말은 없는 것 같습니다."
18. **"Three and a half," said Tor helpfully** → "사흘 반이요." 토르가 친절하게 말했다.

## Scene 4: The test (Sara)

19. **"glass halls on the river"** (where fine magic devices are sold) → 강가의 유리 회랑. Is 회랑 clear?
20. **"a fence post that had ideas"** → 무슨 꿍꿍이라도 품은 울타리 말뚝.
21. **Kolb: "You were meant to put it on the *pell*."** → 그건 *타격목*에 걸라고 한 거였어. The chapter's biggest punchline: Tor thought for two weeks that Kolb meant to kill him.
22. **The crowd's suggestions** ("Try him in the pond", "Poison", "He'd have to eat. Has anyone ever seen him eat?"). Do they sound like cheerful students?
23. **"That's a lot of copper."** → 구리가 엄청 많이 들겠네요. Tor's reply to being told everyone in the kingdom will want one.
24. **Tovey → Sara: 데브리 양.** Right for a count to a merchant's daughter?

## Scene 5: Interlude (Sara)

25. **Sara's letters to her father** are 하십시오체: "방패가 통과했습니다…" Is the register right for a daughter writing a business report?
26. **The payment list** ("데이비: 감사(상당함). 1학년 하나: 사과 한 알, 던진 것.") Should read as a deadpan ledger.
27. **"like a man forever on the point of swearing an oath"** (hand on chest) → 늘 막 맹세를 하려는 사람 같은 꼴.
28. **"Still not, then."** after a pear hits him → 아직 멀었네.
29. **"It's like sending bread to market … But it's *your* bread."** → 빵을 장에 보내는 거랑 비슷해요 … 그래도 *내* 빵이잖아요.

## Scene 6: Kolb's challenge (Kolb)

30. **"Austrans do it."** → 오스트라 놈들은 해. Kolb is a soldier, so 놈들. Too rough?
31. **"like trying to lift yourself up by your own boots"** → 자기 장화 끈을 잡아당겨서 자기를 들어 올리려는 거랑 같아요.
32. **"Nice and loose"** → 부드럽게 잘 가는군 (as in chapter 2). Kolb's tic; here it's an aside about Tor's mind working.

## Scene 7: The ceiling (Rolph)

33. **"It only goes *up*." / "Well, what did you want it to do?" / "Everything *else*."** → "근데 *위로만* 가요." / "그럼 뭘 하길 바랐는데?" / "*나머지* 전부요."
34. **"The feather was the worst, because it was so obviously, hopefully, a feather."** → 깃털이 제일 딱했다. 너무나 뻔하게, 희망에 차서, 깃털이었으니까.
35. **The spoon and the tea** ("What if it wasn't on the board? … The telling.") → 알려 주는 거요. Is 알려 주다 a good word for "telling" a device what to do?

## Scene 8: The cold snap (Sara)

36. **"It moves the warm."** → 따뜻한 걸 옮겨요. Tor's plain words for a heat pump.
37. **"I didn't think about *enough*."** → *이만하면 됐다*는 생각을 안 했어요.
38. **"Tor, marry me."** → 토르. 나랑 결혼하자. Sara is more than half serious; it's said in her steady business voice. Does 반말 with 하자 sound like a real proposal?
39. **The argument** between Sara and Trice in trading terms: 우선 매수권, "값을 매긴다고 산 건 아니라고", "값을 묻는 게 거래를 트는 거라고". Do these sound like two clever girls, not lawyers?
40. **"I've asked a girl to a dance before."** → 저도 전에 여자애한테 춤추자고 해 본 적 있어요. The line has to hurt quietly; it refers to an old humiliation.
41. **"bearing a relic"** → 가보를 받쳐 든 사람 같은 태도로 (made neutral, like 회당).

## Scene 9: The broth (Trice)

42. **"He's gone under."** → 들어갔어. Rolph's word for the trance. Clear?
43. **"That's on *your* account, Master Baker, and I'm charging interest."** → *당신* 외상이라고요, 베이커 선생, 이자도 받을 거예요.
44. **"Trice, what *day* is it?" / "The wrong one."** → 오늘 *며칠*이에요? / 엉뚱한 날이요.
45. **"It's almost as if you—"** → 이건 거의, 아가씨가 마치— Tor stops before saying "like me." Does the break work?
46. **"Then you can fly into a tree with my blessing."** → 그다음엔 날아서 나무에 처박혀도 내가 축복해 줄게요.

## Scene 10: The first flight (Tor)

47. **Tor scolding himself** all the way up the hill in 반말 ("바보. 이 *바보*야. 딱 하나. *딱 하나*였는데. … 그래도 싸. 완전히 싸지."). Natural?
48. **"*Come on*," … and threw both his hands up** → "*좀*," … 두 손을 허공으로 내던졌다. The exasperated 좀 is meant to carry "come on." Does it?
49. **"Oh, *look* at you."** said to the town → 아, 너 좀 *봐*.
50. **The town's rumor** ending: 사람만 한 떼까마귀 … 쟁기 주점에서는 세 사람이 그게 노래하는 걸 들었다고 맹세했다. "the Plough" (a tavern) → 쟁기 주점, by meaning like 무덤 주점.
