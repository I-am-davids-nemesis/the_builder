# 제3장 검토 요청 (Chapter three: review notes)

검토해 주셔서 감사합니다. 이 번역은 초벌 번역입니다. 아래는 번역자가 특히 확신하지 못하는 부분들입니다. 각 항목마다 영어 원문, 현재 번역, 그리고 걱정되는 점을 적었습니다. 이 목록에 없는 부분이라도 어색한 곳이 있으면 자유롭게 고쳐 주세요. 말투(존댓말·반말)의 기준은 `glossary.md`에 정리되어 있습니다.

*Thank you for reviewing. This is a first-pass translation. Below are the passages the translator is least sure of, each with the English, the current Korean and the concern. Please also mark anything else that reads awkwardly. The speech-level choices for each character are in `glossary.md`.*

The chapter runs from terror (a night chase) to comedy (the hearing, and Rolph's laughing fit) and ends on a serious note. Its biggest new voices are the headmaster, Wensa (an accounts teacher who turns out to be a royal guard) and Campbell, the dean.

## Across the whole chapter

1. **The headmaster's speech level.** Mild, old-fashioned 하게체 to Tor with 베이커 군 ("앉게, 베이커 군", "자네도 그렇게 만들지 않나?"), and 해요체 to Wensa (chosen by the author). Dorgal also uses 하게체 to Tor, theatrically. Do the two sound different enough, and does the headmaster sound kind rather than condescending?
2. **Trice → Tovey.** First cousins who grew up together; Tovey is older, so Trice uses 반말 and calls him 오빠 / 토비 오빠, even while teasing him ("오빠가? 정말, 토비 오빠, 잘난 척은."). Does 오빠 sit well with a girl scolding a count in front of the headmaster?
3. **Rolph's voice shift.** When Rolph learns Wensa is a royal guard, his English goes cold and exact. The Korean jumps to 하십시오체 *to Tor*: "그분이 자신이 근위대원이라고 말했단 말입니까. 정확히 그 말로. 확실합니까?" Does this read as alarming (intended) or as a mistake? Earlier, alone, "very nearly formal": "아니요. 그렇게는 안 돼요."
4. **Ducharina** (an invented title for a duke's daughter) → 공녀, by the author's choice: "퍼트리샤 앨리슨 모건 공녀. 모건 공작의 따님." Is the word order natural for a formal title?
5. **Units.** "a foot, three feet" → 한 자, 세 자; "five inches" → 네 치; "a mile from anywhere" → 어디서든 4리; "a great many miles" → 몇십 리.

## Scene 1: The chase

6. **"oh, that's what the pell feels like"** → *아, 타격목이 이런 기분이구나.* Tor, flying through the air after being hit, thinks of the practice post he hits in class. Funny?
7. **Tor muttering to himself** ("You hit him … You hit a count. With a sack. Several times. Tor.") → 반말 to himself: "너 그 사람 때렸어." … "백작을 때렸다고. 자루로. 여러 번. 토르." Natural?
8. **"argued with a bramble"** → *가시덤불과 실랑이*. And "He ran into a tree and apologized to it" → 나무에 부딪히고 나무에게 사과했다.
9. **"to say you'd done a hanging thing"** → 제가 목매달릴 짓을 했으니. Clear?

## Scene 2: The dean

10. **"Thank you for everything."** → 여러 가지로 고마웠어요. Tor is saying goodbye, as if he'll be hanged; Rolph hears it that way. Does the Korean carry the farewell?
11. **"looked at him until he went away"** → 그 애가 갈 때까지 그 애를 쳐다보았다 (scene 6, same idea).

## Scene 3: The warded room

12. **Wards** → 결계. The corridor's wards "turn the mind aside": 마음을 비껴가게 했다. Is 결계 right for a magical concealment (it can feel Buddhist or anime-flavored)?
13. **"It was, Campbell decided, above his pay."** → 이건 자기 봉급으로 감당할 일이 아니라고. Idiomatic?
14. **"Mistress Wensa? From accounts?"** → 웬사 선생님? 회계 선생님이요? The moment Tor sees through her disguise; it should sound polite but alert.

## Scene 4: The hearing

15. **"What are friends for?"** → 친구 좋다는 게 뭐겠어요? Tor says it deadpan, half a joke; everyone except Trice takes it as a sincere claim of friendship with a count. Does it work both ways?
16. **Tovey's apology in 하오체** with 그대 ("내가 그대를 쳤소… 말로 다 할 수 없이 미안하오"). Does it sound stiff and sincere, as intended?
17. **"It was a very sensible tap." / "Several taps."** → 아주 분별 있는 톡톡이었죠 / 여러 번 톡톡이었어요. The comedy is Tor correcting her against his own interest.
18. **"There wasn't a column."** Trice keeps mental accounts: 그녀는 그것도 장부에 올렸다 … 그런 칸이 없었다.
19. **Kolb: "Stand easy, Baker. You'll frighten the lady."** → 편히 서, 베이커. 숙녀분 놀라시겠다.
20. **Wensa's broken sentence** ("which were laid by the same hands that laid the wards on the—") → 그건 바로 그 손들이 친 결계입니다, 그— She almost names something secret. Does the break read naturally?

## Scene 5: The headmaster's advice

21. **"One thing at a time, Mr. Baker. Isn't that how you build?"** → 한 번에 하나씩일세, 베이커 군. 자네도 그렇게 만들지 않나?
22. **"You have a leaf in your hair."** → 머리에 나뭇잎이 붙어 있네. The scene's punchline.

## Scene 6: What Rolph knows

23. **"I can't make toast … I set fire to a fork."** → 난 토스트도 못 만들어 … 포크에 불을 냈지. Does toast fit the setting, or should it become something more Korean (e.g., 밥도 못 해)?
24. **"That's like being told the moon has a family name."** → 달한테 성(姓)이 있다는 말을 듣는 거 같아요.
25. **The joke about Wensa chasing Tor** ("I've never been chased about by a woman before. I'd have thought I'd enjoy it more.") → 여자한테 쫓겨 다녀 본 건 처음인데. 더 즐거울 줄 알았어요. Must stay innocent and silly, not suggestive. Does it?
26. **"the infirmary sister"** → 간호 수녀. Is the religious color wrong for this world? 간호사 would avoid it.
