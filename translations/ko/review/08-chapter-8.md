# 제8장 검토 요청 (Chapter eight: review notes)

검토해 주셔서 감사합니다. 이 번역은 초벌 번역입니다. 아래는 번역자가 특히 확신하지 못하는 부분들입니다. 각 항목마다 영어 원문, 현재 번역, 그리고 걱정되는 점을 적었습니다. 이 목록에 없는 부분이라도 어색한 곳이 있으면 자유롭게 고쳐 주세요. 말투(존댓말·반말)의 기준은 `glossary.md`에 정리되어 있습니다.

*Thank you for reviewing. This is a first-pass translation. Below are the passages the translator is least sure of, each with the English, the current Korean and the concern. Please also mark anything else that reads awkwardly. The speech-level choices for each character are in `glossary.md`.*

The chapter runs from the same night at the beach house to the next afternoon outside the capital. Rolph tells how Maria humiliated Tor in their first year and what came after; Trice tells the tale of Wylde and Doretta. That night the girls slip out on their spy homework while Rolph sets Tor building; at dawn Tor drops an intake stone two leagues out to sea and makes a water line that runs out safely when switched off. Flying home, a village innkeeper charges six silver for lunch because of the drought, and over the King's river Tor sees that he need only fill the rivers. Outside the capital wall he goes head-first down a collapsing midden on a rope, saves a boy, finds a little girl, breaks his ankle, and moves the earth to get her out; he passes out as the bell rings three. New speakers: the innkeeper, the guard captain, the thin guardsman, the old tanner, the scarred soldier, Simon's mother. Viewpoints: Sara (scene 1); Rolph, Tor alone, Tor alone, Sara, Sara (scene 2); Rolph, Tor in the pit, Rolph (scene 3).

## Across the whole chapter

1. **Units.** "two miles" → 8리 (Tor's sum: "A mile out … A mile's plenty … Two miles. To be safe." → "4리 밖 … 4리면 충분해 … 8리. 안전하게."); "thirty feet" → 서른 자; "a couple of hundred yards" → 이백 걸음; "three hundred feet" → 삼백 자; "a hundred miles" → 사백 리. As in earlier chapters. Does Tor's sum still read as a careful boy doubling for safety?
2. **Tor's reading of the giggling.** "Is it a— a noble thing? That you all do? On holidays?" → "그게— 귀족들이 하는 거예요? 다들? 휴가 때?" and at breakfast "I expect it's a— a sort of noble thing. That people do." → "아마 그— 귀족들이 하는 그런 거겠죠. 사람들이 하는 거요." It must suggest Tor imagines something improper without saying anything sexual. Does it?
3. **"Gods."** Rendered 맙소사 throughout (Rolph's "Gods, Tor", "good gods", the captain's "Who in the gods' name" → "대체 이건 누가—"). Neutral enough?

## Scene 1: Maria's Dagger (Sara)

4. **"Doretta was a thing you *were*."** → 도레타는 사람이 *되어 버리는* 무언가였다.
5. **"with an accent you could stand a spoon in"** → 숟가락을 꽂아 세워도 될 만큼 걸쭉한 사투리.
6. **The dean, as Rolph mimics him.** "Mr. Merchant, this is Mr. Baker, from Two Rivers" → "상인 군, 이쪽은 베이커 군이다. 두물머리에서 왔지." "Mr. Merchant" is Rolph's joke for his own cover name. Does 상인 군 read as a joke?
7. **The flowers.** Celandines → 애기똥풀; blackthorn → 가시자두; primroses → 앵초; ivy → 담쟁이. Are these the right common names, and does 애기똥풀 sound too comic for a tender moment?
8. **Maria's screaming, reported.** "That he was poor. That he was ugly. That he was a dirty little nobody from a mud village … did he not know his place?" → 가난뱅이라고. 못생겼다고. 진흙 마을에서 온 더러운 꼬맹이라고 … 주제를 모르느냐고. Consistent with chapter 7, item 5.
9. **"two years hard … under the grin, with its boots on"** → 두 해 묵은 단단함으로 … 그의 웃음 밑에서 장화를 신은 채 기다리고 있었다.
10. **"Unfit for the term."** → 이번 학기 수학 불가. Official enough?
11. **"And you aren't exactly a big job."** → 그리고 네가 딱히 큰일감도 아니고. Rolph's joke on Tor's size.
12. **Tor's war.** "Davie'd come, but he'd want to know what time we'd be back for supper … They could have races. An egg and spoon … It's not a war if it's one person and a picnic." → "오기는 올 텐데, 저녁 먹으러 몇 시에 돌아오느냐고 물어보실 거예요 … 숟가락에 달걀 얹고 달리기요 … 한 사람이랑 소풍이면 그건 전쟁이 아니에요." Funny?
13. **"Vile, vile woman … She's got *nerve*. That's different."** → "더러운, 더러운 여자 … 그 여자한테 있는 건 *뻔뻔함*이야. 그건 달라." Is 더러운 too coarse for Trice? (비열한 was considered.)
14. **The Wylde tale in a nurse's voice.** Trice tells it to Tor in 해요체 ("옛날에, 가게 주인이 하나 있었어요"). A Korean nurse's tale would usually be 해라체 or 반말 (~했대, ~했단다), but Trice's line to Tor is fixed at 해요체. Keep, or shift the tale itself into storytelling 반말 as a signal?
15. **"Don't be a Doretta."** → *도레타가 되지 마라.* The nursery warning. Does it sound like a saying?
16. **"I wouldn't put a *goat* on an island."** → "*염소*도 섬에 두고 오지 않을 거예요." And Trice: "That's rather the trouble … Nobody else does." → "그게 좀 문제예요 … 다른 사람들은 모르거든요."

## Scene 2: Homework (Rolph, Tor alone, Tor alone, Sara, Sara)

17. **Rolph's cover story.** "It's *lists*. It's school." → "*목록*이야. 학교 일이야." And the narration: "There would almost certainly be lists." → 목록은 거의 틀림없이 있을 테니까.
18. **The off-switch idea.** "It doesn't have to drop … You just stop giving it any more … Like the last of the water out of a pump" → "떨어질 필요가 없어 … 그냥 더 주지 않는 거야 … 펌프질을 멈춘 뒤에 펌프에서 마지막 물이 나오듯이." "Well, that's one." → "자, 하나 됐다." Then "*That's* one." → "*이게* 하나야."
19. **"In a minute."** → "조금 있다가요." Rolph: "He said in a minute." → "조금 있다가래." The phrase is a running signal that Tor is under.
20. **"None of my business."** → "내가 상관할 일 아니야." Tor to himself, 반말 as in the glossary.
21. **Trice at breakfast.** "Is there jam," in a voice like a gate on a rusty hinge → "잼 있어" 하고 말했다, 녹슨 경첩에 매달린 대문 같은 목소리로. She says it to the table in 반말 (Sara is there). Fine?
22. **The innkeeper.** Speaks 반말 to the four young strangers ("은화 여섯 닢이다", "서쪽에서 왔구나", "미안하게 됐다"). Tor answers in 해요체. Rolph: "No. I'll tell them." → "아니요. 말할게요." Is the innkeeper's voice right for a hard village woman?
23. **"Six was not a price. Six was a fact about something else."** → 여섯은 값이 아니었다. 여섯은 다른 무언가에 대한 사실이었다.
24. **Corn cakes.** → 옥수수 전병. Flat griddle cakes made with water, not milk. Natural?
25. **"I like it when you laugh."** → "저는 세라 아가씨가 웃는 게 좋아요." And Sara "felt something … that she didn't have a column for" → 그걸 적을 칸이 없었다.
26. **"They've *done* it."** → "벌써 *해 놨어요*." The rivers idea; "for *ever*" → *영원히*요.

## Scene 3: Luncheon Avoided (Rolph, Tor in the pit, Rolph)

27. **"*Situation report!*"** → "*상황 보고!*" Kolb's voice coming out of Rolph. Military enough?
28. **The captain's report.** "Child. Boy. Seven. Tanners' boy." → "아이입니다. 사내아이. 일곱 살. 무두장이 아들." He answers Rolph in 하십시오체 by reflex; to his own men 반말.
29. **"midden"** → 쓰레기 구덩이; "palings" → 울타리 말뚝; "timbers" → 들보.
30. **Tor to the trapped boy in 반말.** "Hello. Simon? It's all right. My name's Tor. Your mum's up top." → "안녕. 사이먼? 괜찮아. 내 이름은 토르야. 엄마가 위에 계셔." Tor is polite to everyone, but a 해요체 to a seven-year-old in a pit would sound strange in Korean. New pairing: Tor → small children, 반말. Agree?
31. **The old tanner.** "That's tight, lad … Any longer and it'll do you damage you'll not walk off." → "꽉 조였다, 얘야 … 걸어서 털고 일어나지 못할 탈이 난다." 얘야 was the carter's word for Tor; reused here.
32. **The scarred soldier.** "Got it." → "알았다." Terse 반말 to a boy in his drawers.
33. **Tor's command to the earth.** "*Move* … Move. *I command you.*" → "*움직여.* … 움직여. *명령한다.*" Tor never uses 반말 except to himself; here it is flat, not his own voice. Does *명령한다* sound chilling rather than pompous?
34. **"*Run!*"** → "*도망쳐!*" Rolph's voice cracking like an eight-year-old's.
35. **The girl's landing.** "as if somebody had nailed her there by every edge" → 누가 가장자리마다 못을 박아 놓기라도 한 것처럼.
36. **"a dead cat, regrettably"** → 유감스럽게도 … 죽은 고양이 한 마리. The narrator's dry aside.
37. **The last lines.** "We're going to be late." / "Yes." → "우리 늦겠다." / "그러네." The chapter's deadpan close.
