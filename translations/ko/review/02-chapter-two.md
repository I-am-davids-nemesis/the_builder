# 제2장 검토 요청 (Chapter two: review notes)

검토해 주셔서 감사합니다. 이 번역은 초벌 번역입니다. 아래는 번역자가 특히 확신하지 못하는 부분들입니다. 각 항목마다 영어 원문, 현재 번역, 그리고 걱정되는 점을 적었습니다. 이 목록에 없는 부분이라도 어색한 곳이 있으면 자유롭게 고쳐 주세요. 말투(존댓말·반말)의 기준은 `glossary.md`에 정리되어 있습니다.

*Thank you for reviewing. This is a first-pass translation. Below are the passages the translator is least sure of, each with the English, the current Korean and the concern. Please also mark anything else that reads awkwardly. The speech-level choices for each character are in `glossary.md`.*

The chapter stays light and funny until the last scene, which turns serious (but still has a sack of copper in it). The comedy is in the gap between how Tor reads people and what is actually happening, and in a running joke about prices: 30 gold (chapter one), then 2, 12, 20, 11.

## Across the whole chapter

1. **Douglas Sorvey's speech level.** A dangerous, courteous older merchant. He speaks smooth 하십시오체, even to Tor and a first-year ("좋은 아침입니다"), so that he never sounds like Tovey's 하오체. Does the courtesy feel slightly menacing, as intended, or just stiff?
2. **Units.** Distances use traditional units: 리, 자, 치. Rolph's "Town's five miles!" / Kolb's "So it's ten" is now 마을까지 20리인데요! / 그럼 왕복 40리군. Does the joke still land, and does 리 suit the setting?
3. **The cutter** → 절단검. A magic blade that appears from a bare hilt and cuts through oak without a sound. Is 절단검 good, or does it sound like a tool?
4. **Tor to younger students.** Tor uses 해요체 even to a first-year ("아, 안녕하세요! 그때 그분이네요. 또 늦으셨어요?"). Does it read as his habitual over-politeness, or simply as odd?

## Scene 1: Combat class

5. **"the particular shade of pale that Rolph associated with dinners where there were too many forks"** → 롤프가 포크가 너무 많은 만찬과 연결 짓는 빛깔. A hint at Rolph's grand family; is it clear?
6. **"as if it had remembered an appointment"** (the cut half of the pell sliding off) → 약속이라도 생각난 듯. Funny in Korean?
7. **The carter: "You want a ride back, love? … I've had worse than you sitting on my cabbages."** → 태워다 줄까, 얘야? … 내 양배추 위에 너보다 못한 것도 앉혀 봤어. Does she sound like a warm, earthy country woman?
8. **"I think possibly to be killed."** → 아마 죽임을 당하려고인 것 같아요. Tor's deadpan; is the grammar too stiff, or is the stiffness funny?
9. **"'Unarmed' was a word Trice had always treated as advice."** → '맨손'은 트리스에게 언제나 권고 사항이었다.
10. **"Air but not dust. A knife's a line. Turn it, don't stop it."** → 공기는 되고 먼지는 안 되고. 칼은 선이야. 멈추지 말고 돌려. This recurs as Tor's refrain; does it sound like a natural mutter?
11. **"Who benefits…?"** → 누구한테 이득일까. Trice's habitual question; it recurs in scene 3.

## Scene 2: The merchant

12. **Dorgal: "Off to be dunked again, Baker? Do try to float this time."** → 또 물에 빠지러 가나, 베이커? 이번엔 좀 떠 보게나.
13. **"He said it the way you'd say it to a large dog that had decided, for now, not to bite."** → 지금은 안 물기로 마음먹은 큰 개에게 하듯이.
14. **The building master hinting at a bribe** ("it is not unusual for the builder to remember the testing") → 제작자가 그 시험을 기억해 주는 건 드문 일이 아니다. Tor takes it literally. Is the hint clear enough for the reader to see what Tor misses?
15. **"the beginning of a laugh, strangled at birth"** → 태어나자마자 목이 졸린 웃음의 시작.

## Scene 3: The afternoon and the visit

16. **"They buy early, and then they own the stable."** → 일찍 사고, 그다음엔 마구간을 통째로 소유하죠.
17. **"the school doesn't kill students. Hardly ever. They're paid by the head."** → 학교는 학생을 죽이지 않아요. 거의 안 그래요. 머릿수대로 돈을 받거든요. Does the pun on "head" work?
18. **"ten in the hundred"** → 백에 열. It must stay ambiguous who gets the ten. Does 백에 열 sound like a natural trade phrase, and keep that ambiguity?
19. **Tor muttering after the girls leave: "I suppose I deserved that."** → 그런 소리 들어도 싸지, 뭐. He thinks they were mocking him. Is the hurt clear?

## Scene 4: Rolph's order and the night build

20. **"That's one gold off for friendship." / "That seems backward."** → 우정 값으로 한 닢 깎으신 거네요. / 거꾸로 된 것 같은데요. (Twelve to strangers, eleven to a friend: Tor thinks the friend should pay more.)
21. **"Fifty-five gold. Honestly, Rolph."** → 금화 쉰다섯 닢이라니. 진짜, 형도 참. Tor scolding his elder fondly; does 형도 참 work?
22. **The Barrow** → 무덤 주점 ("You're famous in the Barrow" → 무덤에서 유명해졌다고). Translated by meaning, since a barrow is a burial mound. Is it funny, or just grim?
23. **"Like the queen's?" / "Just like the queen's."** → 왕비님처럼요? / 왕비님이랑 똑같이. A hint that Rolph's mother *is* the queen. Is 왕비 the right word?
24. **"It's like a recipe. You don't need it to make the bread."** → 요리법 같은 거예요. 빵 만드는 데 꼭 필요하진 않잖아요. Would 레시피 or 조리법 sound better from a baker's son?
25. **Tor talking to the squares: "You're the same as the last one, aren't you."** → 너도 아까 걔랑 똑같지, 그치.

## Scene 5: The courtyard

26. **"This was a face with nobody standing behind it."** → 뒤에 아무도 서 있지 않은 얼굴이었다.
27. **Tor muttering as he lurches over: "—better have a very good reason… Miss Trice. Honestly."** → 아주 그럴듯한 이유가 있어야 할 거야 … 트리스 아가씨가. 진짜. Comic even in danger; does it land?
28. **"Clank. 'Sorry.' Clank. 'Sorry. Leave her alone. Sorry.'"** → *쩔그렁.* 죄송해요 … 그분 건드리지 마세요. The sound word and the polite plea together.
29. **"*well, this is stupid*"** → *참, 바보 같네.*
