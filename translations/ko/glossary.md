# Korean glossary (한국어 용어집)

Every Korean translation of *The Builder* uses these choices. Add to it when a chapter brings a new name, term or speaker, in the same change as the translation. Entries marked (to confirm) are first-pass choices waiting on the user or a native reader.

## Narration

- Literary past, plain style (해라체: ~했다, ~였다). No narrator honorifics, even for nobles.
- Keep the rotating close third: in a scene held by a character, word choice may lean toward that character (Davie's snobbery, Sara's trade eye), as in English.
- Keep sentences short where the English is short. Don't merge the one-line paragraphs; they carry the comic timing.
- Italics stay italics (`*…*`). Scene breaks (`* * *`) stay as they are.

## Speech levels

Korean has to choose a level for every line, so each pairing is fixed here. A shift away from the pairing is a signal, as in the English (`AGENTS.md` §4): don't spend one by accident.
