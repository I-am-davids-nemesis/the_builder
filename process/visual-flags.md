# Visual flags

Things on the page that might be hard to render visually, for a later screenplay (`ideas.md`, 2026-11-08). This is a watch list, not a rule for the draft (writer, November 8, 2026): when a scene puts something on the page that only narration can carry, ask about it in the scene's outline questions and add a line here.

One line per flag: the scene, what the page does, why a camera can't show it, and the answer once there is one.

## Flags

The first five come from `retrospective/2026-11-08-repurposing-decisions.md`. They're starting points, not decisions.

- **Feeling through the field** (`codex/characters/tor.md`; chapter 10 notes): Tor feels strong emotions through the field, such as Trice sizing him up as a way out of her problem. The feeling is inside him, with nothing to see. Answer: open.
- **Rolph feeling a field through the ground** (ch. 8, scene 3): he feels the earth field gather and doesn't recognize it as his own. Answer: open.
- **Noticing what someone else misses** (ch. 9, scene 5): Hale sees that Burks's silk is royal-guard purple; Tor doesn't. It needs a shot of the silk and of Hale's eyebrows. Answer: open.
- **Nobles not recognizing small people** (ch. 6): stated as a condition on the page; on screen it would need a face going blank. Answer: open.
- **Rage fields and Tor's own field** (ch. 10, scene 2): the room feels the duke's rage, and Tor feels his shield-like field drain force into the ground. Answer (writer, November 8, 2026): sparks show a rage; Tor's fly from his sides and back, out past his shield's radius, which is how others know the shield was off; Tor says what he felt aloud in the garden.
- **Six days of trance seen through the palace staff** (ch. 9, scene 3): may translate well, since the staff are already outside Tor. Answer: open.
