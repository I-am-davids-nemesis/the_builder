## 2026-11-08 · Retelling and adapting the book

From the writer's answers to `retrospective/2026-11-08-why-rotating-third.md` (see `retrospective/2026-11-08-repurposing-decisions.md`). Once the book is written, it may be repurposed: retold from other viewpoints, and adapted as a screenplay. A screenplay would need no narration, visual descriptions, and storyboards (probably sketches, not photorealistic images). This is a **later project, not a rule for this draft**. What it asks of the draft now is fully fleshed-out characters with a viewpoint, continuity, motivation, and "sufficient (but not too much) consistency." Things that might be hard to render visually are listed in `visual-flags.md` as they come up. The `camera` branch stays as it is; it isn't clear yet whether it will help with a screenplay.
