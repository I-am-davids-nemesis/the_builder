# Engine

What makes this book work. Keep each section short, and revise it when the book changes. Every revision adds an entry under Changes.

## Premise

One paragraph: who, where, and what goes wrong.

## Central question

The question the reader keeps reading to answer.

## Engine

What keeps producing scenes: the pressure, the constraint, the wants that pull against each other.

## Promise

What the reader is promised: genre, tone, the kind of payoff.

## Form

Point of view and narration, structure, target length.

## Theme

Optional, and often found late. Write down when it was found.

## Changes

Newest last. Each entry: the date, what changed, why, and the scene or conversation that prompted it.
